The call for resilience measures — hardened cyber defenses, protected infrastructure, workforce adjustment programs — sets up parallel national and allied efforts that run alongside the UN track.

The situation stays on this voluntary-cooperation ladder until a major tested model release or a verified AI-enabled breach forces tighter rules.
