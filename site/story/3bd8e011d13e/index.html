US carrier Frontier Airlines to operate direct flights to Colombia from Orlando

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineLa estadounidense Frontier Airlines operará en Colombia con vuelos directos desde Orlando

US airlines have added routes to Latin America for years as passenger traffic between the two regions grew. Frontier Airlines built a network of low-cost flights from US cities to Mexico and the Caribbean starting in the early 2010s. The carrier kept its focus on short-haul leisure routes that other US carriers served less often.

On September 10 2026 Frontier announced it will launch direct service from Orlando to two cities in Colombia. The new flights mark the airline's first scheduled operations inside Colombia.

The route adds another option for travelers between central Florida and Colombia and gives Frontier its initial foothold in the mainland South American market. The schedule now turns on when the first planes leave the gate and how many seats sell.

Thinking ahead
Frontier Airlines announced it will start nonstop service between Orlando and two Colombian cities beginning in late 2026. The carrier already flies to several Latin American spots from Orlando and has added routes steadily since its 2024 expansion push. Colombian aviation officials cleared the flights through standard bilateral approvals. The schedule holds unless fuel costs spike enough to force Frontier to drop unprofitable legs or unless Colombian regulators revoke landing rights. Next visible step is ticket sales opening and aircraft assignments for the first departures. This route addition looks set to run at least through 2028 barring a sharp drop in passenger bookings on the initial flights. Horizon: through 2028.
