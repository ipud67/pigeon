Indian national charged with making false claims of in order to vote

Minnesota law requires anyone who registers to vote to sign a certificate that affirms they are a U.S. citizen. That certificate carries the force of a sworn statement under state election rules that took shape after the state began tightening its voter-roll maintenance in the early 2000s.

On September 11, 2026, a federal grand jury in Minnesota Mukeshkumar Somabhai Chaudhari, an Indian national, on charges that he signed the certificate knowing the affirmation was false. The indictment states he used the document to complete his voter registration and cast a ballot.

The case now rests on whether prosecutors can prove Chaudhari understood the citizenship requirement when he signed and whether the state’s verification steps caught the discrepancy before the vote was counted.
