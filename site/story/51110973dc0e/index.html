UKRAINE
September 22, 2026 · 06:36 UTC

Russian forces fought Ukraine’s Defense Forces in 248 clashes on the front line in Ukraine on September 21, most of them in the Pokrovsk sector

Russian forces have pressed their offensive across multiple sectors of the front line in eastern Ukraine since the full-scale invasion began on 24 February 2022. Ukrainian units have conducted defensive operations to hold positions while striking Russian troop concentrations artillery and with missile systems. The General Staff of the Armed Forces of Ukraine tracks daily combat activity by sector and reports the number of clashes Russian forces initiate.

On 21 September 2026 the General Staff recorded 248 combat clashes between Ukrainian Defense Forces and Russian troops. The highest concentration occurred in the Pokrovsk sector where Ukrainian units turned back 33 Russian assaults near Bilytske Serhiivka Novopavlivka and other settlements including Toretske Sofiivka Dobropillia and Vasylivka. Russian forces also carried out 87 airstrikes that dropped 305 guided aerial bombs launched 9 647 kamikaze drones and fired 2 795 shells at Ukrainian positions and populated areas.

Ukrainian air defense missile forces and artillery struck one Russian personnel cluster three artillery systems and one command post in response. In other sectors Russian forces attempted five attacks near Buniachyne in the north ten near Starytsia and Lyman in the south one near Kivsharivka at Kupiansk five near Stavky at Lyman four near Ozerne at Sloviansk 19 near Kostiantynivka 14 near Rizdvianka at Huliaipole and two near Pavlivka at Orikhiv. No assaults took place in the Dnipro River sector and no signs of Russian offensive preparations appeared in Volyn or Polissia.

Thinking ahead
Russian forces and Ukrainian units recorded 248 combat clashes across the front line in the past day, with the highest concentration of 33 attacks in the Pokrovsk sector near Bilytske, Serhiivka, Novopavlivka, Toretske, Sofiivka, Dobropillia and other villages. Russian troops fired 87 airstrikes with 305 guided bombs, launched 9,647 kamikaze drones and carried out 2,795 shelling attacks on positions and settlements. Ukrainian forces hit one Russian personnel cluster, three artillery systems and one command post in response. In other sectors Russian attacks ranged from 19 in Kostiantynivka to single-digit attempts in Kupiansk, Lyman, Sloviansk and Kramatorsk, while no assaults occurred along the Dnipro or signs of new groupings in Volyn and Polissia. The daily clash count has held in the 200-plus range for weeks, showing steady pressure rather than sudden escalation. This level of contact is likely to continue for weeks more as long as Russian units keep feeding assaults into the same sectors and Ukrainian defenses hold the same lines. Next moves will hinge on whether Russian forces shift more troops toward Pokrovsk or spread attacks wider to stretch Ukrainian artillery and drone supplies. A drop below 150 clashes or a clear Russian withdrawal from one of the current hot spots would mark a change in posture; neither indicator has appeared yet. The fighting stays locked on the existing front for the next month.
