18:T4ce,The EU-Canada relationship has moved through trade deals like CETA in 2017 and joint security talks since Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine. On Wednesday in Strasbourg, von der Leyen told the European Parliament that the EU aims to take ties with Canada as far as possible and floated the idea of Canada as the first associate member, prompting a standing ovation for Prime Minister Mark Carney.

This step sits at the proposal stage. Any associate membership would require changes to EU treaties, approval by all 27 member states, and consent from the European Parliament and Council. Carney's government would also need to spell out what sovereignty trade-offs it would accept on tech rules, defence procurement, and energy policy.

The process could stall if one or two member states block treaty tweaks or if Canadian domestic politics shift after the next federal election. It could advance if working groups deliver concrete draft language by early 2027 and core capitals signal support first.

Next visible indicators are whether von der Leyen assigns a commissioner to lead formal talks and whether Carney names a point person in Ottawa by year-end.
