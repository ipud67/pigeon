Seven noncitizens charged in North Texas election-integrity investigation

Federal law has barred noncitizens from voting in national elections since 1996. Texas added its own state charge for illegal voting in 2003 and began routine checks of voter rolls against immigration records in 2017. Those checks expanded after the 2024 election when the new directed federal agencies to share more data with state prosecutors.

On September 15 2026 authorities in North Texas filed charges against seven noncitizens accused of registering to vote or casting ballots in the 2024 election. The cases were brought by the district attorney’s office in coordination with federal immigration records that flagged the as ineligible.

The seven now face state felony counts that carry prison time and deportation proceedings. The filings mark the first batch from an ongoing review that continues to examine registration lists in several North Texas counties.
