Taiwan swimmer Wang Kuan-hung won the men's 200-meter butterfly gold at the Asian Games in Tokyo on Friday in 1 minute 55.05 seconds

Tokyo, Sept. 25 (CNA) Taiwanese swimmer Wang Kuan-hung (王冠閎) won the men's 200-meter butterfly gold medal at the Asian Games in Japan on Friday, leading a 10-men final in 1 minute, 55.05 seconds.
