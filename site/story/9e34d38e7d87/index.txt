17:T4ef,The Texas Department of Transportation stopped approving new installs of Flock automated license plate readers on state roads after privacy and data security questions piled up. TxDOT’s job is limited to checking that the poles and wiring meet safety rules on state right-of-way; it does not set policy on who stores the plate data or how long they keep it. The suspension is a procedural pause while the agency reviews its permit checklist.

Local police departments and cities that already run Flock systems keep using the cameras they installed earlier. The hold affects only new applications on state highways. To lift the suspension TxDOT would need to either update its safety criteria or wait for direction from the legislature or a court order that changes the legal ground rules.

The pause has already slowed rollout schedules in several Texas counties that counted on quick TxDOT sign-off. How long it lasts depends on how fast the agency finishes its internal review and whether lawmakers step in with new rules on data retention.

Next likely step is a revised TxDOT permit form or a formal request from the legislature for broader statewide standards.
