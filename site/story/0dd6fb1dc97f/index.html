Death row inmate Christa Pike in critical condition after 'torture', says lawyer

Translated from Dutch · read the original →
The original headlineTerdoodveroordeelde Christa Pike na 'marteling' in kritieke toestand, zegt advocaat

Christa Pike has sat on death row in Tennessee since a state court sentenced her to die in 2001 for the 1995 murder of a fellow student at the Job Corps center in Knoxville. Tennessee carried out its last execution in 2022 after a pause that began in 2009 over problems with lethal injection drugs and protocols. The state kept the death penalty on the books for capital murder cases and scheduled Pike for execution on September 30 2026.

On that date the lethal injection procedure began but was halted when medical staff could not establish reliable intravenous access after repeated attempts. The execution team stopped the process and returned Pike to her cell. Within hours she developed severe medical complications that her lawyer Randy Spivey described as the result of the prolonged effort to insert lines.

Pike remains in critical condition inside the prison medical unit and is receiving ongoing life-saving treatment. The Tennessee Department of Correction has not released details on the exact nature of her current injuries or the next step for her execution warrant.
