The individuals — from Mexico, Nigeria, India, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo — face counts that include voting by an alien and false statements of citizenship to register. This fits the pattern of state-federal handoff on election cases that started with the 2024 vote audits and moved to indictments in the past week.

The cases now sit with federal prosecutors in Northern Texas. Next step is arraignment and pretrial motions. Convictions would require the government to prove each defendant was not a citizen at the time of registration and casting the ballot. Any plea deals or dropped counts would show up first in court filings.

The situation changes only if federal judges dismiss the charges on citizenship proof or if higher courts narrow the statutes used. Without those moves the prosecutions run their course through trial or settlement.
