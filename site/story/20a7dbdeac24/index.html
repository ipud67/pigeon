RAWALPINDI
September 15, 2026 · 04:19 UTC

PTI office-bearers told to speed up mobilisation for Islamabad protest

Pakistan Tehreek-i-Insaf has run protests in Islamabad for the release of Imran Khan since his arrest in 2023. The party maintains local chapters across Punjab that organise transport, rally points and worker lists for each march on the capital. North Punjab leaders meet regularly with district office-bearers to track headcounts and adjust plans when police move first.

On 15 September 2026 the north Punjab chapter directed its office-bearers to accelerate the drive to gather workers and activists for the 27 September protest. The same directive told participants to stay clear of situations that could lead to arrest. Party officials stated that police had taken 119 prominent workers and activists in six districts of the Rawalpindi division during the previous two days. They put the two-day total for all of north Punjab at 225.

The chapter now works with a shortened timetable and a reduced pool of known activists while still aiming to reach the capital on the set date. The count of detained workers stands as the immediate limit on turnout, and the next days will show how many more the party can add before 27 September.

Thinking ahead
PTI leaders in north Punjab told local office-bearers to push harder on getting workers to the September 27 protest in Islamabad. The party reported 225 arrests of its people across the division in the past two days, with 119 of those in six Rawalpindi districts, and instructed members to stay clear of police to keep the numbers up. This fits the pattern of repeated PTI calls for street action tied to Imran Khan’s detention, where each fresh mobilization round follows fresh arrests and court dates that keep him locked up. The protest will go ahead on the set date unless authorities block roads or make more arrests that cut turnout below usable levels. Next visible steps are the size of the crowd that actually reaches Islamabad and any immediate police response there. The situation shifts only on a court order that releases Khan or a government deal that ends the standoff. It runs at least until September 27 and likely a few days after, depending on what the turnout and crackdown numbers show.
