Judge allows forced feeding of accused National Guard shooter

A federal judge in Washington allowed the Bureau of Prisons to force-feed Rahmanullah, an Afghan national held on charges from an ambush on two National Guard members in the capital last year. The order, issued August 30, 2026 by U.S. District Judge Amit Mehta, permits BOP staff to administer nonconsensual nutrition and medical treatment when the inmate refuses food. Rahmanullah has been in BOP custody since his arrest following the 2025 shooting. The ruling came after prison officials reported his hunger strike had reached a point requiring intervention to sustain his life during pretrial detention.
