The body-camera rollout had been slowed by procurement and training delays that stretched back two years; the new statement marks the point where supply finally caught up. The funding vote follows the same pattern of last-minute deals that have kept shutdown fights off the calendar since the 2024 cycle.

No clear market signal ties the Treasury yields to these two items. The 3-mo bill at 3.92 percent, 2-yr at 4.39 percent, 10-yr at 4.79 percent, 30-yr at 5.27 percent, and 2s10s spread at 40 bps show steady borrowing costs with no fresh move tied to this week's votes.
