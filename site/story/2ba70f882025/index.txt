1f:T49c,Tennessee Governor Bill Lee rejected Christa Pike's clemency petition on Monday after reviewing the 1995 first-degree murder conviction that sent the then-18-year-old to death row. Pike's lawyers filed a last-ditch petition with the US Supreme Court seeking a stay of the lethal injection set for September 30. The court has denied her prior appeals over the past 30 years.

The petition sits with the justices who can grant or deny a temporary halt. A grant would send the case back for further review on claims tied to her age at the time of the offense and the state's execution method. A denial would clear the path for the sentence to be carried out as scheduled.

This marks the latest step on a ladder that began with Pike's 1996 conviction and death sentence, followed by decades of unsuccessful state and federal appeals that kept the execution date intact. No new procedural hurdles appear before the September 30 window.

The situation continues at least until the Supreme Court acts on the petition. It moves next to either a one-time execution or an order for additional hearings if the court intervenes.
