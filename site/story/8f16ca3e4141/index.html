Opposition holding consultations to call all-parties moot on creation of new provinces, other issues: Gohar

Pakistan’s opposition parties have operated inside a parliament where the ruling holds the majority since the February 2024 general elections. The Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf lost its main leader to and faced legal cases that kept many senior figures out of active politics. Smaller parties such as Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam-Fazl stayed in the opposition benches and maintained contact with PTI on shared about governance.

On September 11, 2026, PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan said after a joint meeting with JUI-F that the opposition would begin consultations to an all-parties conference. The agenda listed the possible creation of new provinces, the state of law and order, and the performance of the economy.

The move places fresh pressure on the government to defend its handling of constitutional questions and daily administration. The opposition now works to line up enough parties to make the proposed conference credible and to decide the exact date and format for the gathering.
