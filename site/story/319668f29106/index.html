Sindh Assembly passes LG law amendment amid opposition uproar

The Sindh Local Government Act of 2013 set rules for elected mayors, chairmen and councils to run city and district affairs across the province. Those terms have now ended and new elections have not yet taken place.

On 14 September 2026 the Sindh Assembly passed the Sindh Local Government Act 2026 Bill. The measure gives outgoing mayors and chairmen the power to keep acting as until fresh polls are held. Law Minister Ziaul Hassan Lanjar moved the bill after a special committee submitted its report on the changes.

The new text now forms part of law. Its operation turns on when the government schedules the next local elections and how the continued administrators exercise day-to-day powers in the meantime.
