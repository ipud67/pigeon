Hot potato Box 3 postponed another six months after yet another week of talks

Translated from Dutch · read the original →
The original headlineHete aardappel Box 3 na nog een week overleg een half jaar doorgeschoven

The Netherlands taxes wealth through Box 3. Since 2017 the system has calculated tax on returns rather than actual income from savings, investments and property. Successive governments have adjusted the rates and brackets but kept the core structure in place while talks repeatedly ran into disputes over whether to replace it with a tax on real yields.

On 11 September 2026 the told parliament that coalition parties D66, VVD and CDA had again failed to agree on changes to Box 3. The three parties held another round of talks this week after missing the deadline ahead of Prinsjesdag, the annual budget presentation day. Finance Minister Heinen wrote that differences with opposition parties prevented consensus.

The postponement now runs six months. The future of the wealth tax remains open while the cabinet searches for a new agreement that can pass parliament.
