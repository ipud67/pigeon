H.R. 1276 orders the Department of the Interior to lift deed restrictions on 3.62 acres previously conveyed to Paducah, Kentucky. Federalist 43 explains this clause allows the national government to manage western lands and public holdings without turning them into perpetual federal domains. The other three bills address spending disclosure, an extension for a memorial to emergency medical workers, and tax relief for disaster losses.
