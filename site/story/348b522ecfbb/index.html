Witnesses heard a burst of gunfire around 3 p.m. local time, saw him fall with wounds to the head and chest, then watched the older couple walk away from the scene together. An officer who drove past arrested them minutes later. McKinsey was pronounced dead where he lay. The couple remained in the area until police took them into .
