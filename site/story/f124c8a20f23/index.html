Supreme Court will hear case on Trump asylum ban at Mexico border

Presidents have issued to limit entry at the southern border for decades. On one such proclamation the Trump directed the Department of Homeland Security to bar noncitizens from applying for and to remove them without the usual procedural steps.

The case asks the justices to decide whether the proclamation and the guidance issued under it can stand after lower courts blocked them. The D.C. Circuit had largely upheld a district court's order that blocked the policy after a group of individuals and nonprofits the matter.

The case now sits on the court's docket for the 2026-27 term alongside a separate securities-fraud matter. The justices will hear argument on the limits of presidential suspension authority under the immigration statute and what procedural protections that statute requires before removal.
