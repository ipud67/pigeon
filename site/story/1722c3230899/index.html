Watch live: House GOP leaders speak as vote to avert shutdown looms

House GOP leaders, including Speaker Mike Johnson, spoke on the House floor Tuesday as lawmakers prepared to vote on a Senate-passed continuing resolution. The measure extends federal government funding from the Sept. 30 deadline through Dec. 11. The Senate passed its version of the stop-gap bill earlier in September. This marks the latest step in the annual process that has repeatedly used short-term funding extensions to avoid shutdowns when full-year bills are not completed by the fiscal year end.
