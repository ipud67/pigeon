1a:T503,ICE ran a five-day sweep in Indiana called Operation Safe Community-Indiana and picked up 426 people who were in the country illegally. The arrests happened statewide with heavy focus on the Indianapolis area and included members of MS-13 and Tren de Aragua plus people with records for sex crimes, assault, drug trafficking, weapons offenses, burglary, and DUI. No incidents were reported during the operation, which used partnerships with state and local agencies under the 287(g) program that lets those agencies help enforce immigration law.

This fits the pattern of stepped-up ICE enforcement that has run without reported resistance since the current administration took office. The agency has kept expanding these joint operations across multiple states by adding more local partners to the 287(g) agreements. The procedural next step is continued sweeps in the same region while ICE processes the cases for removal.

The pace holds steady as long as state and local agencies keep signing on and field offices report no operational pushback. It slows or stops only if federal funding drops, court orders block 287(g) use in Indiana, or local departments pull out of the partnerships.
