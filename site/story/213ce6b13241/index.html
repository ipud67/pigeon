IRAN
September 2, 2026

Iran state media reports U.S. strike hit wedding, killing 4, CENTCOM denies civilians targeted

“never targets civilians.”
Overview
U.S. Central Command spokesman Commander Tim Hawkins said, unlike Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, the U.S. military "never targets civilians."
Short history
US and Israeli strikes on Iran began 27 February 2026. A June lifted the mutual on the Strait of Hormuz and set a 60-day negotiation period. That deal collapsed on 8 July when Iran hit commercial tankers; Trump ended the the same day and US strikes resumed nightly. The fighting has since centered on control of the strait. On 2 September 2026 Iranian state media reported a US strike hit a wedding and killed four civilians. CENTCOM said the US military never targets civilians.
Predictive model
US and Iranian forces have been trading strikes and counter-strikes since late February 2026, with the fight now narrowed to control of the Strait of Hormuz. The latest rung is a reported US strike on 2 September that Iranian state media says hit a wedding and killed four civilians; CENTCOM says it does not target civilians and that its operations focus on military sites. Both sides have stuck to their public lines for months: Iran calls every civilian death proof of aggression, the US calls Iranian claims propaganda while continuing nightly strikes on surveillance, logistics, weapons storage and maritime targets. This pattern of claim and denial has run since the first weeks of the war and shows no sign of breaking. Each reported civilian incident adds another round of Iranian statements and proxy pressure but has not yet forced a pause in US operations or a new diplomatic opening. The procedural next step is continued US strikes on Hormuz-related targets and Iranian attempts to harass shipping or launch missiles in response. Without a clear shift in either side’s red lines or a third-party mediation effort that both accept, the exchange of strikes and competing narratives is likely to grind on through the fall. Horizon estimate: at least another 6–8 weeks of the same cycle unless one side visibly changes its targeting rules or accepts outside talks.
