The foreign minister also barred Umar Kremlev, a boxing official who helped fund a Trump family wedding. The move follows a pattern of Latvia listing Russian-linked figures since the 2022 invasion of Ukraine, with entry bans that last five years unless lifted by cabinet decision.

Ovechkin holds dual Russian citizenship and has played in the NHL since 2005. The ban blocks him from Latvia for official games or personal travel. Similar lists already cover hundreds of Russian officials, athletes, and business people.

The ban holds until at least 2031 unless Ovechkin or Kremlev appeal through diplomatic channels and Latvia’s cabinet removes them. No public sign of such a request has surfaced. Future changes would require a shift in Latvia’s foreign ministry stance or a broader thaw in EU-Russia travel rules that shows no current movement.
