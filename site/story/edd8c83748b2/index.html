East-West Road: Two die as families sue FG, contractor for N1bn

Construction of Phase IV of the East-West Road in Akwa Ibom State reached the Ataobong Bypass section years after the federal project began. Work on that stretch changed how rainwater moved across the ground. The new drainage sent floodwater into homes and businesses beside the road instead of away from them.

On 15 September 2026 two people died when buildings collapsed during heavy night rain in the bypass area. Isaac Nkenyie was killed when his house fell. A child from the Etito family died in a separate collapse the same night. Families of the dead and other residents filed suit marked HEK/20/2026 that day in the state high court in Eket. The named the Minister of Works, Gitto Construczioni General Nigeria Limited, the Akwa Ibom State Government and the estate valuers Iniama and Partners. They demanded one billion naira in general damages for loss of life, property, businesses and land taken without payment. They also asked the court to order full already valued for marked properties and to stop any selective payments to some owners while others waited.

The case now turns on whether the court will compel the defendants to complete the stalled compensation process and whether further flooding can be stopped while the road work continues.
