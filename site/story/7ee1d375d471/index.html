WASHINGTON
October 5, 2026

Commerce kept antidumping duties on carbon steel plate from China and Russia in Washington

The U.S. has maintained antidumping duty orders on cut-to-length carbon steel plate from China since October 21 2003 and from Russia since August 14 2023. These orders impose extra duties on the imported steel plate when it is sold below fair value. The Act of 1930 as amended requires Commerce to review the orders every five years to decide whether they should continue.

On June 1 2026 Commerce started the fifth sunset reviews of both orders. Domestic producers filed notices of intent to participate by mid-June and submitted full substantive responses by July 1. No Russian or Chinese producers filed responses. Under the and Commerce rules this triggered expedited 120-day reviews without full hearings or new data collection.

Commerce published its final results on October 5 2026. The department determined that revoking the orders would likely lead to continued dumping at the margins listed in the notice. The orders therefore remain in place at their existing rates until the next scheduled review.

Thinking ahead
