Oso has spent 27 years in the state civil service, rising from education officer II in 1999 to director of public schools in the ministry of education before this posting.

The appointment follows standard procedure for filling senior civil service posts and does not require legislative approval. Oso takes over an administrative role that reports inside the board and carries out policies already set by the governor’s office. No public signs of dispute or vacancy pressure have surfaced.

This pattern of internal promotions for experienced directors has held steady across Ogun state ministries for the past several years. Unless a policy shift at the state level or a change in governor occurs, the board’s operational setup stays on the same track.
