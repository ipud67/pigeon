Pope Leo XIV will celebrate 4 large masses in Peru: dates, times and places

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlinePapa León XIV celebrará 4 multitudinarias misas en Perú: fechas, horas y lugares

Pope Leo XIV became the first pontiff from the United States when the College of Cardinals elected him in May 2025. He chose the papal name in honor of Leo XIII, who issued the 1891 encyclical on workers' rights. The new pope scheduled an apostolic visit to Peru for late September 2026, the first such trip by any pope since Francis went there in 2018.

The schedule released by the Peruvian Conference on September 26 sets out four public masses. On September 28 he will celebrate one at 10 a.m. in Chiclayo. The next day he will lead a second mass at 4 p.m. in the same city. On October 1 he will offer mass at 9 a.m. in Pucallpa. The final mass is set for 5 p.m. on October 3 in Lima.

The four ceremonies mark the central public events of the six-day visit. Each will draw crowds of Peruvians and foreign pilgrims to the designated venues where the pope will and deliver the homily.
