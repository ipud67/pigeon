The strike damaged eight houses and left nine civilians aged 46 to 74 with blast injuries, shrapnel wounds, fractures, and mine-like trauma. Donetsk Oblast Police reported four women and five men hurt, one woman in serious condition.

This fits the pattern of regular Russian rocket and artillery strikes on Donetsk Oblast towns behind the current front line. Ukrainian forces hold Kramatorsk as a key logistics and command hub in the Donbas sector.

The attacks rely on MLRS systems like Uragan, which have a range of about 35 kilometers and fire unguided rockets, some carrying cluster submunitions. Continued use points to Russian forces keeping pressure on Ukrainian rear areas without shifting major ground units forward in this sector right now.

Next moves will likely stay in the same lane: more strikes on the same towns if Russian supply of rockets holds and weather allows targeting. A real change would need either Russian troops advancing close enough to use shorter-range guns or a Ukrainian push that forces Russian launchers to pull back.
