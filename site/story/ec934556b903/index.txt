21:T4b9,The UN Security Council committee that handles the 1267 sanctions list updated one entry on 28 September 2026. It changed the name Khatiba Jama`at al-Tawhid wal-Jihad from its former alias Jannat Oshiklari and listed the group as operating under the Al-Nusrah Front umbrella with about 500 fighters active in Syrian provinces plus cross-border moves into Turkey and Central Asian states. The committee acts on information supplied by member states and regional bodies, then posts the revised list on its public site.

This kind of update is routine maintenance that has happened dozens of times since the list began in 1999. It keeps the assets freeze, travel ban, and arms embargo current without adding or removing any group. The next change will occur only when a member state supplies fresh information that meets the committee’s criteria for listing, delisting, or further amendment.

The situation stays stable until new verified details on the group’s structure, location, or ties reach the committee. No procedural deadline exists, so the list can sit unchanged for months or years between updates.
