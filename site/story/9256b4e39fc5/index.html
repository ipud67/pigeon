November 17, 2026 · 21:55 UTC

U.S. military struck an IRGC missile site on Iran's Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz

The memorandum collapsed on 8 July after Iran struck commercial tankers bypassing its approved routes and the IRGC moved to shut the strait again.

On 17 November US Central Command struck a missile launch site run by the Islamic Revolutionary Guard on Qeshm Island inside the strait. A US military official described the action as self-defense after that targeted American personnel and assets.

Iranian state media reported the strike caused no damage to residential commercial or infrastructural areas in Hormozgan province. US Central Command said it remains highly and fully postured to protect Americans across the region. The conflict now turns on whether either side can force the other to accept terms that reopen safe passage through the strait.
