IMF switches top economist candidate at last minute to avoid Trump criticism

Translated from Dutch · read the original →
The original headline'IMF wisselt op laatste moment van topeconoom om kritiek Trump te vermijden'

The IMF has filled its chief economist post through a board vote that selects one candidate from a short list of academics and central bankers. The process began after the previous chief economist Gita Gopinath stepped down earlier this year.

On September 10 the board dropped Portuguese economist Ricardo Reis, a London School of Economics professor who once wrote against aspects of Donald Trump’s trade policy, and installed a different economist in his place. The switch came after board members voiced concern that Reis’s past statements would draw public attacks from the incoming Trump .

The IMF now has a new chief economist whose selection avoided that friction. The institution’s leadership continues to set forecasts and policy advice for its 190 member countries under the same voting rules that produced the last-minute change.
