County prosecutors in Washington state cut deal with anti-Israel 'UW33,' accused of causing $1 million in damage at University of Washington

King County prosecutors reached agreements with 33 people charged after they occupied the Interdisciplinary Engineering Building at the University of Washington on May 5, 2025. The group, known as UW33 and affiliated with the student organization SUPER-UW, entered the building that Boeing had helped fund with a $10 million contribution. Police reports listed $1 million in damage and first-degree criminal trespass counts against the defendants, who included students. The university placed 23 of them on emergency suspension and later revoked SUPER-UW's official status as a recognized group.

On September 16, 2026, the prosecutor's office filed stipulated orders of continuance. The orders require each defendant to complete community service and avoid new violations for 12 months. Defendants must also accept the police reports as accurate without entering guilty pleas. Charges will be dismissed at the end of that period if the terms hold. A court can revoke the order after a hearing and move to a stipulated bench trial if any term is broken.

The agreements mean none of the defendants will face jail, trial, or fines provided they meet the conditions through May 2027. The university had already reinstated all suspended students after its own disciplinary process concluded.
