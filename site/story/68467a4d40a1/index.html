Jury convicts Jane Frances Pierce of fraud and money laundering in a tax refund scheme

The jury convicted Pierce of mail , filing false tax returns, corruptly interfering with an IRS investigation, and money laundering. Court records showed she obtained more than one million dollars from the first set of filings, spent the money on a house and relatives' mortgages, then sent the IRS a false return, a fake check, and a false court document while laundering the rest.

Pierce now awaits sentencing on February 16, 2027. The district court will set the term after review of the U.S. Sentencing Guidelines, with maximums of 20 years for mail fraud, 10 years per money laundering count, and three years each for the false-return and counts, plus supervised release, , and monetary penalties.
