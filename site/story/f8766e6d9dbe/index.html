Carmel Shelly said Israelis are brave people and not tired though tired of war after her son IDF Maj Ben Shelly died in Gaza in 2023

“Israelis are brave, brave, brave people. And we are not tired, though we are tired of war,” said Carmel Shelly, whose 26-year-old son, IDF Maj. Ben Shelly, died in Gaza on Dec. 12, 2023.
