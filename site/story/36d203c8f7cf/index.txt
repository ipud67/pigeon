17:T447,Visually impaired fans at Rock en Seine are getting live audio descriptions of the shows through headphones and receivers supplied by the Valentin Haüy Association. The setup puts them on dedicated platforms so they can follow the stage action in real time, and the festival is running one described concert per day from August 26 through August 30. The association has been pushing this service for years, so the offer this week is the latest step in a steady rollout rather than a one-off experiment.

The program will run for the three remaining days of the current festival. After that it stops unless the festival books the same service for next year’s dates. No big procedural hurdle stands in the way; it only takes the organizers to renew the partnership and the association to supply the gear again.

If attendance numbers or feedback from this run are strong, the festival could expand the number of described shows in 2027. If turnout stays low or the association shifts resources, the daily slot could shrink or disappear.
