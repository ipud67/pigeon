20:T48b,DEA just put four synthetic opioids — 5,6-dichloro brorphine, 5,6-dichloro desmethylchlorphine, N-propionitrile chlorphine, and spirochlorphine — into Schedule I on a temporary basis. The move follows the usual DEA playbook for new nitazene-type compounds that show up in the illicit supply and are linked to overdoses. This temporary order locks in manufacturing, distribution, and possession controls for two years while the agency gathers data for a permanent rule.

The order runs 24 months from the August 27, 2026 effective date unless DEA publishes a final scheduling decision sooner or Congress steps in. Expect the next public step to be either an extension notice or a formal permanent rule once lab and forensic data packages are complete. The only things that would change the trajectory are a successful court challenge on the “imminent hazard” finding or new legislation that alters the emergency scheduling process.

This is the latest rung in the DEA’s rolling temporary scheduling of novel opioids that keep appearing faster than permanent rules can be written.
