Suspect confesses to series of assaults and mistreatments in Delft

Translated from Dutch · read the original →
The original headlineVerdachte bekent reeks aanrandingen en mishandelingen Delft

Police in Delft had been investigating several assaults on women near the Delftse Hout park over the past few months. The attacks involved pushing victims to the ground and other mistreatment. Officers collected DNA from the scenes but had no immediate suspect.

On 10 July a woman was pushed down in one of the incidents. Earlier this week authorities arrested a 19-year-old man from the area on suspicion of that assault in nearby Den Hoorn. During questioning he admitted to five separate attacks around Delft. DNA taken from him matched evidence left at the scenes.

The Public Service now holds confessions and forensic links that tie the man to the string of cases. The investigation that began months earlier with scattered reports has narrowed to one suspect in custody.

Thinking ahead
The 19-year-old suspect arrested this week in Den Hoorn has now admitted to five separate assaults and mistreatments near Delft, with DNA evidence tying him to the cases. Police had linked the incidents through reports of women being pushed, grabbed and assaulted around the Delftse Hout over recent months, the latest on 10 July. Dutch prosecutors will now move the file to an examining magistrate for formal charges and a decision on whether he stays in custody while the full investigation wraps up. This stays in the court pipeline until the prosecutor files the indictment and a trial date is set. The next visible step is the first court hearing, which usually comes within weeks if the suspect remains detained. The situation changes only if the magistrate orders release or the defense files a successful challenge to the confession or the DNA match. The case will likely run through pretrial hearings and a full trial within the next three to six months unless new victims come forward or evidence disputes the confession. Horizon: early 2027 for verdict.
