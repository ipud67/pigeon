UN panel changed one name on its Congo sanctions list

The United Nations Security set up a committee in 2004 under resolution 1533 to keep a list of individuals and entities tied to conflict in the Democratic Republic of the Congo and to measures that block their travel, freeze their assets, and bar arms supplies to them.

On 11 November 2026 the committee one entry on that sanctions list by striking through and underlining specific text in the name and identifying information of the listed entity.
