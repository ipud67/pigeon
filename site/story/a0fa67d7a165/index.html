The two new trials against Cristina Fernández will begin on October 23 and March 19, 2027

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineLos dos nuevos juicios contra Cristina Fernández comenzarán el 23 de octubre y el 19 de marzo de 2027

Cristina Fernández de Kirchner has faced federal charges in Argentina since 2016. Prosecutors first opened the Vialidad case that year over road contracts awarded during her 2007-2015 presidencies. A second probe, known as the Cuadernos case, began in 2018 after notebooks surfaced that described a cash network tied to public works. Both cases reached trial in 2022. A three-judge panel convicted her in the Vialidad matter in December 2022 and sentenced her to six years in prison while barring her from holding public office. An appeals court upheld that verdict in 2024. The Cuadernos trial remains pending.

A federal court in Buenos Aires set the start dates for two additional trials against the former president on September 17. One trial opens October 23. The other opens March 19, 2027. The new proceedings concern separate sets of contracts and bribery that prosecutors carved out from the earlier investigations.
