NIGERIA
September 20, 2026 · 19:47 UTC

Enyimba lost 1-0 at home to Inter Lagos on Sunday and dropped to the bottom of the NPFL table after Kano Pillars beat Doma United

The 2026-27 Nigeria Premier Football League season opened with 20 teams playing a round-robin format that awards three points for a win and one for a draw. After the first three matchweeks, only one side had failed to collect a point while several others sat level on seven points near the top of the table.

On Sunday Enyimba lost their third straight game when Inter Lagos scored once through Ezekiel Moses in the 18th minute to take all three points at the Aba club’s home ground. That result left the nine-time champions at the foot of the 20-team table with a goal difference of minus four. The same afternoon Kano Pillars beat Doma United 4-1, with goals from Abdullahi Musa in the 20th minute, captain Rabiu Ali 13 minutes later, Ahmed Musa in the 77th, and Nasiru Salihu. Doma United stayed first with nine points from four matches, two ahead of Barau. Kano Pillars rose to third on seven points from three games, level with Rivers United, Rangers International and Plateau United.

Plateau United came from a goal down to win 3-1 against Shooting Stars, Abia Warriors beat Sporting Lagos 2-0 with both goals after the 88th minute, Warri Wolves edged Kwara United 1-0, Nasarawa United drew 1-1 with Rivers United, and Rangers International defeated Kun Kalifat 4-2 in the Oriental Derby.
