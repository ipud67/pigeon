LOS ANGELES
November 4, 2026

A Los Angeles woman faced charges for an 18 million dollar covid relief fraud scheme

The Paycheck Protection Program and Economic Injury Disaster Loan program operated under the Coronavirus Aid Relief and Economic Security Act.

A grand jury in the Central District of California Marisela Nuno 53 of Los Angeles on an of an 18 million dollar scheme. Court papers state she filed applications for businesses she claimed to own that together sought more than 15.7 million dollars in Paycheck Protection Program funds and more than 2.6 million dollars in Economic Injury Disaster Loan funds. She then spent the proceeds on private property business startups and payments to relatives.

Nuno stands charged with wire and money laundering. The case was investigated by the FBI Los Angeles Field Office IRS Criminal Investigation and Small Business Administration Office of Inspector General and is prosecuted by trial attorneys from the Justice Department Fraud Division. An indictment remains an allegation until conviction.

A federal grand jury in Los Angeles indicted Marisela Nuno on three counts of wire fraud and one count of money laundering for an $18 million scheme. She allegedly filed false PPP and EIDL loan applications for businesses she controlled, overstating employee counts, payroll, revenues, and eligibility while attaching fake tax records. The case sits inside the new Justice Department Fraud Division formed on April 7 and the broader Task Force to Eliminate Fraud. No court dates have been set. The indictment itself is the only public marker so far. Too few indicators to forecast how long the case will run or what comes next. Horizon: unknown.
