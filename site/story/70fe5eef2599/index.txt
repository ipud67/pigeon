18:T5b4,A federal appeals court struck down a Trump administration order that kept the J.H. Campbell coal-fired power plant in West Olive, Michigan, running past its planned retirement date. The D.C. Circuit ruled that the Energy Department exceeded its statutory authority in issuing the directive.

The Constitution grants the federal government only enumerated powers. Article I, Section 8 lists Congress’s authorities over commerce, but it does not hand the executive branch open-ended power to force private facilities to operate against their owners’ plans. The founders designed the federal structure to prevent exactly this kind of national command over everyday economic decisions that belong to states and individuals.

The Tenth Amendment reserves to the states and the people all powers not delegated to the United States. Michigan regulated its own electric generation and retirement schedules under that retained authority. Washington’s attempt to override it runs against the founding settlement that left most internal improvements and local industry to the people on the ground.

Federalist 45 explains that the powers delegated to the federal government are few and defined, while those remaining in the state governments are numerous and indefinite. Energy Department officials ignored that line when they stepped in to keep one plant open by decree. 1b:T407,The D.C. Circuit on Friday struck down the Energy Department's order that kept the J.H. Campbell coal plant in West Olive open past its planned shutdown date. The three-judge panel held that the department lacked statutory power to issue the directive. The ruling follows the plant owner's earlier plan to retire the unit and the Trump administration's intervention to extend operations.

The decision sends the case back to the lower court for any further proceedings on related claims. The Energy Department can ask the full appeals court to rehear the matter or petition the Supreme Court for review. The plant stays on its retirement track unless the government secures a stay or a new order grounded in different legal authority.

This round of litigation has run eight months since the original department order. Observable next indicators are a formal mandate from the appeals court and any filing for en banc review within the standard 45-day window.
