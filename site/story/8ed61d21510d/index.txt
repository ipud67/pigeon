1d:T436,USCIS put out the latest inflation tweak to the immigration fees set by H.R. 1. The law passed July 4 2025 laid out base fees for fiscal 2025 and ordered yearly CPI-U adjustments rounded down to the next $10 multiple. Last year’s notice on November 21 2025 set the fiscal 2026 rates that took effect January 1. This notice gives the fiscal 2027 schedule effective October 16 2026; any filing postmarked on or after that date without the new amount gets sent back. The announcement follows the same CPI-U formula used in the prior round.

The adjustment runs on a fixed annual clock written into the statute. Nothing in the current 3-month Treasury bill rate at 4.2 percent, the 2-year yield at 4.88 percent, the 10-year at 5.29 percent, the 30-year at 5.64 percent, or the 2s10s spread at 41 basis points changes the statutory trigger or the rounding rule. The next rung will be the fiscal 2028 notice expected late 2027 using the July 2026 to July 2027 CPI-U change.

This pattern continues at least through the current law’s horizon.
