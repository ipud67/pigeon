18:T465,Argentina has secured US State Department approval to buy four UH-60L Black Hawk helicopters. The deal covers new Sikorsky-built airframes fitted with General Electric engines, radios, transponders, and rescue gear. This step follows standard export authorization and now moves to contract finalization and production scheduling by the manufacturers.

The purchase sits on a ladder of Argentine military modernization that has included prior US approvals for other equipment. Observable indicators are the approval notice itself and the listed equipment package; no delivery timeline has been released. How long this takes depends on production line slots at Sikorsky and any additional Argentine budget or training steps that must clear.

If Congress raises no objection in the next 30 days and Argentina signs the letter of offer and acceptance, the program advances to build. A funding shortfall or policy shift in Buenos Aires would stall it. Absent those signals the helicopters reach operational status in 24 to 36 months.
