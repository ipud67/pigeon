Court records show the woman filled out the paperwork, sent a photo of the man in front of her house, and later pretended to be a household member when HHS called.

The pair has been in custody since late 2025 and early 2026. Sentencing is locked in for January 6, 2027. Each faces up to five years per count, though the judge will set the actual time using federal guidelines that look at prior record, role in the offense, and acceptance of responsibility.

This case sits inside a larger federal push by Joint Task Force Alpha to catch sponsorship fraud tied to smuggling networks. No new arrests or policy shifts have surfaced since the plea. The next visible step is the presentence report and the January hearing; nothing in the record points to a faster track or a bigger roundup yet.

The situation stays on the sentencing calendar unless prosecutors file a cooperation deal or the judge grants a delay.
