Illegal Alien from Honduras and Kansas Woman Plead Guilty to Fraudulently Seeking to Obtain Custody of an Unaccompanied Alien Child

The Office of Refugee Resettlement at the Department of Health and Human Services requires anyone who wants to sponsor an unaccompanied alien child to submit an application that lists a valid home address and household members and to answer questions from agency staff about those details. That process has operated under federal rules since Congress assigned the agency responsibility for the and placement of such children in 2002.

On September 30 Romulo Hernandez Murillo, a 46-year-old man from Honduras who entered the country without authorization, and Lisa Marie Lopez Perdomo, 49, of Syracuse, Kansas, each pleaded guilty in federal court in Kansas to charges that they submitted a false sponsorship application and followed it with a false phone interview. Court records state that Hernandez Murillo paid Lopez Perdomo to prepare and file paperwork claiming he lived at her Syracuse address with her and that she posed as a household member when an HHS employee called to verify the information. A photograph of Hernandez Murillo standing in front of her house was also sent with the packet.

The two have been in federal custody since late 2025 and early 2026. Each now faces a maximum of five years in prison on each count. A federal district judge will set their sentences on January 6, 2027, after reviewing the U.S. Sentencing Guidelines and other factors required by . The case was investigated by HHS inspectors general, Homeland Security Investigations, and other federal and state agencies and prosecuted by the Justice Department’s Human Rights and Special Prosecutions Section as part of Joint Task Force Alpha.
