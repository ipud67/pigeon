He is listed as the head of La Nueva Jauría, a group that competes with Los Pulpos for control of extortion, contract killings, and related crimes in La Libertad. The move is a standard step that increases the cash payout for information leading to his arrest.

The reward program has worked before in the same region by pushing informants to come forward once the price on a leader’s head rises. Police still need a solid tip, followed by a raid or negotiated surrender, to take him off the street. Until that tip arrives or the group fractures under pressure, the extortion and killings tied to the rivalry are likely to keep going at their current pace.

This one change alone does not shift the balance between the two gangs. Only an arrest, a major seizure of their money or weapons, or a clear split inside La Nueva Jauría would mark a real turn.
