Murder accused former police officer Beau Lamarre-Condon trial begins

Beau Lamarre-Condon worked as a New South Wales police officer until February 2024. That month Jesse Baird, a television presenter for Channel Ten, and Luke Davies, a Qantas flight attendant, were last seen at a pre-Mardi Gras party in Sydney. Police later found their personal items in a bin at Cronulla. Days after a public appeal for information, officers charged Lamarre-Condon with their deaths. Their bodies turned up the next week in surf bags at a rural property in Bungonia, 200 kilometres south-west of the city.

On 20 September 2026 the murder trial of the 31-year-old former officer opened in the New South Wales Supreme Court at King Street in Sydney. He has pleaded not guilty to two counts of domestic violence-related murder and one count of aggravated break and enter. The break-and-enter charge relates to the same Paddington terrace in August 2023. The Crown alleges he used his service firearm to shoot the couple inside the shared terrace at Paddington in Sydney’s east.

The case now sits before Justice Natalie Adams. A jury is being selected from a larger pool before the Crown Prosecutor delivers an opening address. The trial is listed to run for three months.
