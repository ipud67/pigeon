Taiwan's Lin Yun-ju finished runner-up in the men's singles at the WTT China Smash in Beijing on Sunday

Taipei, Oct. 12 (CNA) Taiwan's Lin Yun-ju (林昀儒) finished second in the men's singles at the World Table Tennis (WTT) China Smash on Sunday in the final against world No. 1 Sora Matsushima of Japan.
