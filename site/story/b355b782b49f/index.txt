26:T588,The FDA put out notice on September 18 2026 that it will start taking requests October 1 for year five of the CMC Development and Readiness Pilot. The program has run every year since fiscal 2023 under the PDUFA VII commitment letter and gives selected sponsors extra meetings plus science and risk based options to line up manufacturing data with fast moving clinical trials for drugs or biologics aimed at serious conditions with no good treatment yet. Sponsors must show their product is in an accelerated IND program, meets the unmet need standard, and faces real CMC timing pressure; FDA then picks participants based on those same criteria plus how much the extra help could speed readiness while still hitting CGMP rules for later approval.

The pilot itself has kept the same structure and goals across the prior four cycles with no announced change in scope or eligibility. Requests will be reviewed as they come in after the October 1 start date using the published selection process. No new legislative or court trigger appears in the notice so the program runs on the existing PDUFA VII timeline that ends in 2027.

This cycle will run at least through fiscal 2027 unless Congress alters PDUFA or FDA drops the pilot in the next commitment letter. Next concrete step is the first batch of sponsor requests arriving in early October 2026 and FDA decisions on who gets in.
