Trump pardons golf caddy whose drug-dealing conviction kept him from British Open: ‘I got a chance now’

Eric Larson worked as a caddy on the PGA Tour for more than two decades until a 1990s drug-dealing barred him from obtaining the international travel clearance required for major tournaments outside the United States. That restriction kept him off the grounds of the British Open and other overseas events for years even after he had rebuilt his career inside the ropes at domestic stops.

On September 19 President Trump issued a full for the conviction. The order restored Larson's eligibility for the visas and entry documents he needs to cross borders with the players he serves.

Larson now holds the paperwork that lets him compete for a spot in the British Open field next year. The pardon stands as the final administrative step that removed the last legal barrier between him and the tournaments that had been closed to him since the conviction.
