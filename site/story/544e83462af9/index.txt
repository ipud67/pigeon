A detective said the 29-year-old died in a drug-related dispute and that the scene was staged to look otherwise. The medical examiner ruled the death a homicide. Three men, all Black, have been arrested in the case so far. Jarques Ratliff, 51, pleaded not guilty and stays in custody without bond. Earnest Lloyd Jr, 25, was arrested this week. Eric Clark, 45, turned himself in Friday on a murder charge and is due in court Monday.

The investigation has moved from scene examination and initial arrests to formal charging and early court appearances. Fortune's mother has spoken publicly about the drug link and her belief that her daughter fought back. No exact cause of death has been released.

The case will likely stay in local court for the next several months as evidence is presented and pleas are entered. It moves forward only if prosecutors file a full indictment and the defense files motions. If new witnesses or forensic details surface, the timeline stretches. If all three defendants reach plea deals, it ends quicker.

Next court date for Clark on Monday is the clearest near-term marker.
