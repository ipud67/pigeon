The stalled low-pressure system that fed off warm Gulf of Thailand moisture has moved on but the ground stays saturated and rivers run high. Monsoon patterns in the region often drag into late October so the flooding is likely to linger in low spots for another two to four weeks until drier air settles in or officials release water from upstream reservoirs in a controlled way. Relief crews will keep moving sandbags and pumping out urban districts while farm recovery waits on the water to drop enough for replanting.

Next visible shift will show up in official tallies of displaced families dropping or in new satellite passes that measure how much standing water has receded. If another tropical disturbance tracks across the Gulf the numbers could climb again.
