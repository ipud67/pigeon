Security Council Committee Established Pursuant to Resolution 2653 (2022) concerning Haiti Discusses Final Report of Panel of Experts

The UN Security Council set up a committee on Haiti in 2022 under resolution 2653 to oversee measures against individuals and groups involved in gang violence and arms trafficking. In December 2025 the council passed resolution 2794 which created a panel of experts and required it to deliver a final report after one year of monitoring.

On 3 September 2026 the committee met in informal consultations to review that final report which covered October 2025 through July 2026. The panel coordinator briefed members that the panel had made three visits to Haiti and trips to neighboring countries during its mandate. The coordinator reported that Haitian security operations begun in December 2025 stopped further gang advances in Port-au-Prince and cleared some neighborhoods yet left most of the capital still under gang control. The same period brought a new surge of violence in the lower Artibonite, Centre and South-East departments where the gangs showed resilience and ability to adapt. The panel also handed over ten recommendations for making the sanctions regime work better.
