South Africa: Thabo Bester Trial Postponed Again, This Time to 2027

Thabo Bester escaped from Mangaung prison in May 2022. Police caught him in Tanzania in April 2023 and brought him back. He and his co-accused, Nandipha Magudumana, face charges of murder, fraud, and escape from lawful custody. On 28 August 2026 the High Court in Bloemfontein postponed their plea and trial to 19 April 2027 after Bester’s lawyers said prison conditions blocked his access to a laptop needed to prepare his defence.

Thinking ahead
The Thabo Bester trial has been pushed back once more, now set for April 19 2027. His lawyers keep flagging bad prison conditions and no proper laptop for him to review evidence and build his case. Court records show this is the latest in a string of delays driven by defence complaints about access and preparation time. Look for the next hearing to test whether prison authorities fix the laptop and conditions issue. If Bester’s team keeps getting blocked on basic prep tools the case will drag further. Real change only comes if the court forces handover of usable equipment or switches his custody setup enough to satisfy the defence. Without those fixes the pattern points to more postponements. Horizon: likely stuck in procedural delays through 2027.
