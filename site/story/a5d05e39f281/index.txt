17:T428,The Commerce Department just wrapped the expedited third sunset review and ruled that dumping from China would likely resume if the antidumping order on steel grating is lifted. This order has been in place since 2010, with prior reviews in 2016 and 2021 both keeping the duties alive. The latest finding locks the order in for another five years unless a full review changes it later.

The decision rests on straight Commerce data showing Chinese exporters would undercut U.S. prices again without the duties. No new court action or industry petition is listed as the trigger; it is the routine statutory review cycle hitting its third round. Trade flows and pricing margins cited in the notice line up with the pattern that kept the order going twice before.

With no fresh indicators pointing to quick repeal, the order looks set to run at least until the next mandatory review around 2031. Steel grating imports from China will stay hit with the same antidumping rates listed in the final results.
