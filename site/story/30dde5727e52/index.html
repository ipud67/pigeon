Fox News host says Trump made her husband Transportation secretary ‘because that was all that’s left’

Fox News host Rachel Campos-Duffy said on Sept. 2, 2026, that President Trump chose her husband Sean Duffy as Transportation secretary because that was all that’s left. The comment came during an interview on “The Katie Miller Podcast,” hosted by the wife of White House chief of staff Stephen Miller. Duffy, a former Wisconsin congressman and Fox News host, had not been named to a post in Trump’s first term that began in 2017. Trump announced Duffy’s nomination for the Transportation role in late 2025 after his reelection.
