"She had taste in everything": assistant to ex-defence council secretary killed in Kyiv strike on Academy of Sciences

Nataliia Bukalo worked as assistant to Volodymyr Horbulin, who served as secretary of Ukraine's National Security and Defence Council from 1994 to 1999 and later became vice president of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine. She handled documents, phone logs, schedules and event planning inside the academy's buildings in central Kyiv. Horbulin kept an office there after he left government.

A Russian drone struck the academy building on 28 September 2026. The drone hit a room one floor above Horbulin's office. Glass from the blast cut him. Bukalo stood near the building at the time of impact and died on the spot. Her husband answered her phone when a colleague called to check on her.

Horbulin has returned home with minor injuries. Bukalo leaves a husband and a daughter. The strike forms the latest Russian attack on non-military sites inside the Ukrainian capital this year.

Thinking ahead
A Russian drone hit a National Academy of Sciences building in Kyiv on September 28 and killed Nataliia Bukalo, assistant to former National Security and Defence Council secretary Volodymyr Horbulin. Horbulin, now vice president of the academy, was in his office one floor below, took minor glass injuries, and went home. Bukalo had been outside the building at the time of the strike. This is the latest in a pattern of Russian strikes on Ukrainian government and research sites in the capital that began after the full-scale invasion and continued through 2025-2026. The strike follows repeated Russian use of single drones or small groups against non-military targets in rear areas. The pattern holds as long as Russian forces keep long-range drones and missiles in inventory and choose to allocate some of them to Kyiv. Next moves depend on Russian launch rates and Ukrainian air-defense coverage over the city; a sustained drop in either would change the frequency. No indicator yet shows a shift in targeting priorities or defense posture around academy buildings. Likely to continue at current pace until supply or interception rates move.
