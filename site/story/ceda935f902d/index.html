Cuban delegation walks out of UN after hearing Trump say the regime 'will end up falling'

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineLa delegación cubana abandona la ONU al oír a Trump decir que el régimen “acabará cayendo”

The United States has maintained a trade on Cuba since 1960 under the Trading with the Enemy Act. Successive kept the sanctions in place through the Cuban Act of 1992 and the Helms-Burton Act of 1996, which codified the restrictions and limited presidential easing without approval. Presidents Obama and Biden loosened some travel and remittance rules; President Trump reimposed tighter limits in 2017 and kept them through his first term.

On September 22 2026 at the United Nations General Assembly in York, President Trump told the gathered delegates that the Cuban regime will end up falling. As the words left the podium the Cuban delegation stood and walked out of the hall.

The departure left the Cuban seat empty for the remainder of the session. The United States continues to enforce the embargo under the same statutes that have governed it for sixty-six years, and Cuba retains its seat in the General Assembly.
