1a:T4ed,The Maritime Administration just dropped an interim final rule that rewrites the Title XI vessel and shipyard financing regs. It folds in the latest statutory changes, tightens up borrower requirements to match current federal credit rules, cuts out 14 obsolete sections, fixes old legal cites, and modernizes the language so people can actually read it. This is the latest step in a long-running program that has been updated piecemeal for years as Congress and OMB changed the underlying law and best practices.

Barring a court injunction or fresh legislation that overrides it, the revised rule takes effect next year and will govern all new Title XI applications going forward. Shipyards and vessel owners will face clearer but stricter financial tests; MARAD staff will operate under streamlined procedures. The next observable rung is the end of the public-comment window and any adjustments MARAD makes before the final rule locks in.

If Congress passes new maritime funding legislation or the administration changes the federal credit policy, the rule could get amended again within 12–24 months. Without those triggers it settles in as the standing playbook.
