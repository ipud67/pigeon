18:T603,Mahan Air flight IRM093 from Iran touched down in Guangzhou on September 23 at 4:39 pm local time. The US Treasury Secretary had warned days earlier that any country offering landing rights or fuel to Iranian planes would face exclusion from the dollar system, and he had set September 23 as the deadline for all Iranian airlines to shut down worldwide. Chinese airport records did not list the arrival and staff at Mahan’s Shanghai office reported no cancellation notices with tickets still on sale for the coming week. Iran continues direct flights into Beijing, Shanghai and Guangzhou while other routes to Baghdad, Muscat, Qatar, Georgia and Azerbaijan remain suspended.

This round of sanctions pressure has run six months since the naval blockade was first imposed in April. The pattern since the July 8 collapse of the 60-day Islamabad Memorandum shows Iran testing third-country airspace and ports while the US escalates financial threats without immediate enforcement. China has not altered its civil-aviation schedule with Iran.

The flight’s safe landing sets the next procedural step as whether Beijing supplies fuel for the return leg or clears further departures. If China keeps handling the planes without visible penalty, more Iranian carriers will route through Chinese hubs. The situation changes only if the US Treasury follows through on a concrete dollar-system cutoff aimed at a Chinese bank or airport operator.

Next move stays in the air lanes until a bank or airport loses dollar clearing access.
