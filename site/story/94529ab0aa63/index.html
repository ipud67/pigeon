The Civil Commission on October 7th Crimes by Hamas Against Women, Children and Families keeps working to record sexual violence from the 2023 attacks. Its head, attorney Merav Israeli-Amarant, said first responders faced chaotic scenes, fragmented evidence and no forensic records while they focused on saving lives. She called for guidelines that put responder safety first, plus training, resources and a broader look at brutality, humiliation and sexual torture beyond rape alone. Israeli-Amarant stated that establishing facts takes painstaking work and honesty about what stays unknown, which points to continued slow progress as new guidelines get developed and training rolls out. Next steps hinge on whether first-responder protocols gain traction and whether more resources arrive. Without those, the gaps in evidence will stay.
