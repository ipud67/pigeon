NASA telescope disintegrates in atmosphere after more than 20 years

Translated from Dutch · read the original →
The original headlineNASA-telescoop na ruim twintig jaar vergaan in atmosfeer

The Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory launched in 2004 to scan the sky for gamma-ray bursts and other high-energy events from space. A later attempt to raise its altitude and extend its life failed.

The observatory entered the atmosphere above the Indonesian island of Sumatra and broke apart.
