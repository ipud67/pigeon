The order sets a 15-day clock for the ministry to process the representations. This sits inside the broader pattern of Gulf states tightening ties with the US after Iran’s missile moves and the ongoing fight over the Strait of Hormuz that began with US and Israeli strikes in late February.

The ministry now has to review the cases, decide, and report back inside the deadline. Families can challenge any rejection in court. The next clear step is the ministry’s written reply by Sept 29; if it denies relief the petitions return to the high court for further hearings.

This runs as long as the two men remain detained and the families keep pressing through Pakistani channels. It moves next on the ministry’s decision or on any fresh diplomatic contact between Pakistan and the UAE.
