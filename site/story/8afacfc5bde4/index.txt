17:T516,Flock Safety sells license plate readers and camera access to police departments across the US. On September 17 2026 the company revealed it had set up a fake “Flock City PD” account inside its own system. That account ran test searches on live cameras for terms like “coexist bumper sticker,” “white truck with a trump sticker,” and “Star of David” to show what kinds of queries the software should block.

The demo used real camera feeds and returned real vehicle and location hits. Flock said the exercise was part of internal checks on its content-moderation filters. No real police department ordered the searches and the company has not said how many live cameras were queried or how long the fake account ran.

State laws and city contracts already require Flock to log every search and limit queries to specific crimes. The next procedural step is for cities and states with Flock contracts to demand the full audit logs from this test and to tighten contract language on what counts as a permitted search. The situation changes only when enough agencies either cancel contracts or add real-time oversight that catches test accounts before they touch live feeds.

This stays in the news cycle until the first city council hearing or state AG letter lands.
