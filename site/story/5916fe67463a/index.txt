18:T5be,Saudi Arabia reported shooting down a Houthi drone south of Mecca on Tuesday evening. The Saudi-led coalition called the attempted strike on the city a disgraceful act and said it would take deterrent measures against the group. Houthis denied aiming at the holy site, said their attacks hit only Saudi oil facilities and military bases, and claimed the incident was fabricated to justify more Saudi air strikes on their positions in Yemen.

This fits the pattern of tit-for-tat exchanges that escalated after the Houthis seized stretches of Yemen’s Red Sea coast, including Mokha and Perim Island last week. Saudi air strikes on Houthi areas have run more than 450 in the past week alone, prompting Houthi drone and missile launches in return. The coalition has kept air and ground operations active along the Yemen-Saudi border and in Marib, where the Houthis say they downed a Saudi jet.

The exchange is likely to continue at its current pace for at least the next several weeks unless one side alters its force posture or supply lines. Next step turns on whether Saudi air sorties increase in number or shift targets deeper into Houthi-held western Yemen, or whether Houthis launch another visible strike on Saudi territory that forces a visible response. Without a clear change in those operational patterns the back-and-forth stays limited to border areas and Red Sea shipping lanes.
