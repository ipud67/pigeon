Prime Minister Carney last travelled to Türkiye in July 2026 to attend the NATO Summit in Ankara. On the margins of the Summit, he met with President Erdoğan where the leaders announced the launch of negotiations toward a comprehensive Canada-Türkiye free trade agreement. Public consultations in Canada on this agreement concluded last month.
