Iran war has cost $45.1 billion, Pentagon tells Congress

The United States opened direct strikes against Iranian military targets in June 2026 after months of tit-for-tat attacks between Iranian-backed and American forces across Iraq and Syria that began in late 2023.

On Friday the Pentagon sent Congress a new cost report that put the price of the war at 45.1 billion dollars. The total combined 43.6 billion dollars spent through September 3 with another 1.5 billion dollars for extra fuel burned since then. The figure tops the Budget Office's earlier projection of 38 billion dollars through the first of August and the Pentagon's own July estimate of 37.5 billion dollars.

The new total does not count repairs to bases or buildings hit in the fighting and leaves out the indirect operating costs lawmakers had requested. The Congressional Budget Office told Congress the monthly bill has run between two and three billion dollars since early August and will keep that pace except during spikes in combat.
