25:T4a9,The Pentagon told Congress the US war with Iran has reached $45.1 billion. That breaks down to $43.6 billion through September 3 plus $1.5 billion more for extra fuel. The figure tops the Congressional Budget Office's earlier $38 billion projection through August 1 and the Pentagon's own $37.5 billion count from July. Both tallies leave out repairs to damaged bases and full indirect costs that lawmakers requested.

This running cost sits on the ladder of monthly Pentagon updates that have climbed from $37.5 billion in July to the current $45.1 billion in mid-September. The Congressional Budget Office laid out a pace of $2 billion to $3 billion per month after August 1, with jumps during heavier fighting.

The next procedural step is Congress receiving any detailed breakdowns it still demands and deciding on further funding requests. The war continues at the current pace until one side alters its force posture enough to force a shift in operations or a ceasefire. Without that change the monthly burn rate points to another $4 billion to $9 billion added by the end of the year.
