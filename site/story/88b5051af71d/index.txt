The pattern since the July 8 collapse of the Islamabad Memorandum shows US naval escorts and strikes have kept tanker traffic moving despite Iranian missile and mine threats. No new Iranian attempt to block the channel has succeeded in the last eight weeks. Seoul's possible deployment would add another non-Gulf flag to the escort rotation already run by the US and Gulf states.
