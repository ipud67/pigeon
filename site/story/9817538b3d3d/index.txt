The attack itself ended when the driver shot and killed himself inside the truck after striking a security officer. No one else died. The FBI identified the attacker as Ayman Mohamad Ghazali, a 41-year-old naturalized citizen born in Lebanon, and said the truck carried improvised explosives.

This return is likely to hold for the foreseeable future barring fresh violence at the site. Next indicator to watch is whether attendance and donations stay steady through the fall holidays.
