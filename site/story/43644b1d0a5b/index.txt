Polls showing Paxton behind in a state that has not elected a Democrat to this seat since 1948 remain the key measure of whether the cash closes the gap.

This push will run through the final stretch to Election Day. Next measurable steps are tracking whether ad saturation shifts poll numbers or fundraising reports in the coming weeks, and whether more holdout Republicans like Cornyn add public endorsements.
