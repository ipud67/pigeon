A mother stabbed her 5-year-old child to death and wounded two others inside a New York apartment on September 21. Police took her into custody at the scene. This marks the latest rung in a 48-hour stretch that Deputy Commissioner Peter McKenna called the department's most tragic period in recent memory. The case now sits with the Manhattan district attorney's office for charging. Detectives have started the standard review of family records, prior calls to the address, and witness statements. The two wounded children remain in hospital care; their release or further treatment will mark the next visible step. Prosecutors must present evidence to a grand jury within days or seek an extension. A change would require either a psychiatric evaluation altering her fitness to stand trial or a plea deal before indictment. Without those, the matter heads to arraignment and pretrial hearings on the homicide and assault counts. The situation stays in the criminal court pipeline until a verdict or settlement is recorded. Horizon: months in the court system.
