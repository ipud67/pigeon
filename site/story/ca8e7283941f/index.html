PUNCH correspondent in Akwa Ibom dies

Bassey Akra, chairman of the Correspondents’ Chapel of the Akwa Ibom State Council of the Nigeria Union of Journalists, confirmed the death of Mr Odey to Premium Times on Wednesday.

Odey worked as a correspondent for The PUNCH newspaper in Akwa Ibom.

That is all that is known from the verified record.

Thinking ahead
The death of the Punch correspondent in Akwa Ibom was confirmed Wednesday by Bassey Akra, chairman of the Correspondents’ Chapel of the state NUJ council. This marks the latest loss in a string of journalist deaths in Nigeria that have drawn union attention over the years. Next step is the standard NUJ mourning process: chapel meetings, funeral arrangements, and a formal statement from the national body. How much longer this runs depends on whether the union treats it as routine turnover or pushes for an official probe into cause of death. If no health details or foul-play claims surface in the next week, coverage fades to obituary notices. If the chapel files a report or the family disputes the circumstances, it could stretch into state government correspondence and a coroner’s review. Observable indicators to watch: any NUJ press release naming a cause, family statements, or state information ministry comment. Thin indicators right now — no public details on age, illness, or accident. Horizon: 10 to 14 days until the story drops unless new facts emerge.
