Nepal army rescues survivors from hydropower tunnel

On August 28, 2026, Nepal Army teams pulled survivors out of a hydropower tunnel in Rasuwa where more than 100 workers had been trapped. The tunnel collapse cut off the only exit and blocked rescue access for days. Army engineers drilled through debris and used heavy equipment to reach those inside. The operation continues as crews search for anyone still unaccounted for.
