Richard Schroyer of Oklahoma is listed for assistant secretary at Homeland Security, Heidi Semann of Missouri for inspector general at Education, and Robert Stebbins of New York for deputy chief executive at the International Development Finance Corporation.

These names now sit with the Senate committees that handle each agency. The committees schedule hearings, take votes, and send recommendations to the full floor. Confirmation needs a simple majority unless a hold or filibuster blocks it.

The list is the latest batch in the regular nomination pipeline that runs through every administration. No floor action has been scheduled yet.

This process will run until each nominee is confirmed, returned, or withdrawn.
