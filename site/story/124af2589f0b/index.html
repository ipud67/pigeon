State Attorney General secures S/ 1 million in civil damages in case against Rodolfo Orellana

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineProcuraduría General logra S/ 1 millón de reparación civil en caso contra Rodolfo Orellana

The State's legal defense, through the Public Prosecutor's Office Specialized in Money Laundering Crimes, obtained a favorable result in the criminal proceedings against Rodolfo Orellana Rengifo and others for money laundering, after the set one million soles in civil reparation in favor of the State as for the damage caused by the crime proven in the Parinacochas case.
