The executive order issued September 4, 2026, aims to let American ranchers butcher, process, package, and sell meat across state lines under high safety standards while expanding market access and addressing beef supply shortfalls. This touches enumerated federal powers over interstate commerce and foreign imports, yet it also revives the founding generation’s direct experience with trade barriers.
