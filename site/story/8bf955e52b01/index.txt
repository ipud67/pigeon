19:T40b,The Coast Guard just put a temporary safety zone on part of Lake St. Clair off Grosse Pointe Farms for a fireworks show set for September 2 2026. No boats or people can go in without the Captain of the Port Detroit saying okay. This is the standard move they make every year for over-water fireworks to keep everyone clear of falling shells and smoke.

The zone will stay in place only for the length of the display and the cleanup afterward, usually a few hours on the night of the show. Once the fireworks are done and the Captain of the Port calls it off, the restriction lifts automatically. Nothing points to it stretching past that night unless the event gets postponed by weather or the sponsor changes the date.

Next step is the usual notice to mariners and local announcements so boaters know the exact times and boundaries before Labor Day weekend. If the sponsor cancels or moves the show, the Coast Guard will cancel the rule early. No signs of that right now.
