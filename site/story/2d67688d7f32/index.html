UEFA, CONCACAF demand FIFA make $2.1 billion payout to member groups amid funding row

FIFA has run world football since it formed in 1904 with seven national groups and now counts 211 member associations. Each member pays annual dues and receives development grants from the governing body every four-year cycle between World Cups. The organization built reserves that stood above $4 billion after the 2022 tournament in Qatar.

On September 18 UEFA and CONCACAF wrote to President Gianni Infantino and asked him to release $2.1 billion from those reserves so every member association could get an extra $10 million for the 2027-2030 cycle. The letter arrived while Infantino was still recovering from the collapse of his plan to sell a stake in the 2026 and 2030 World Cups to private investors after European clubs and federations opposed the move.

The demand now sits inside the next budget talks for the 2027-2030 cycle and turns on how much of the existing reserves FIFA will distribute directly to the 211 members rather than hold or direct through its own programs.

Thinking ahead
UEFA and CONCACAF have pressed FIFA to pay out $2.1 billion from its reserves so each of the 211 member groups gets $10 million across the 2027-30 cycle. The push follows the collapse of Infantino's plan to sell a World Cup stake to private investors after European pushback. FIFA has held large reserves for years and the two confederations now want that cash moved to the national associations. The demand sits inside FIFA's regular four-year budget cycle that sets funding for members. Next step is a formal vote or agreement at the FIFA Council or Congress where the 211 associations hold the seats. The payout happens only if enough members back it or if Infantino shifts position to avoid a floor fight. The row stays alive until the budget is locked or a compromise splits the difference. Without a Council vote or public concession the pressure from the two big confederations keeps the reserves issue on the table. This runs at least until the next FIFA Congress in 2027.
