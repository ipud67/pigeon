Moldova closed six Ukraine border crossings in Ocnița, Otaci, Unguri, Briceni, Grimăncăuți and Clocușna over security concerns after a Russian drone incursion

The closures affect crossings in Ocnița, Otaci, Unguri, Briceni, Grimăncăuți and Clocușna. Moldova cited security concerns following the drone incursion.
