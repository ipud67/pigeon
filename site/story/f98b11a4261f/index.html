IDF: 170 Gazans presented as journalists were terror operatives

The Israeli military says 118 served in Hamas’s military wing and 49 in Palestinian Islamic Jihad, while 17 participated in the Oct. 7 massacre.

Thinking ahead
The IDF released names, photos and internal records on 170 men killed in Gaza since October 2023 who had been listed as journalists or media workers. Of those, 118 belonged to Hamas’s military wing, 49 to Palestinian Islamic Jihad, two to the Popular Resistance Committees and one to the Mujahideen Brigades. Seventeen took part in the October 7 attack. The list includes men who worked for Reuters, CNN, the BBC, AFP, AP, Le Monde and ABC Australia. The release follows a pattern the IDF has shown before with hospital staff and ambulance crews, using captured documents, open-source material and its own intelligence to match public job titles to combat roles. This disclosure is the latest step in an information campaign that runs parallel to the ground fight. It adds one more public data point to the running dispute over who counts as a protected civilian in Gaza. No new strikes or ground movements are tied to the release itself. The pattern of these releases has continued at irregular intervals for nearly three years whenever the IDF gathers enough captured records to support a batch. It is likely to keep appearing as long as the war lasts and the IDF holds territory that yields more Hamas and Islamic Jihad paperwork. Next step is further batches or updates to the website when additional evidence surfaces from current operations. Horizon: months, until one side loses the ability to hold ground and produce records.
