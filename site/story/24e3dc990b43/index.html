Search intensified for killer of Claudia Tacoronte; university students interrupt parade

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineIntensifican búsqueda de asesino de Claudia Tacoronte; universitarias irrumpen en desfile

Police in Morelos have hunted a suspect in the killing of a young Spanish woman since the body was found last week. The state prosecutor's office named Francisco Javier “N” as the alleged killer of Claudia Tacoronte. Governor Margarita González asked residents to report any sighting of him.

University students broke into the independence parade in Cuernavaca on Wednesday morning and blocked the route for several minutes. They carried signs demanding faster action on the case and an end to violence against women.

The search now turns on tips from the public after the governor's appeal, while the students' disruption has drawn official attention back to the murder investigation.
