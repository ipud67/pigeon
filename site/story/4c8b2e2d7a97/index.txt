21:T487,Russian Molniya drones hit two apartment buildings in Sumy on 27 September, one on a roof in the Kovpakivskyi district and one on the wall of a 10-storey block in the Zarichnyi district that had been damaged before. A woman was injured in the first strike. The same day a separate guided aerial bomb attack on the city killed two people, one of them a 16-year-old girl, and wounded six others, according to the head of the Sumy City Military Administration.

This fits a pattern of repeated Russian strikes on the border city using small drones and bombs aimed at urban blocks. Ukrainian air defence has downed some incoming drones in prior weeks but not all. Local officials report damage and casualties after each wave, then crews patch roofs and walls before the next round.

The pace is likely to hold steady as long as Russian forces keep launching from nearby territory and Ukrainian intercept rates stay below 100 percent. Next strikes will probably follow the same mix of drone and bomb attacks on civilian blocks unless one side shifts air-defence numbers or launch sites enough to change the hit rate.
