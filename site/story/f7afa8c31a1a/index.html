MICHIGAN
November 1, 2026 · 19:48 UTC

US Rep Haley Stevens backed Republican Mike Rogers for US Senate in Michigan

U.S. Rep. Haley Stevens entered the Democratic primary for an open Michigan seat last year and lost to state Sen. Abdul El-Sayed by a narrow . After the primary she said she would vote for El-Sayed in the general election but would not campaign for him.

Stevens withdrew her support for El-Sayed and endorsed Republican former Rep. Mike Rogers instead. She recorded a video statement from her car in which she said she does not belong in El-Sayed’s campaign and does not stand with Piker. Several of her staff members, including longtime chief of staff Justin German, resigned in protest over the switch.
