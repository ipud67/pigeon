Lahore High Court asked Punjab home secretary for reply on plea against Aleema Khan detention on September 20

LAHORE: The Lahore High Court (LHC) on Friday sought a reply from the Punjab home secretary on a petition challenging the of PTI founder Imran Khan’s sister, Aleema Khan, under the Maintenance of Public Order (MPO) . Aleema was detained by authorities on Sept 20, after the office of Lahore’s (DC) ordered her detention or arrest for 30 days. The DC’s order cited a letter by the Lahore capital city police officer, which referred to police reports that Aleema and Ameer “are prejudicial to the public safety and maintenance of public order”.
