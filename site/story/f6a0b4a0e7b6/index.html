Early voting stations in Helsingborg may have been affected by error in advance voting

Translated from Swedish · read the original →
The original headlineVallokaler i Helsingborg kan ha drabbats av fel i förtidsröstningen

Sweden holds national elections on a fixed schedule set by law. Voters can cast ballots at polling stations on election day or at early voting stations during the weeks before. The Election Authority oversees the process and issues instructions to local election offices on how to handle ballots and verify voter eligibility.

An error at several early voting stations in Helsingborg and one in Ängelholm allowed some advance ballots to be processed incorrectly on or before September 11 2026. The Election Authority identified the mistake and notified the public the same day.

The authority now directs any voter who used one of the affected stations to cast a new ballot at their assigned polling station on election day. Those ballots will replace the earlier ones.
