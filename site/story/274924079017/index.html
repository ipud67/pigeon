Loan Performance Categories and Financial Reporting

“Formally restructured loans (TDR),”
Overview
Rule. The Farm Credit Administration (FCA, we, or our) amends our high-risk loan performance categories by removing "Formally restructured loans (TDR)," also known as troubled debt restructurings. In 2022, changes in generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) eliminated the accounting guidance for TDRs, enhanced disclosure requirements for certain loan refinancings and restructurings undertaken when a borrower is experiencing financial difficulty and changed existing vintage year disclosure requirements for public business entities. This final rule removes TDRs from our regulatory loan performance categories to reflect changes in GAAP. Because FCA regulations require Farm Credit System (System) institutions to prepare financial statements and reports in accordance with GAAP, retaining TDRs as a regulatory loan performance category is no longer consistent with current accounting standards. In addition to making conforming technical changes, the rule also makes minor technical and organizational revisions to ensure internal consistency within the regulation. In addition, FCA determined that no regulatory are necessary to implement GAAP's enhanced disclosure requirements for loan modifications to borrowers experiencing financial difficulty or for amended vintage year disclosures, as existing FCA regulations already require GAAP-compliant financial reporting. (Farm Credit Administration)
Short history
The Farm Credit Administration requires Farm Credit System institutions to prepare all financial statements and reports under GAAP. In 2022, GAAP dropped the accounting category for troubled debt restructurings (TDRs), replaced it with new disclosure rules for loan modifications to borrowers in financial difficulty, and changed vintage-year disclosure requirements. On August 26, 2026, the FCA issued a final rule that removes “formally restructured loans (TDR)” from its regulatory high-risk loan performance categories. The rule also makes minor technical and organizational fixes to keep the rest of the regulation consistent. FCA left its existing GAAP-compliance rules in place, so institutions must follow the new GAAP disclosure requirements without any additional FCA amendments.
Predictive model
The Farm Credit Administration is dropping “Formally restructured loans (TDR)” from its high-risk loan performance categories because GAAP stopped using that label in 2022. System banks already file their books under GAAP, so the old regulatory bucket no longer lines up with the numbers they report. The final rule, set for August 26 2026, also cleans up wording and confirms that existing GAAP disclosure rules already cover the new “modifications to borrowers in financial difficulty” data points. This change has been locked in since the accounting shift four years ago; the rule simply removes the mismatch. No further FCA action is scheduled once the rule takes effect. The only thing that would reopen the category is a fresh GAAP reversal or a deliberate policy shift by the FCA board. The rule will take effect on schedule and stay in place unless GAAP changes again.
