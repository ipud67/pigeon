1d:T4e1,The SEC issued an order on September 15 2026 that gives clearing agencies, exchanges, broker-dealers and security-based swap entities a pass on tagging certain regulatory filings in Inline XBRL. The forms covered are portions of Form CA-1 and Form 1 plus the full Form X-17A-5 Part III, Form 17-H and the chief compliance officer annual report. This relief sits on top of the December 2024 rule that first set the Inline XBRL mandate for those same documents.

The order rests on the commission’s finding that the machine-readable format adds little value for these internal supervisory filings that staff review directly. No compliance deadline is altered and no new filing platform is required. Registrants can continue to submit the forms in their current unstructured format until the exemption is lifted or narrowed.

The relief stays in place until the SEC takes further action. It ends only if the commission issues a new order or amends the underlying rule after another rulemaking. Observable indicator of change would be publication of a proposal to revisit the exemption or a fresh adopting release that restores the tagging requirement.
