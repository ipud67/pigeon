C64 maintains its march in Kananga despite suspension of political demonstrations

Translated from French · read the original →
The original headlineLa C64 maintient sa marche à Kananga en dépit de la suspension des manifestations politiques

Political demonstrations in Kananga have been banned since the urban authority issued the order on security grounds. The measure halts all such gatherings in the capital of Kasaï-Central until further notice. The C64/Kasaï-Central opposition has operated inside this restriction since it took effect.

The coalition denounced the ban as on September 13. It declared it would proceed with the march set for Tuesday, September 15, to oppose any alteration of the Constitution.

The scheduled march now stands against the suspension order. The coalition continues to organize while the urban authority enforces the halt.
