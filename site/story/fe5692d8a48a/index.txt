1a:T44d,The DRC government and the AFC/M23 rebels just wrapped five days of talks in Geneva and put out a joint roadmap for formal peace negotiations. This sits on top of years of on-and-off fighting in eastern Congo where M23, backed by Rwanda, has taken ground and displaced hundreds of thousands. The roadmap itself is only a schedule for future talks, not a ceasefire or troop pullback.

How much longer this goes depends on whether both sides actually sit down for the next round and whether Rwanda keeps supplying the rebels. Observable indicators will be whether a date and venue for the first substantive negotiation is announced in the coming weeks, and whether ground clashes drop or spike in the eastern provinces. If one side walks away or the roadmap gets ignored, the fighting simply continues at current levels.

Right now the process has momentum from the U.S.-facilitated statement but no enforcement mechanism is visible. Next visible rung is the first scheduled negotiation session or a public breakdown.
