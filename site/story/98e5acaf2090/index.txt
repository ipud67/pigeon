1a:T50d,NIST handed out more than $1.7 million in cooperative agreements on September 18 2026 to nine groups in eight states under the RAMPS program run by the NICE office at NIST. The money funds local projects that mix classroom work with internships apprenticeships hands-on tasks workshops boot camps competitions and hackathons all built around partnerships between schools industry and community groups. This brings the running total to 55 RAMPS sites spread across 26 states since the program started.

The awards follow the same pattern set in earlier rounds where each grant runs up to $200 000 and lasts long enough to stand up new training pipelines that match job openings reported by nearby employers. Recipients in Florida Kentucky Virginia New Mexico Michigan and Ohio will now move from planning to delivery of the first cohorts of students and workers.

The effort keeps expanding at roughly six to eight new communities per award cycle as long as Congress funds the NICE budget at current levels. Next visible step is the public rollout of each site’s curriculum and first class schedule over the coming months. If federal spending on workforce grants stays flat or drops the pace of new sites will slow.
