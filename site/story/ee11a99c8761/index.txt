Damage hit houses, shops, cars, power lines, a cultural center, a school, and rail lines in districts from Kupiansk to Lozova to Chuhuiv. This fits the pattern of regular cross-border strikes that have run since the front lines stabilized near Kupiansk in late 2022.

The rate of these daily shelling reports has held steady for the last 18 months with no drop tied to weather or supply shifts. Ukrainian air defense intercepts some of the drones but has not blocked the volume that reaches civilian zones. Russian launch crews keep operating from inside their own border and from rear areas out of effective Ukrainian artillery range.

Without a major shift in ground control near the border or a sustained drop in Russian drone and missile stocks, the pattern continues at the same pace. Next reports will likely list similar casualty and damage counts unless Ukrainian forces push the firing positions farther back.
