17:T4e8,Filling stations in Abuja raised retail petrol prices this week after Dangote Refinery lifted its gantry price from N1,265 to N1,350 per litre. MRS outlets moved from N1,350 to N1,395, NIPCO from N1,350 to N1,430, and Mobil from N1,350 to N1,400. The step follows a global crude price climb that pushed the refinery’s wholesale above the prior landing cost of N1,311.

This is the latest rung in Nigeria’s post-subsidy ladder where refinery and landing costs set the floor for pump prices. Retailers have passed on each upstream jump within days. Economists and union figures quoted in the reporting flagged faster pass-through to transport and food costs.

No U.S. Treasury figures supplied bear on Nigerian fuel margins, so none are used here. The gap in local inflation or naira data leaves the speed of the next round unclear.

The price ladder has moved every time Dangote or landing costs rose in the past year. Expect another retail adjustment if the gantry price stays above N1,350 or crude holds the recent gain. Retail stations will likely post the new numbers within one to three days of fresh stock arrival.

Next rung depends on the next gantry posting and any regulatory hold.
