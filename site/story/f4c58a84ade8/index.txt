17:T428,Russian forces reported ten Ukrainian drone raids on the Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant overnight into September 13. The plant director said the strikes aimed at the staff training center and that radiation levels stayed normal. This fits the pattern of repeated strikes on the site that have gone on since Russian troops took it in March 2022.

The raids show Ukrainian forces keep pressing attacks on the facility even after earlier waves drew warnings from the IAEA. Russian statements point to continued drone launches from Ukrainian-held territory across the Dnipro. No change in control of the plant or in radiation readings has been recorded.

How much longer this goes depends on whether Ukrainian drone units keep getting through Russian air defenses at the site. Next step could be another Russian claim of downed drones or a fresh IAEA call for demilitarization. The situation shifts only if one side seizes the ground around the plant or both agree to pull heavy weapons back.
