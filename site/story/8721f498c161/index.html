Court grants Atiku’s motion to serve Tinubu documents through APC

The Federal High Court in Abuja has handled election-related suits since the 2023 presidential vote. Atiku Abubakar ran as the Peoples Democratic Party candidate that year and lost to Bola Tinubu of the All Progressives Congress. Tinubu took office in May 2023. Atiku later joined the African Democratic Congress and filed papers to run again in 2027.

On September 16 the court granted Atiku’s ex-parte motion for substituted service. His lawyer told Justice Inyang Ekwo that personal service on Tinubu had failed. The judge ordered the originating summons and other documents served on the All Progressives Congress instead. The suit names Tinubu as first , the APC as second, and the Independent National Commission as third. It asks the court to disqualify Tinubu and the APC from the 2027 ballot on the ground that the APC gave INEC a forged NYSC discharge certificate for Tinubu.

The plaintiffs now have seven days to complete service. The case returns to court on September 28 for mention. Tinubu, the APC and INEC have already filed counter-affidavits and preliminary objections that say the suit is incompetent and that the plaintiffs lack standing.
