The Portfolio Committee chairperson, Ian Cameron, came out today and slammed any plan to open an internal investigation against those officers. This sits on top of years of documented vehicle shortages in South African police stations that have forced cops to improvise with private cars, private fuel, and private repairs.

The next visible rung is whether the provincial police commissioner actually opens that probe or shelves it. If the committee keeps the pressure on and no charges are filed, the story fades inside two weeks. If the investigation starts and names officers, it will pull in union statements, more parliamentary hearings, and fresh reports on fleet shortages within a month. The situation only shifts if the SAPS budget allocates new fleet or repairs enough vans to put the personal-car practice to an end.

Observable indicators remain thin beyond the committee statement itself. No confirmation yet that an investigation file has been opened.
