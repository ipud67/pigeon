19:T439,Deloitte Netherlands cut its headcount by 900 over the past two years and now stands at 7,400 employees. The drop hit hardest among staff under thirty. The annual figures released Friday mark the latest step in a contraction that began after the post-pandemic hiring surge.

Big-four firms added staff fast when client demand ran high in 2021-2022. That wave reversed once audit and consulting budgets tightened. Fewer young hires signal the firm is letting natural turnover reduce numbers rather than replace everyone who leaves.

The pattern lines up with steady but not booming economic signals. U.S. 10-year Treasury yield sits at 5.01 percent and the 2s10s spread holds at 25 basis points, both consistent with moderate growth that does not yet push companies to expand outside staff.

Next moves will show in the next annual headcount and in graduate recruitment numbers for 2027. If the under-thirty cohort keeps shrinking, the firm will rely more on laterals and automation for routine work.

Three to six months more of flat or declining staff likely.
