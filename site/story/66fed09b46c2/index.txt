17:T593,The Constitution gives Congress the power to provide for the common defense, raise and support armies, and maintain a navy, while the president serves as commander in chief. The founding generation expected the federal government to meet its treaty and alliance obligations without excuse, as Washington did when he called out the militia to suppress the Whiskey Rebellion in 1794 and Congress passed the Militia Acts of 1792 to organize state forces for national needs. Lithuania bought the HIMARS systems under a 2022 contract now delayed by American industry, with crews already trained and temporary identical launchers available from the Michigan National Guard until the eight ordered systems arrive.

The Preamble lists common defense and domestic tranquility as core reasons for forming the Union. A government that cannot deliver weapons it has sold to a treaty partner weakens the security arrangements the founders designed to protect American liberty and peace. The Federalist Papers warned that weakness invites danger; Hamilton in Federalist 25 called for a Union strong enough to deter threats before they reach our shores.

Congress holds the purse and the power to regulate the armed forces, so lawmakers bear responsibility to see that defense production meets the contracts the executive signs. The delay does not change the founding duty to keep faith with allies who help guard the eastern flank of Europe.18:T4fd,The US has fallen behind on the 2022 contract for eight HIMARS launchers that Lithuania bought for 495 million dollars, with a second battery added this April for another 280 million. Lithuanian military chief Raimundas Vaikšnoras said Thursday that crews finished training but the systems are held up on the American production side and the original end-of-2026 target will slip. He pointed to a standby deal with the Michigan National Guard to bring identical launchers over for exercises and to cover any gap until the ordered units show up. Defense Minister Robert Kaunas added that some division infrastructure may also run late but both men said the 2030 full-operational target for the national division stays on track because training and risk management are in place. The next visible rung comes when US industry reports catch-up production numbers or when Lithuanian officials name a revised delivery window.

This delay will probably run another six to twelve months before the first batch arrives, unless US factories accelerate output or Lithuania triggers the temporary loan clause. The situation changes only when the US delivers the first systems or both sides amend the contract again with new dates.
