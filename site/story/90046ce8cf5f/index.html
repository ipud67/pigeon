A Minneapolis father and son pleaded guilty in October to a $9 million fraud conspiracy against the United States

A federal law bars people with certain prior from possessing any firearm or ammunition at any time. Between June 2022 and October 2024 LaMar Javis Burgess and his son LaMar Dazjar Burgess prepared and filed at least 300 false federal income tax returns for themselves and others. The returns claimed refundable sick and family leave credits for self-employed people affected by COVID-19 and other credits the taxpayers were not entitled to receive. The scheme produced requests for at least 9.2 million dollars in refunds.

In October both men pleaded guilty in Minneapolis federal court to to the United States. The father also pleaded guilty to possessing a firearm after his felony conviction. Investigators executing a search warrant at his home in Golden Valley found a loaded Glock 19 9 mm handgun that had been reported stolen, along with extra magazines and loose ammunition.

Both defendants remain free until their sentencings. A federal district judge will later set prison terms, periods of supervised release, restitution amounts and monetary penalties after applying the U.S. Sentencing Guidelines and other statutory factors.
