1a:T574,The FCC put out a proposed rule on August 31 2026 asking for public comment on four pieces of how the Universal Service Administrative Company runs the Universal Service Fund: day-to-day processes, USAC’s overall role, its operating costs, and how its board affects things. This is the latest step in a long-running effort to tighten oversight of the fund that has been managed by USAC under FCC direction for decades. Next concrete rung is the end of the comment period, followed by FCC review of whatever replies and reply comments come in.

How much longer this stays alive depends on how many serious replies the FCC gets and whether they flag problems that need fixing. If the comments are light or mostly supportive, the docket could close with only minor tweaks and no big structural change. If carriers, states, or consumer groups lay out clear operational failures or cost overruns with data, the FCC can move to a further notice or an order that alters USAC’s charter, reporting rules, or board makeup. The only observable trigger that would kill momentum is if the Commission simply shelves the docket after review.

Right now the ball is in the public’s court to file comments. Without a heavy response the process likely winds down inside twelve months. If the record builds, expect a follow-on order in 2027 or 2028.
