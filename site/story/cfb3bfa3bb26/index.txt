19:T459,NASA plans to undock the SpaceX Crew-12 capsule from the space station no earlier than 8:05 a.m. Eastern on October 7. That sets a splashdown off California for roughly 11:34 a.m. Eastern on October 8 if weather and spacecraft checks hold. The four crew members — NASA’s Jessica Meir and Jack Hathaway, ESA’s Sophie Adenot, and Roscosmos’ Andrey Fedyaev — have been aboard since their launch earlier this year as the latest rotation on the station.

Live NASA coverage begins with the undock and continues through reentry and recovery, including an audio-only loop once the capsule moves away from the station. Managers are watching Pacific recovery-area weather, Dragon readiness, and team prep as the main variables. A scrub would push the timeline by at least one day while crews recheck conditions.

This return marks the scheduled end of the Crew-12 increment after months of joint operations on orbit. Next step is the post-splashdown medical checks and capsule safing once the recovery ship reaches the crew.
