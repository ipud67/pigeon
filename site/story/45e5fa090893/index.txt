The Constitution grants Congress the power to regulate commerce with foreign nations and to make rules for the common defense. The first Congress exercised that authority immediately by passing the Tariff Act of 1789 and by creating the Department of State to conduct foreign negotiations under Senate advice and consent where treaties are concerned. A 123 agreement is not a treaty; it rests on statutory authority Congress itself wrote in the Atomic Energy Act. Congress therefore retains the right to review and reject such deals when they weaken the barriers against the spread of nuclear weapons technology.

The founders placed the protection of life and the security of the people first. The Declaration lists life as the first unalienable right, and the Preamble names providing for the common defense and securing the blessings of liberty as core reasons for forming the government. A Middle East nuclear arms race would put millions of civilians at risk of sudden mass death. Congress has the duty to weigh that danger before any executive agreement proceeds.

The pact omits the standard requirements that Saudi Arabia forgo uranium enrichment and reprocessing and accept the additional protocol for IAEA inspections, steps that were part of the earlier U.S.-UAE deal. A joint resolution needs House and Senate passage to block the deal, but Speaker Johnson recessed the House early and the measure would not reach the floor until after the midterms. Even if both chambers approve it, the president can veto.

The resolution sits with the Foreign Affairs Committee where ranking member Meeks serves. A parallel bipartisan Senate letter Wednesday demanded the side letters be declassified, citing unbroken past practice of releasing them with the main text. No floor action is scheduled before the election recess ends.

The disapproval process runs on the congressional calendar and veto math. It continues at least until the post-midterm session convenes unless the administration withdraws the pact or supplies the side letters in a way that shifts enough votes to drop the resolution. Next step is committee markup or direct floor scheduling once the House returns.
