Mike Pence says he has ‘no plans’ to run for president, other offices in 2028

Mike Pence ran for the Republican presidential nomination in 2024 and dropped out before the Iowa caucuses. He had served one term as vice president under Donald Trump from 2017 to 2021, then left office after the two men broke over the 2020 election results and the January 6 Capitol attack. Pence kept a low national profile for the next several years while he wrote a book, gave paid speeches, and spoke at conservative gatherings.

On September 17, 2026, Pence appeared at an event held by the Economic Club of Washington. When club chair David Rubenstein asked him directly whether he would consider another run for president, Pence answered that he has no plans to run for president or any other elected office in 2028.

The statement closes off one of the last open questions about Pence’s future in politics and leaves the 2028 Republican field without a former vice president who once stood at the top of the party’s ticket.
