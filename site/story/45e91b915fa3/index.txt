Balakliia changed hands twice in 2022, first taken by Russian troops then retaken by Ukrainian forces in the September counter-offensive. Since then it has stayed behind the front line but close enough for regular attacks.

The rate of such strikes has held steady through summer 2026 with no sign of let-up in the Kharkiv sector. Next moves will depend on whether Russian units keep the same volume of fire or shift munitions to other axes. The situation stays the same until one side alters force posture or supply lines along that part of the front.
