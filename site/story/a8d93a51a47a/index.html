Illegal immigrant rideshare driver charged with rape in Maryland had prior NYC sex abuse arrest: DHS

Abdoulaye Dia worked as a rideshare driver for the Empower app in the Washington area. Federal immigration records show he entered the United States without legal permission years earlier and remained in the country. In New York he faced an earlier arrest on a sex-abuse charge that did not result in conviction.

On September 18, 2026, Montgomery County police in Silver Spring charged Dia with first-degree rape after a teenage female passenger told officers he assaulted her during a ride. Detectives linked him to the Empower trip through app data and matched his description to the suspect.

The new charge placed Dia in Montgomery County Detention Center pending a court appearance. Immigration and Customs Enforcement placed a detainer on him, starting the federal process that determines whether he stays in local custody or moves into removal proceedings.
