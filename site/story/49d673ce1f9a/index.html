HAVANA
September 5, 2026 · 17:56 UTC

La Policía interviene con una decena de patrullas para disolver una protesta en el barrio habanero de Fontanar

Police moved in with about ten patrol cars on September 5, 2026, and broke up a protest in Havana’s Fontanar neighborhood. Residents had gathered to denounce electricity blackouts that lasted more than 20 hours while some homes with swimming pools kept power without interruption. The protest followed the looting of a truck carrying cooking oil, which marked a rise in social tensions across Cuba.
