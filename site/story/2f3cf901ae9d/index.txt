1a:T53e,Russia took control of Nestle assets along with those of three French companies and handed them to a state-linked firm called LEV Management under a decree signed by President Putin on September 18. This follows the pattern set after the 2022 invasion of Ukraine when most Western firms sold their Russian holdings fast and the government tightened rules on any exit. The move adds to earlier seizures of foreign-owned factories, brands, and bank stakes that Moscow has transferred to local operators since 2022.

The decree names specific assets but does not list their exact value or full scope in public records. LEV Management now holds operational control. Reversal would require either a negotiated settlement between Russia and the companies or a court order that lifts the transfer, both of which have not occurred in prior similar cases.

The situation is likely to continue at least through the next several months while the new operator integrates the sites and supply lines. Next procedural step is registration of the ownership change in Russian business registries followed by any production or sales adjustments at the affected plants. Change would need a formal agreement or higher-level decree that returns title, indicators still absent.
