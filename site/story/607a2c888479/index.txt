On September 20 2026, 11 of those suspects were put on a plane and sent to Miami to stand trial in US federal court on charges that include murder, conspiracy, and weapons violations tied to the raid on Moïse’s home.

US prosecutors have already convicted two other men in the same plot. The new group faces the same courtroom track, which moves through pretrial hearings, evidence fights over statements taken in Haiti, and then jury selection. Haitian authorities still hold a handful of high-profile names at home, and their extradition would need fresh paperwork and political approval from whoever runs the next Haitian government.

The case stays alive as long as witnesses keep showing up and no major defendant cuts a cooperation deal that collapses the rest. If key Haitian figures remain unextradited or if local gangs block further arrests, the US trial becomes the main public record of the killing.
