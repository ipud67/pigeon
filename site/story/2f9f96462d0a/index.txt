This pattern of strikes on rear-area civilian targets in central Ukraine has run at similar scale for most of the past year. The next strikes are likely to follow the same daily or near-daily rhythm unless Russian drone and artillery stocks drop sharply or Ukrainian intercept numbers rise enough to force a change in targeting. The procedural step remains local officials tallying hits and sending casualty counts to Kyiv.
