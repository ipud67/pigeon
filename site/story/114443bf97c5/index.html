Trump begins two-day Ireland visit, security operation costs €20 million

US presidents have visited Ireland regularly since the 1990s. The trips usually combine talks with government leaders in Dublin and stops at personal or ancestral sites around the country. Donald Trump first came as president in 2018 and again in 2019, each time splitting time between official meetings and his golf resort at Doonbeg in County Clare.

On September 12 2026 Trump landed in Dublin at the start of a two-day stay. The Irish government mounted a large security operation for the visit that officials put at a cost of €20 million. Trump planned to meet Irish leaders in the capital before heading west to the Clare resort.

The operation locked down parts of Dublin and Clare for the duration. Local opinions on the visit and its price tag stayed split, with some welcoming the economic lift and others questioning the expense. The schedule now turns on the balance between the closed-door talks and the time set aside at Doonbeg.
