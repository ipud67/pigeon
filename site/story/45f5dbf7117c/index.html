Judge orders US agriculture secretary to stop sending religious emails from official account

On November 19, 2026, US District Judge Trina Thompson ordered Agriculture Secretary Brooke Rollins to stop sending religious messages from her official email account. The ruling followed a lawsuit by employees and religious freedom groups who said the secretary's repeated use of "we," "us," and "our" when Christian holidays created the appearance that the agency expected the same beliefs from its staff.

The order requires the secretary to draw a line between her personal and her official one. It leaves her free to speak as she chooses outside government channels while binding the official account.
