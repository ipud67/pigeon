1b:T41b,The Committee for the Implementation of Textile Agreements published a new annual cap on duty-free U.S. imports of apparel assembled in sub-Saharan beneficiary countries using fabric from the region or from third countries. This cap sits at 7 percent of total U.S. apparel imports over the prior 12 months, with a 3.5 percent sub-cap for goods from lesser-developed countries. The limit stems directly from Section 112(b)(3) of the Trade and Development Act of 2000 as amended through Public Law 119-103, which keeps the preference in force until the end of 2028. 

The notice sets the enforceable ceiling that customs will apply starting with the new trade year. Once the cap is reached, further qualifying shipments face normal tariffs. No immediate change in the law or preference schedule is scheduled. The cap will reset at the next 12-month look-back unless Congress alters the percentages or the program expires.

The arrangement continues at current scale until at least December 31, 2028.
