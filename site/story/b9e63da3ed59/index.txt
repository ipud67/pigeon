18:T484,Turkey pulled the operating license for Iran's Mellat Bank on September 18. The move lines up with stepped-up US sanctions that target Iranian banks for their role in evading restrictions on oil sales and financial transfers. Turkish regulators cited compliance with international banking rules as the reason for the revocation.

This is the latest in a string of similar license withdrawals by Turkey and other countries since the US tightened secondary sanctions in 2024. Mellat Bank had kept a branch in Istanbul to handle trade payments between the two neighbors. Without the license the branch must close or shift to limited non-banking activities.

How long this lasts depends on whether Iran can reroute its remaining Turkish transactions through other banks or if Turkey faces fresh US pressure to cut more links. The next clear procedural step is the formal shutdown deadline set by Turkish authorities, expected within weeks. The situation would change only if the US eases the underlying sanctions or if Turkey grants a new license after a compliance review.
