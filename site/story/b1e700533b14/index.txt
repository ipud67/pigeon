This follows House Democrats filing similar articles in April focused on the same Iran operations, which a preliminary investigation tied to an accidental strike on a girls' elementary school that killed as many as 175 people.

The House now has two legislative days to take up the articles or vote to table them. With Massie on his way out and both parties' prior filings centered on the same dispute over authority for deployments, the measure has little floor momentum. No court action has produced a ruling on the underlying legal claims.

This step stays alive only if a committee takes it up and forces a vote; otherwise it ends on the calendar. Next indicator is whether leadership schedules debate or tables it outright.
