17:T45d,The Islamabad Bar Association called a one-day boycott of court work on Saturday after police rearrested lawyer Imaan Zainab Mazari-Hazir and her husband Hadi Ali Chattha on fresh charges late Thursday. The Supreme Court had just suspended their earlier sentences and directed their release on bail in a case tied to social-media posts. Lawyers say the new arrest looks like a move to keep them in custody despite the court order.

The boycott is a standard procedural step used by bar associations when they see interference with court rulings. It puts direct pressure on the district courts and the police to explain the timing of the second case. How long it lasts will depend on whether the Supreme Court hears arguments on the rearrest early next week or sends the matter back to a lower court for review.

If the court schedules an urgent hearing and repeats its earlier directions, the lawyers will likely end the boycott and return to work. Without that hearing the boycott could spread to other bar groups or stretch into next week.

Next move hinges on the Supreme Court listing the case.
