This fits the pattern that has been running since early 2024: Ukraine keeps launching long-range strikes inside Russia whenever they can reach new targets with drones or modified missiles. Russian air defenses have intercepted many but not all of these attacks, so the refineries, airfields, and production shops stay on the target list.

How much longer this goes depends on two observable things: whether Ukraine keeps getting fresh batches of long-range strike weapons from Western partners, and whether Russia can move enough air-defense systems or jamming gear to cover these sites. Right now both sides are still able to replace losses and keep flying, so the strikes continue at a pace of several per month.

Next rung is probably more of the same: Ukraine will claim fresh hits on oil or air targets, Russia will report intercepting most drones, and each side will wait to see if the other escalates with new weapon types or deeper strikes. The situation only shifts if one side loses the ability to produce or supply the drones and missiles that make these raids possible.
