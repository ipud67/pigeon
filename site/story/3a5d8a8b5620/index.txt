The companies have expanded fast since 2018, when Flock first sold its pole-mounted units to small-town departments. Axon and Motorola added their own AI camera lines to existing police contracts. Verkada focused on indoor enterprise systems with cloud storage. No prior Senate hearing on this exact panel has occurred.
