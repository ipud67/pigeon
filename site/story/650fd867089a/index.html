Provincial deputies from Kasai-Central concerned by delay in Kananga-Kalamba Mbuji road works

Translated from French · read the original →
The original headlineLes députés provinciaux du Kasaï-Central préoccupés par le retard des travaux de la route Kananga-Kalamba Mbuji

The delay recorded in the execution of works on the Kananga-Kalamba Mbuji road continues to raise concerns within the Assembly of Kasai-Central. A of provincial met on Thursday 8 October in Kinshasa with officials of the Congolese Agency for Major Works (ACGT) to review the progress of this project considered strategic for the Kasai region.
