Sharqiya Governorate imposes total ban on tree cutting to save greenery

Sharqiya Governorate in Egypt operates under a system of that set rules for land use and public works inside each governorate. These decrees run alongside national programs that direct governorates to add green cover through planting and maintenance on public land.

On September 19 the governor of Sharqiya, Hazem al-Ashmouny, issued a total ban on cutting any trees throughout the province. The order prohibits felling in all municipalities and villages, directs penalties for violations, and requires provincial teams to irrigate, prune, and plant on vacant roadside land and town entrances as part of the national expansion of green zones.

The province now enforces the prohibition while its ground teams carry out the planting and care schedule. The situation turns on compliance with the ban and the pace at which new trees are added under the presidential initiative.
