19:T438,The FCC just put out a proposed rule asking for public comments on tweaking the Rural Health Care Program. They want input on how to define similar services and rural areas, ways to replace the old cost study method for setting telecom rates, backup tech options, an eligible services list, performance metrics to speed up funding decisions, and dropping the evergreen contract approvals plus the yearly report requirement. This sits on top of years of the Commission slowly adjusting the RHC program to get better broadband into rural clinics and hospitals. Next concrete step is the comment window closing, then FCC staff sorts the replies and drafts final rules. How long it runs depends on how many heavy comments roll in from carriers, rural providers, and states; big pushback on rate changes or metrics could drag the review into 2027. If the comments line up with the proposals, the Commission could vote final rules by late 2026 or early 2027 and start rolling out the new setup in 2028 funding cycles.
