Satisfaction scores for state performance sit below 4 out of 10 across housing, education, transportation and youth conditions, with housing at 2.5. About 60 percent of respondents place themselves on the right or center-right.

This focus on domestic pocketbook matters has held steady through the US-Iran fighting that began in late February 2026 and narrowed to control of the Strait of Hormuz. The poll shows no measurable shift in voter priorities tied to the July collapse of the Islamabad Memorandum or the resumed nightly US strikes.

The election cycle will keep domestic issues in front of candidates. Parties that put forward concrete plans on housing, education and transport stand to pick up undecided young voters, as 75 percent say they would consider a new party that shows courage on long-term fixes. Security talk stays secondary unless a major escalation directly hits Israeli population centers.

The pattern points to continued emphasis on cost-of-living platforms through the campaign. Any change would require either a sharp drop in housing prices or a security event that registers on daily life for this age group.
