Energy bills to rise in coming years, especially for households with heat pumps

Translated from Dutch · read the original →
The original headlineEnergierekening stijgt de komende jaren, vooral voor mensen met warmtepomp

Households across the country have paid energy bills that track the cost of gas, electricity and other fuels under a price-cap system set by regulators each quarter. That system has stood since regulators took over direct control of default in 2019 after years of supplier failures and volatile wholesale markets.

A draft letter prepared for the Council of Ministers projects the average bill will climb ten percent across the next five years. The increase hits hardest for homes that run heat pumps, because the letter sets out higher projected charges on the electricity those pumps draw compared with homes that still burn gas. Ministers are scheduled to discuss the letter on Friday.

The new figures lock in a steeper long-term path for electricity-led heating than for legacy gas systems and shift the debate inside government onto how the grid charges and subsidy design will distribute those added costs.
