1d:T402,The US removed Theint Win Htet from its SDN List on October 9 after she filed a lawsuit to get off it. This step follows a report that the Trump administration started direct talks with Myanmar's military government, which took power in the 2021 coup. The Treasury Department posted the delisting with no stated link to the talks.

The delisting is a concrete procedural win for one sanctioned individual tied to a military-linked businessman. It shows the US is now willing to adjust specific blacklist entries while talks continue. Further delistings or broader policy shifts would need clear movement in those talks or new court filings by others on the list.

This pattern of targeted adjustments is likely to continue as long as the talks stay open and more sanctioned parties file legal challenges. Next it could move toward additional names removed from the SDN List or a formal easing of broader restrictions if the talks produce concrete agreements.
