November 13, 2026

Securities and Exchange Commission approved Nasdaq ISE rule change for options on trusts that hold digital commodities

The Securities and Exchange Commission holds authority under Section 19 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 to review rule changes filed by self-regulatory organizations such as Nasdaq ISE. Exchanges must meet standards for listed options on underlying securities before those contracts can trade on their platforms.

On November 5, 2026, Nasdaq ISE withdrew an earlier and filed Amendment No. 2, which replaced the original proposal in full. The amendment also spells out continued listing rules for the shares and the options, the exchange's direct access to Nasdaq data for investigations, and the trading, surveillance, and systems that will govern the new contracts.

The commission published the notice of the filing and granted accelerated approval of the rule change as modified by Amendment No. 2. The listing criteria now sit in ISE Options 4, Section 3, and the options on these trusts become for trading once all other exchange requirements are met.
