September 24, 2026 · 13:08 UTC

A federal judge ordered the White House to restore access for CNN, MSNBC and Politico early Thursday

A federal judge temporarily blocked President Donald Trump’s ban of three news outlets from the White House grounds, ordering early Thursday that CNN, MS NOW and Politico must have their access restored.

Constitutional analysis
Does Judge orders White House to restore access to barred media companies keep the executive inside the powers the Constitution enumerates (Art. I §8; 10th Amendment), or does it extend federal reach beyond them?
By the founding standardIf the action uses a power the Constitution actually lists in Article I §8, it stays inside the lines the founders drew. Madison called the federal powers "few and defined" (Federalist 45).
The founding-based tensionIf it reaches into matters the 10th Amendment leaves to the states or the people, it stretches past those lines. The founders meant the states' powers to stay "numerous and indefinite" (Federalist 45).
— Federalist No. 45 (Madison); Constitution Art. I §8; Amendment X
Is the executive exercising its own constitutional power here, or absorbing one assigned to another branch (Federalist 51; for war, Art. I §8 cl. 11)?
By the founding standardIf the executive is using a power that belongs to its own branch, it respects the split Madison built so "ambition counteract[s] ambition" (Federalist 51).
The founding-based tensionIf the executive takes a power the Constitution gives another branch — only Congress can declare war (Art. I §8 cl. 11) — that piling-up of power in one hand is what Madison called "the very definition of tyranny" (Federalist 47).
— Federalist No. 51 & No. 47 (Madison); Constitution Art. I §8 cl. 11
