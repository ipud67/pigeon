Florida plastic surgeon Tomasz Kosowski defended himself in a death penalty trial for the murder of attorney Steven Cozzi whose body was never found

A Florida plastic surgeon stood trial for first-degree murder after a colleague accused him of shooting and dismembering an attorney inside his office. The charge against Tomasz Kosowski dates to 2022 when Steven Cozzi disappeared after a scheduled appointment at the surgeon's practice in the Tampa area. Prosecutors said Kosowski killed Cozzi during a dispute over a case the lawyer had filed against him on behalf of a former patient, then disposed of the body so it was never found.

Kosowski chose to represent himself before the jury that began hearing evidence on September 20 2026 in a Pinellas County courtroom where could bring the death penalty. He told the panel the state had no physical proof linking him to any crime and argued that investigators rushed to blame him without locating a body or a murder weapon.

The case now rests on whether the evidence of the appointment, the prior lawsuit and Kosowski's own statements can establish guilt beyond a reasonable doubt in the absence of a corpse.
