1d:T50e,A federal judge in California ruled Friday that the Trump administration violated the constitutional rights of foreign students by using immigration rules to yank their visas over pro-Palestinian activism. The 90-page order from U.S. District Judge Noël Wise marks the latest step in lawsuits that started after the administration began canceling student visas tied to criticism of Israel. Government lawyers now have to decide whether to appeal to the 9th Circuit or adjust their enforcement on these cases.

The ruling blocks further use of that visa-cancellation approach against these students unless the government shows it did not target protected speech. How long this holds depends on whether the Trump administration files an appeal within the standard 60-day window and how fast the appeals court moves. If the 9th Circuit upholds the order, the visas stay intact and similar cases get harder to bring; if the government wins on appeal or the Supreme Court takes it up, the original policy could resume.

Next rung is the procedural deadline for any appeal and any immediate stay request from the Justice Department. Without fresh enforcement action or a higher-court reversal, the students keep their status.
