The last such vote came in 2024 when the House passed a version of the release bill but the Senate took no action. A successful discharge petition would bypass committee and leadership control and set a floor vote within days once the signatures are formally filed.

The procedural next step is for the two members to present the petition to the clerk and trigger the vote. For the situation to change, either enough signatures would have to fall away before filing or the House would have to pass the measure and send it to the Senate where it would need 60 votes to clear a filibuster.

This round of signatures looks likely to hold at least through the end of the month given the public commitments. The effort stalls if the petition is filed and the bill fails again on the floor.
