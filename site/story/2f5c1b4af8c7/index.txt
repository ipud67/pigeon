Treasury yields and spreads stayed steady in the days right after the bar: 3-mo bill at 3.9 percent, 2-yr at 4.34 percent, 10-yr at 4.73 percent, 30-yr at 5.22 percent, and 2s10s spread at 39 basis points as of August 28. The restriction is likely to hold for the rest of this G20 cycle. Pushback will probably stay inside the press corps and a few congressional offices, with no sign yet of lawsuits or broader credential fights.
