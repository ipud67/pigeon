Two non-citizens were charged with casting ballots where they could not legally vote, and one person was charged with registering an ineligible voter.

The cases sit at the indictment stage. Brass was arrested last week, the others remain at large or in custody pending arraignment. Next steps are initial court appearances, plea negotiations or trials, and possible sentencing if convictions follow. The situation changes only if prosecutors drop charges, defendants win acquittals, or new arrests widen the probe.
