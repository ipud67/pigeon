Taiwan will auction two Taichung MRT cars damaged in a 2023 crane accident that killed one and injured 15 others for a starting price of NT$180,000

Two Taichung MRT cars damaged in a 2023 crane boom accident that killed one person and injured 15 others will be auctioned off at a starting price of NT$180,000 (US$5,468) on the Taipei Shwoo Net…
