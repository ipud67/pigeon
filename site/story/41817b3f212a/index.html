Queensland 18yo charged with possessing violent extremist material

Police in Queensland run a joint counter terrorism team that takes tips from foreign agencies about users on social media platforms. That arrangement has operated for years. On 27 May this year officers executed a search at a house in Warner north of Brisbane after the FBI flagged an Australian account that was posting material on multiple channels.

Forensic review of seized devices turned up content that met the legal definition of violent extremist material together with child abuse material. The 18-year-old man who lived there was charged with 30 offences that day including transmitting violent extremist material and possessing child abuse material by carriage service. He appeared in court immediately and was remanded in .

He is scheduled to appear again in Pine Rivers Court on 18 September.
