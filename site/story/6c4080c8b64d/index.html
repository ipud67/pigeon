Trump: 'With my support Netanyahu went from defeat to victory'

Translated from Italian · read the original →
The original headlineTrump, 'con il mio sostegno Netanyahu passato dal ko alla vittoria'

The US and Israel opened strikes on Iran in late February 2026. A June memorandum lifted the dual blockades and opened a 60-day window that collapsed on 8 July and brought US strikes. The fight has narrowed to control of the Strait of Hormuz.

Trump said with his support Netanyahu went from defeat to victory. The president added that he only wants the best for the Middle East for Israel and for the United States.

The war now turns on nightly strikes against Iranian surveillance sites military logistics underground weapons storage capabilities and infrastructure in Hormozgan province.
