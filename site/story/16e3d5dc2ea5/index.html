Ambassador Greer to Travel to Milwaukee, Wisconsin to Host the G20 Trade Ministerial pbrouillette Fri, 09/25/2026 - 12:01 WASHINGTON – Next week, Ambassador Greer will travel to Milwaukee, Wisconsin to host the G20 Trade Ministerial. During the ministerial, Ambassador Greer will spearhead discussions with the G20 Trade Ministers on a wide range of issues, including eliminating forced labor in global supply chains, updating the Most-Favored Nation (MFN) principle, denouncing the used of trade in food, and addressing structural excess capacity and production. On the margins of the ministerial, Ambassador Greer will tour Rockwell Automation with the G20 Ministers. While walking the factory floor, Ambassador Greer and the G20 Ministers will meet with blue-collar workers and industry leaders to discuss how President Trump’s trade program is accelerating America’s reindustrialization and reinforcing our supply chains.
