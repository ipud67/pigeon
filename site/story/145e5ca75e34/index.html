UK joined the CPTPP trade pact back in 2023 after two years of talks. The deal cleared all member approvals and ratification steps by late last month. As of 1 September this year, UK firms get zero-tariff access to the full £13 trillion bloc that already includes Japan, Canada, Australia, Vietnam and seven others. This setup now runs on autopilot unless a member walks away or new rules get written. No big procedural votes sit on the calendar. The only things that could unwind it are a formal withdrawal notice from London or a consensus vote by all twelve members to change membership terms. It will keep rolling forward on current terms for years unless a new UK government files to exit or the bloc agrees to rewrite the accession rules.
