17:T414,Death toll in the Nepal-Tibet floods has now topped 600 with almost 3,000 people still listed as missing. Rescue teams keep pulling bodies from rivers and landslides while crews dig through fresh mud and rock in the hardest-hit valleys. The monsoon rains that started the slides have eased in the past 48 hours but forecasts call for another round of heavy rain in the next week.

Search-and-recovery operations will likely run at least another two to three weeks before crews shift to longer-term cleanup. The missing count will probably drop as more bodies are found but could stay high if remote villages remain cut off. Government crews are moving supplies by helicopter and mule; any fresh downpour that blocks those routes would stall progress fast.

Next visible change comes when the official missing list stops growing and aid groups declare the rescue phase over. That shift usually happens once daily body recoveries fall below a handful for several days straight.
