Chiclayo celebrates Pope Leo XIV's birthday with faith and recalls his closeness to Monsefú

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineChiclayo celebra cumpleaños del papa León XIV con fe y recuerda su cercanía con Monsefú

The diocese of Chiclayo has marked the feast of Jesús Nazareno Cautivo de Monsefú on September 14 for decades. The event draws crowds from the town of Monsefú and surrounding districts each year. Robert Prevost served as bishop of the diocese from 2015 until his election as pope in 2025.

On September 14 2026 the local Catholic community held Masses and processions that also honored the new pope's birthday. The date lines up exactly with the saint's feast he led while bishop. Parishes across Chiclayo recalled how Prevost joined the Monsefú procession and stayed close to the people there.

The observances now tie the annual regional devotion directly to the pope's personal calendar. Catholics in the diocese plan further events that continue to link their traditional feast with the man who once presided over it.
