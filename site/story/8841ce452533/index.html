German units reinforce Gotland in NATO operation

Translated from Swedish · read the original →
The original headlineTyska förband förstärker Gotland i Nato-operation

Sweden kept Gotland as a demilitarized zone after the Cold War ended. In 2015 the Swedish government reversed that policy and began to station troops there again. The island sits in the middle of the Baltic Sea between the Swedish mainland and the Russian exclave of Kaliningrad. Sweden joined NATO in 2024. The alliance drew up plans that call for rapid reinforcement of the island by member forces if a conflict starts.

German units began to arrive on the island today. They moved in alongside Swedish troops in a live exercise that tests the NATO reinforcement plans drawn up since Sweden joined the alliance.

The exercise now runs under combined Swedish and German command. It checks how quickly alliance forces can reach the island by sea and air, how they integrate with Swedish units already in place, and what supplies and routes would be needed to hold the position in a real crisis.
