Congress: Committee approves report recommending ratification of Julio Velarde at BCR

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineCongreso: Comisión aprueba informe que recomienda ratificar a Julio Velarde en el BCR

The Central Reserve Bank of Peru has operated under a board president appointed by the executive and confirmed by the since the 1990s. Congress holds the power to or reject that choice through its Special Procedures Commission, which reviews the nominee and votes on a report that then goes to the full chamber.

On September 9 the commission, led by Hugo Ccahuana Aymachoque of Por el Perú, voted by majority to approve the report recommending of Julio Velarde Flores for another term as board president.

The report now moves to the full Senate for a final vote that will decide whether Velarde continues to head the institution.
