CROATIA — 1 outlets report: Yang Yung-wei (楊勇緯), Taiwan's Olympic medalist, won the men's 60-kilogram judo title at the Zagreb Grand Prix in Croatia after battling Kazakhstan's Aman Bakytzhan for more than 11 minutes in the final on Friday.
