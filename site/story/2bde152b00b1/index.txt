He paid people to send over 100 fake registration forms to Pinellas County elections officials in early 2023 so he could build synthetic identities, then used those to open credit cards and loans against banks hundreds of times. The case started with an FBI probe that included the Florida Department of Law Enforcement and postal inspectors; prosecutors from the Justice Department Public Integrity Section and the Middle District of Florida handled it from the July 2025 charging papers onward.

The sentence marks the end of this specific federal case. Shushlebin now moves into the Bureau of Prisons system to serve the term. No further court dates are set unless he files an appeal or the restitution collection hits a snag.

This pattern of using voter rolls to seed fake identities for bank fraud has shown up in other federal cases over the past decade. Without new co-conspirators coming forward or fresh fraud tied to the same ring, the matter stays closed after he finishes the prison stretch and the three years of supervised release.
