MTC sets rules for commercial calls: those starting with 500 want to sell you something

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineMTC fija reglas para llamadas comerciales: si empiezan con 500, quieren venderte algo

Peru has required phone companies to assign numbers in blocks that start with certain digits to government agencies, businesses and individuals since the early 2000s. The system lets callers see the origin of a call or text before they answer. Mobile operators must route those numbers through approved gateways and display them on handsets.

On September 15 the Ministry of Transport and Communications published a supreme that sets aside the entire 500 block for commercial and advertising calls and messages. Any number beginning with 500 now signals that the sender wants to sell a product or service. Companies that use these numbers must register with the ministry and follow new identification rules.

The decree gives phone users and regulators a single clear marker to separate sales traffic from other calls. Operators have until a set deadline to update their routing tables and inform customers. The ministry will enforce the block through its existing oversight of numbering plans.
