AREQUIPA
September 10, 2026 · 19:21 UTC

Sunass handled more than 4,000 inquiries from water and sewer users in Arequipa

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineSunass atendió más de 4 mil consultas de usuarios de agua y alcantarillado en Arequipa

Sunass has run the office for drinking water and sewer services across Peru since the 1990s. In Arequipa its local team fields calls and written requests from households and businesses that pay monthly bills to the regional provider Sedapar.

Between January and August 2026 that team logged 4,939 separate inquiries and requests for guidance. The count reached that total by the end of August, before the office released the figure on September 10.

The volume shows how many users turned to the regulator this year with questions about billing, service cuts, quality, or complaints against the provider. The office now stands with a record that covers eight months and will add any new cases that arrive in the remaining four months of 2026.
