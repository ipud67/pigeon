Domestic Quarantine: Quarantined Areas and Regulated Articles

The Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service maintains lists of domestic areas under for plant pests. The agency places specific counties, parishes or parts of states on those lists when it detects regulated pests. Once listed, the areas face movement restrictions on certain plants, soil, equipment and other articles that could spread the pests. Those lists have stood for years and get updated as surveys find new infestations or declare areas free of them.

On September 11, 2026, the agency told the public that it had revised the lists during 2025. The changes consist of additions, removals and boundary adjustments across multiple plant pest programs.

The revised lists now govern which articles require permits or treatments before they can move out of the quarantined zones. The agency posted the current versions on the program websites where growers, shippers and inspectors check compliance requirements.
