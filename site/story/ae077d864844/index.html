FRANCE
November 24, 2026 · 19:52 UTC

FRANCE — 5 outlets report: details pending.

Senegal on Tuesday regained their Africa Cup of Nations title after winning an appeal at the Court of for Sport (CAS) against the decision by the of African Football to declare Morocco the winners following a chaotic final in January. CAS said in a statement it had "upheld the appeal by the Senegalese Football Federation (FSF) against the Confederation of African Football (CAF) and the Royal Moroccan Football Federation (FRMF)" by a " decision". FRANCE 24's Emerald Maxwell reports.
