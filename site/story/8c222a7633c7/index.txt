18:T416,Trump is set to sit down with Venezuela's interim president Rodriguez in New York on Tuesday. The meeting follows years of US recognition of opposition figures as the legitimate leadership after disputed elections in Caracas. Rodriguez holds no formal control inside Venezuela where Maduro's government still runs the state apparatus and security forces.

The talk will likely focus on what steps the US could take to widen that gap between the two claims to power. Next moves hinge on whether Rodriguez can show tangible backing from more countries or institutions that currently treat Maduro as the sitting president. Without shifts in who controls the oil revenue or the military inside Venezuela the interim label stays in place.

This pattern of high-profile meetings has repeated since 2019 without flipping the ground situation. The Tuesday session counts as one more procedural step unless Rodriguez brings new leverage that forces other governments to pick a side.
