US military strikes alleged drug boat in Caribbean, killing 4 ‘narco-terrorists’

The U.S. military has struck boats in the Caribbean and elsewhere in Latin America since the Trump administration began the campaign against what it calls narcoterrorists one year ago. Those operations have killed at least 231 people across 69 strikes.

A strike on September 20 hit an alleged drug-smuggling boat in the Caribbean and killed four people, the Pentagon said.

The total death count from the campaign now stands at least 235. The pattern of strikes continues on the same terms that have governed it for the past year.
