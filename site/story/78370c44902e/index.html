Ssangyong E&C wins $2.1 bln hospital construction project in Singapore

South Korean construction companies began winning large contracts in Singapore in the 1970s when the city-state started its drive to build public . Ssangyong Engineering & Construction entered that market early and completed several high-profile buildings there over the decades, among them Marina Bay Sands, Raffles City, W Singapore and the Grand Hyatt Singapore. The company kept a permanent presence on the island while Singapore continued to expand its health-care capacity to match a growing and aging population.

On Sept. 17, 2026, Ssangyong E&C announced it had been chosen as the sole contractor for the Tengah General Hospital project commissioned by Singapore’s Ministry of Health. The $2.1 billion order covers seven buildings that will hold 1,534 beds in the western district of Tengah. It is the largest single contract the company has ever secured.

The award gives Ssangyong E&C a 63-month schedule that includes design work and locks in its role as lead builder on one of Singapore’s next major public hospitals. The company now moves from past landmark projects to the construction phase of this new medical complex.

Thinking ahead
Ssangyong E&C landed the $2.1 billion contract to build Tengah General Hospital in Singapore on Sept. 17 2026. The job covers seven buildings with 1,534 beds and runs 63 months from design through finish. It sits on top of the company's earlier Singapore work that includes Marina Bay Sands, Raffles City, W Singapore, and Grand Hyatt. No prior contract size from the firm exceeds this one. The project is locked in and runs on a fixed schedule with no visible off-ramps right now. Construction demand in Singapore has pulled in Korean builders for years, so this fits the pattern. Nothing in the tender or the company's statement points to delays or scope creep yet. The deal itself will keep moving forward on its 63-month track. Next visible marker is the start of site work after design wraps. No market indicators tie directly to this contract. Horizon: at least 63 months on current schedule.
