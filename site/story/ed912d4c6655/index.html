Taiwan's Hsieh Su-wei and Liang En-shuo won the Asian Games women's doubles tennis gold in Nagoya Japan on Saturday

Nagoya, Oct. 3 (CNA) Hsieh Su-wei (謝淑薇) and Liang En-shuo (梁恩碩) won women's tennis doubles gold at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Saturday, securing Taiwan's fifth gold medal of the Games.
