Peru Judiciary promotes Eurolatin American network to combat corruption and transnational crime

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlinePJ impulsa red eurolatinoamericana para combatir corrupción y crimen transnacional

Judges in Peru and Europe have worked for years on ways to seize money and property tied to cross-border crime cases that one country alone cannot finish. Peru’s courts took part in those talks through existing agreements on mutual legal aid that date back more than a decade.

On 16 September 2026 the Peruvian Judiciary released the draft of the Eurolatin American Network for Asset . The text sets up a permanent group of specialized judges from both regions who will share case files, coordinate seizures, and enforce forfeiture orders across borders under rules written into the statute itself.

The draft now sits with participating judicial bodies for review and signature. Once adopted, the network will operate as a standing channel for handing off evidence and seized assets between member courts without starting a new treaty each time a case crosses the Atlantic.

Thinking ahead
The Peruvian Judiciary put forward a draft statute on September 16 for a new Eurolatin American Network for Asset Forfeiture. The plan lines up specialized judges from Europe and Latin America to handle cases on corruption and cross-border organized crime. Next step is review and approval of the statute by the participating countries before any judges start joint operations. This network builds on existing judicial cooperation agreements that Peru has used in past asset cases. How long it runs depends on how many countries ratify the statute and fund the shared database and training. If ratification stalls in two or more key nations the project stays on paper; if at least six sign on within a year the first joint forfeiture cases could start in 2027. Observable indicators are the number of countries that formally adopt the statute and the first recorded transfer of seized assets between members. Horizon: 12 to 18 months until first operational cases or clear stall.
