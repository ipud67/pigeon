Judge dismisses Trump lawsuit against Iowa newspaper and pollster

A federal judge in Iowa has authority under the rules of civil procedure to end a lawsuit before trial when the claims cannot succeed as a matter of law. Donald Trump filed suit in state court against the Des Moines Register and a pollster it hired after the newspaper published a poll in the fall of 2024 that showed him trailing Kamala Harris in Iowa. The suit accused the paper and the pollster of publishing false numbers to hurt his campaign and sought damages for and false light.

On September 24 2026 the judge dismissed the entire case with prejudice. The ruling held that the poll results counted as protected opinion based on methodology and that Trump had not pleaded facts sufficient to show actual , the standard that a public figure must meet to win damages for defamation.

The newspaper and pollster no longer face any claims in the matter. Trump has the option to appeal the dismissal to the Iowa Court of Appeals.
