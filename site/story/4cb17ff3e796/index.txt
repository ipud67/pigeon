1a:T48c,Commerce has run the administrative review on the antidumping duty order for mattresses from Poland that started July 1 2024. The single mandatory respondent CORRECT reported no sales during that period and supplied data showing the one entry logged by Customs was not a bona fide sale. On that record Commerce now says it will likely drop the review.

This step sits on the ladder that began with the order published in July 2024, the requests filed by both the company and petitioners in late July 2025, the selection of CORRECT alone in September 2025, and the repeated deadline extensions that pushed preliminary results into October 2026.

The review has already run fourteen months past the end of the period it covers. With no bona fide sales found and no other respondents left, the case has reached the point where rescission is the standard next move once comments are in. No fresh indicators point to reversal.

The notice is likely to become final within three to four months. After that the order stays in place with the existing cash-deposit rate until the next review window opens or someone ships again.
