Watch: Trump says ban on some news outlets is 'because they're fake news'

The White House has long maintained a press pool that gives certain outlets regular access to the president and his staff for briefings and travel. That setup dates back decades through of both parties and lets reporters from selected organizations ask questions on behalf of a larger group.

On September 18 2026 Donald Trump announced he is immediately removing CNN MSNBC and Politico from that pool. He stated the outlets are banned because they are fake news and the move came during remarks carried on video.

The pool now operates without those three organizations. Access turns on which the White House chooses to honor for the daily rotation.
