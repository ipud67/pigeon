WASHINGTON
November 4, 2026

A gunman killed a Spokane County deputy and two others in Washington state on Tuesday, a second deputy is on life support

On November 3 a woman ran to a neighbor's house and said her brother had shot her. learned the man had an active arrest and access to several guns, which raised the possibility that other people remained inside the house, so they called in the SWAT team.

They returned fire, pulled two wounded deputies to safety, and transported them to a hospital along with the woman who had first reported the shooting. One deputy died at the hospital. Investigators later found one person dead inside the house, another body in the yard, and the also dead on the property. The second deputy remained in critical condition on life support and is not expected to survive.

The sheriff's office confirmed the second deputy is an organ donor and that arrangements for donation are underway. The identities of the dead and the reason the shooting began have not been released.
