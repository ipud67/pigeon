Talks like these have run every year or two since launch, so the pattern points to another round in 2027 or 2028. No court action or new treaty text appeared this week, so the immediate next rung is a joint readout expected in the next 30 days that will list any new pilot projects or barrier-reduction steps the two sides commit to test.

This round is likely to continue on the same two-year cadence for at least the next 12–18 months. It will probably produce a short list of sector-specific pilots in digital trade and advanced manufacturing by early 2027, anchored to the same small-firm export numbers and investment stock that have held steady since 2024.
