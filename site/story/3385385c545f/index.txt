23:T43f,The 10th US-UK Small and Medium Enterprise Dialogue took place in Cardiff on September 9 2026. It is the latest meeting in a series that started in 2018 under the Trump Administration and has alternated between the two countries since then. Officials from USTR, Commerce, SBA, and their UK counterparts met with small business groups to talk through trade barriers, manufacturing shifts, digital and AI innovation, and supply chain links. The US-UK goods trade stood at $162 billion last year with $1.8 trillion in two-way investment, and roughly 90 percent of American exporters to Britain are small and medium firms that shipped $22.1 billion in 2024.

This format has run twice a year on average since launch. With no new trade deal announced and the same topics recurring, the dialogue is likely to keep its current pace of one or two sessions per year. The next rung will probably be the 11th meeting in the US in early 2027 unless a concrete policy change such as tariff adjustment or regulatory alignment alters the schedule.
