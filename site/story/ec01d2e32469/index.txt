District officials have told village heads to watch 50 households already wet outside the main embankments and to keep lists of vulnerable residents ready. This setup holds while the river stays below the outer bank line and dam releases do not jump sharply. If levels top the outer bank the barriers become the next line and crews will run the pumps longer. The district has already moved to the next procedural step of briefing community leaders on further rises. No new barrier sites or evacuation orders have appeared yet.

The protection work at the two temples is likely to stay in place at least until the dam release pattern shifts or the rainy season ends. Next visible change would be either extra sandbag layers if the gap to the bank closes or pump logs if seepage starts.
