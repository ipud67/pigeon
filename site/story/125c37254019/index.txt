17:T415,The National Vaccine Injury Compensation Program has run since 1986 under the Public Health Service Act. It gives individuals a no-fault route to file petitions for compensation over injuries disabilities illnesses conditions or deaths they say came from covered childhood vaccines listed in the Vaccine Injury Table at 42 CFR 100.3. That table names each vaccine the conditions that can support a claim and the exact time window after vaccination when the first symptom must appear. Petitioners file directly with the United States Court of Federal Claims and serve a copy on the Secretary of Health and Human Services who is named respondent in every case. The Secretary has assigned day-to-day duties to the Health Resources and Services Administration. The court appoints special masters to hear evidence run proceedings and issue the first ruling on eligibility and payment amount. Claims can cover table injuries inside the time limits or off-table injuries or those outside the limits if the petitioner proves the vaccine caused the harm.19:T4d5,HRSA published its monthly list of new petitions filed under the National Vaccine Injury Compensation Program for injuries claimed from covered childhood vaccines. The list covers filings received from August 1 through August 31 2026 and names each petitioner plus the state where the shot was given. The statute requires this notice within 30 days of service on the Secretary of HHS so the public record stays current.

These petitions now sit with the Court of Federal Claims where special masters will schedule evidence hearings and rule on eligibility and amounts. Most prior monthly notices have run 20 to 60 new cases with the majority settled or dismissed inside 18 to 36 months. The pace of filings has held steady for the last three years at roughly 500 to 700 petitions per year.

The next rung comes when HRSA publishes the September list in mid-October. If that count jumps above 80 or clusters around one vaccine the court docket will lengthen and more special-master time will be assigned. Otherwise the system keeps its current backlog and timeline.

This routine notice will continue at the same monthly cadence until Congress changes the statute or the vaccine injury table.
