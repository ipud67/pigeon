House Republicans sent a resolution to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee

Congress has used it against secretaries before but not against a civilian who runs the Pentagon in 150 years.

Republicans who control both chambers voted along party lines to refer an resolution against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee. The move kept the measure from reaching a floor vote and left it shelved for the rest of the current Congress. The resolution had accused him of high crimes and tied to military operations in Iran Venezuela and Yemen which he called lawful orders from the commander in chief.

The effort now stands as an abortive attempt that Democrats say they will revive once they take the majority on January 3. The unrepealed resolution remains in committee and the question of whether the House will act on it turns on the next Congress.
