The strike killed 53 security recruits and wounded more than 200 at the Martyr Ahmed al-Sulaimani Training Centre, according to the provincial media office and army spokesman. Hospitals moved the worst cases to Hadramout and Aden. Houthi spokesman Yahya Sarea said the camp held Saudi officers and enemy concentrations; government officials called it a non-military target.

This sits inside the wider Yemen civil war that sharpened in early October when pro-government units, backed by Saudi air power, began pushing to retake Red Sea coast ground and areas around Taiz, Marib and al-Jawf. The Houthis answered with missile and drone hits on Saudi bases and facilities. The Shabwa attack ranks among the highest-casualty single strikes since that flare-up began.
