19:T407,Russia has kept up nightly drone strikes on Ukraine for the last two years, ramping up the number of cheap UAVs sent in single waves as Ukrainian air defenses improved. On 12 September they fired 410 in one day, the highest single-day total recorded so far. Ukrainian forces reported downing or jamming 336 of them while 21 got through and hit targets.

The pattern shows Russia testing defense gaps with volume, then adjusting the next waves based on what gets through. Ukrainian crews have steadily raised their intercept rate by adding electronic jamming and mobile gun teams, but each big night still produces some strikes on energy sites or rear areas.

Next moves depend on whether Russia can sustain 300-to-400-drone nights without burning through stocks faster than it replaces them, and whether Ukraine receives more Western jamming gear or longer-range interceptors. No sign yet of either side changing the basic night-after-night rhythm.
