19:T509,NASA gave Modulate Space Corporation a firm-fixed-price contract worth about $38 million to build and test key pieces of a 5G system with built-in Wi-Fi 6 for the lunar surface. The work splits into two phases: first a network-in-a-box setup gets built and shown in a lab by January 31 2028, then the full integrated 5G and Wi-Fi network flies to the Moon for surface tests that wrap up December 31 2028. The contract runs through NASA’s Glenn Research Center in Ohio and feeds directly into larger plans for a sustained Moon base where crews need reliable voice, data, and video links that follow open standards so more gear can plug in later. 

The next visible step is the start of Phase 1 hardware and software work on October 1. If the lab demos hit their performance targets on schedule, the project moves to flight hardware fabrication and integration with a lunar lander mission. Any slip in the 2028 flight window would push the surface test into the early 2030s when NASA lines up the next set of crewed landings.

This contract marks one more rung in the steady ladder of lunar comms development that has run since the Artemis program laid out its surface architecture years ago.
