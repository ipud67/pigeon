Rescuers extinguish fires caused by Russian attack in Kyiv's Podilskyi District

Russian forces have struck the Ukrainian capital with drones and missiles at intervals since the full-scale invasion began in February 2022. Kyiv maintains layered air defenses around the city and its districts, including Podilskyi, where and residential buildings stand close together along the Dnipro River.

On September 11 a Russian UAV hit a five-story administrative building in Podilskyi District. The impact started fires that spread through parts of the structure.

Rescue crews put out the fires later that afternoon. No casualty figures have been released for the strike.
