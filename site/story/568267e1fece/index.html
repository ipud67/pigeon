DELAWARE
September 21, 2026

Hunter charged with manslaughter after he allegedly shot a woman he mistook for a deer

Walter Moorhead of Salisbury, Maryland, had been hunting deer from a ground blind in an open field near Ellendale, Delaware.

On the evening of September 19, 2026, he fired a shot at what he believed was a deer and struck a 39-year-old woman who was walking through the same field with a man and two children.

Troopers reached the scene, gave the woman life-saving medical care, and transported her to a hospital where she was pronounced dead. Moorhead was taken into custody and now faces manslaughter charges referred to the Delaware State Police Homicide Unit.
