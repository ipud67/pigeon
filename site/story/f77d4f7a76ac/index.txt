21:T4e9,The Australian Grand Prix Corporation lost its appeal today when three Court of Appeal justices unanimously refused to overturn the 2024 Victorian Supreme Court order. That order required the corporation to pay World Touring Melbourne $2.84 million for breaching its contract over the cancelled Robbie Williams show scheduled for March 14 2020 at Lakeside Stadium. The original ruling found organisers had given the promoter misleading information about Chief Health Officer Brett Sutton directing the cancellation when no such direction existed.

The corporation has now exhausted its main avenues in the Victorian courts. Next step would require it to seek special leave from the High Court of Australia if it wants to keep fighting. That leave is granted in only a small fraction of applications and turns on whether the case raises a question of general legal importance rather than just the size of the payout.

Without a successful High Court application the payment stands and the matter ends. The corporation could choose to pay promptly and close the file. If it applies for special leave and that bid is refused the timeline ends within months.

This chapter runs until either payment or a final High Court rejection.
