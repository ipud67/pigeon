Trump wants to accelerate AI development and announces 'AI Force'

Translated from Dutch · read the original →
The original headlineTrump wil ontwikkeling van AI versnellen en kondigt ‘AI Force’ aan

The federal government has run AI policy through scattered offices inside the White House, the Pentagon, and the Commerce Department since the first national strategy appeared in 2019. Congress passed laws directing agencies to spend on research and set safety rules, while each administration added its own executive orders on procurement and export controls.

On September 19, 2026, President Trump posted on Truth Social that he will create a dedicated unit called the AI Force to speed up development across government and private industry. He said he will name an AI czar in the coming days to advise him directly on the work.

The plan now turns on how the new unit will sit inside the existing bureaucracy, what spending and hiring powers the president will give it, and which or prior orders it will replace or build on.
