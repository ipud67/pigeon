IHC forms larger bench to hear plea against PTI's Sept 27 protest

The Islamabad High Court has handled on public gatherings and political activity for years. PTI supporters have filed multiple cases there since the party began organising street protests after its founder entered prison in 2023. Those earlier petitions tested rules on assembly permits, police powers to block roads, and the timing of rallies near sensitive sites.

On September 8 the court formed a larger bench to take up a fresh petition filed against the PTI’s announced protest set for September 27. The judges called the case sensitive because it touches on questions of order. They issued notices to the police chiefs of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Punjab and Balochistan plus other officials and scheduled the next hearing for September 10.

The larger bench now holds the petition. Its decision on whether to allow, restrict or postpone the protest will set the immediate legal boundary for the September 27 event and for any similar gatherings that follow.
