MOSCOW
September 20, 2026 · 05:26 UTC

At least two dead in massive drone attack on Moscow and its region

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineAl menos dos muertos en un ataque masivo de drones contra Moscú y su región

Russia and Ukraine have traded long-range strikes on each other's capital regions since the summer of 2024. Both sides use modified commercial drones and occasional cruise missiles to reach cities far behind the front lines in eastern Ukraine. Moscow has built layered air defenses around its urban core and suburbs, yet single drones and small groups have slipped through on several nights in the past year.

Early on September 20, 2026, a wave of Ukrainian drones struck multiple sites in Moscow and the surrounding Moscow Oblast. Russian authorities reported that air defenses intercepted most of the drones, but at least two people died from the impacts or falling debris.

The deaths mark the first confirmed fatalities in the Moscow region from a single drone raid in months. Officials have not released the names of those killed or the exact locations hit inside the capital district. The exchange of deep strikes continues.

Thinking ahead
Two people died in a large drone strike on Moscow and the surrounding area early on September 20. Russian air defenses intercepted most of the incoming drones before they reached the capital. Ukrainian forces have stepped up long-range strikes on Russian rear areas this year as front lines in eastern Ukraine stay locked in place with little ground movement. The pattern shows Ukrainian operators launching bigger groups of drones at night to test Russian detection gaps around the capital. Russian crews have added more electronic jamming and short-range missiles near Moscow since the first strikes hit in 2023. For the pace to slow, either side would need to shift air-defense gear or drone stocks in ways visible on satellite images or supply reports. Next moves likely stay in this cycle: another wave in coming weeks if Ukrainian production holds, matched by Russian patrols tightening over key sites. This round of strikes will probably run at least through winter unless one side visibly runs low on drones or interceptors.
