Virginia Democrat says FBI monitored his calls in corruption probe

The FBI has run public investigations out of its Richmond field office for years. Those probes have used court-authorized wiretaps and other monitoring on state and local officials suspected of trading influence for cash or favors.

On September 3 a Democratic member of the Virginia House of stated in open remarks that agents had recorded his telephone calls as part of one such investigation. The lawmaker identified himself as a target of the probe and said the monitoring captured conversations he held in the course of his official duties.

The statement places the lawmaker on notice inside an active federal inquiry that has already produced subpoenas and witness interviews in Richmond-area government circles. The case now turns on what the recorded calls contain and whether prosecutors can link any statements to violations of federal or honest-services .
