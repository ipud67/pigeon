Ukraine: about ten loud explosions heard in Kyiv

Translated from Italian · read the original →
The original headlineUcraina: udite a Kiev una decina di forti esplosioni

Russian forces have struck Ukrainian cities with missiles and drones since the full-scale invasion began in February 2022. Air-defense sirens sound across the country on most nights when incoming threats are tracked. Kyiv has come under repeated attack during that period, with residents rushing to shelters at the sound of the sirens.

On the night of 16 September 2026 a red alert for missile attack threat went up in the capital. About ten loud explosions were heard in Kyiv shortly after 11:40 p.m. local time.

The city now waits for official confirmation of what struck where and whether any missiles or drones were intercepted. No casualty figures have been released.

Thinking ahead
Explosions were reported in Kyiv after a missile alert sounded on the night of September 16. This fits the pattern of Russian strikes on the capital that have come and gone since the first weeks of the war. Air defenses usually fire back within minutes, debris falls, and officials count impacts once the all-clear is given. The next visible steps will be damage reports from city districts, any confirmed hits on infrastructure, and statements from Ukrainian and Russian commands. How long the current wave lasts depends on whether follow-up salvos are launched tonight or if Russian forces shift to other targets in the coming days. A drop in alert frequency would show only if launch rates from Russian territory fall or Ukrainian intercept numbers rise sharply. The situation stays on this rung until one side visibly changes missile stocks or air-defense coverage. Horizon: days to weeks.
