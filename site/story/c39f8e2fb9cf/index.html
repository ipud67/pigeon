Botafogo signs Moroccan midfielder Hakim Ziyech

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineEl 'Mago' llega a Brasil: Botafogo ficha al marroquí y experimentado volante Hakim Ziyech

Botafogo competed in Brazil's top league and reached the 2025 Copa Libertadores final while building a squad that mixed local talent with experienced foreign players. The club had spent recent transfer windows targeting attackers who could add creativity and European pedigree. Moroccan international Hakim Ziyech spent five years at Chelsea after leaving Ajax in 2020, where he collected a Champions League medal in 2021 and a Club World Cup title the same year.

On Thursday Botafogo announced the signing of the 32-year-old winger on a deal that brings him to Rio de Janeiro. The move ends his time in Europe and places him in a South American league for the first time.

The club now holds a player who has started in major European knockout ties and who adds direct set-piece delivery to its attack. Botafogo's season continues with domestic matches and any remaining continental commitments, while the squad adjusts to the new arrival's style in the weeks ahead.

Thinking ahead
Botafogo signed Moroccan winger Hakim Ziyech on Thursday. The move adds a player with Champions League and Club World Cup medals from his Chelsea days to the Brazilian club’s roster. The next procedural step is his registration with the Brazilian Football Confederation and medical clearance before he can appear in matches. This transfer sits on the ladder of Botafogo’s recent push to bring in experienced foreign talent after their strong 2025 campaign. Ziyech’s contract length and salary details remain undisclosed so far. The situation stays stable unless injury, performance drop, or a bigger European offer pulls him away. The deal looks set to run at least through the 2027 season.
