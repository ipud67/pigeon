Nvidia's board said it will investigate reports that CEO Jensen Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence operation

The board meets in California under standard corporate governance rules that require directors to review any credible claim that could affect leadership or operations.

The board announced the same day it would open an internal review of those reports. Huang agreed to cooperate with the inquiry and will continue as chief executive while it proceeds.

The review now turns on what the board obtains and whether that evidence meets the threshold for any change in executive status under Nvidia’s bylaws.
