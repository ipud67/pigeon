17:T550,Tim Read died on September 19 at age 64 after a battle with metastatic cancer. He had announced his retirement from the Victorian parliament in January and spent his final months as the Member for Brunswick campaigning against the state government's plan to close VicHealth, the world's first health promotion foundation. Read entered politics after years as a GP and researcher focused on HIV prevention and antibiotic-resistant infections, starting with activism in the BUGA-UP group in his 20s, then running for federal and state seats before winning Brunswick in 2018.

His death leaves the Brunswick seat vacant in the Victorian Legislative Assembly. The next procedural step is a by-election, which the Victorian Electoral Commission would schedule within months once the Speaker declares the vacancy. The Greens hold the seat with a strong margin from the 2022 result, so the party would need to select a new candidate and run a campaign focused on the same local health and reform issues Read pushed.

The situation stays quiet until the by-election is called and candidates are named. If turnout drops or another party fields a strong local figure with health credentials, the margin could narrow. Without those shifts, the seat stays with the Greens on current form.
