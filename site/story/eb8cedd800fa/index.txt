Beijing listed the talks as covering the Middle East, Ukraine and the Korean Peninsula while the US released no details.

The pattern of high-level meetings between the two has run since Trump's first term, with a similar tech lineup at the 2017 Mar-a-Lago summit and the 2019 Osaka G20. No trade deal or joint statement came out of this round, matching the 2018-2019 pattern where summits produced pauses but not resolutions on tariffs or technology limits.

The next visible step is Xi's Friday visit to the National Archives followed by his return to Beijing. Further movement would require one side to table concrete steps on Taiwan patrols, export controls or investment screening before the next working-level contact. Without those the cycle of talks and dinners repeats on the same issues.
