Nigerian woman in Massachusetts charged after alleged illegal voting in federal elections

Gladys Adaeze Okafor moved to Massachusetts from Nigeria years ago and filled out voter registration forms that asked whether she was a U.S. citizen. Federal law bars anyone who is not a citizen from registering or voting in elections for president, Congress, or other federal offices. Massachusetts election offices accept the signed forms and add names to the rolls used for both state and federal ballots.

On September 16, 2026, prosecutors charged Okafor in federal court with knowingly submitting false claims on those forms and then casting ballots in federal elections. Court papers say she signed multiple registration cards over time while she remained a non-citizen.
