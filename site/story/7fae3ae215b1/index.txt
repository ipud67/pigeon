The party has run similar street mobilizations before to back President Tshisekedi when it needed to show numbers in the capital and selected provinces.

The pattern holds that these marches stay one-day events unless the party sets a follow-up date or the government blocks them outright. Next step is either a quiet wind-down or another announced action once party leaders meet to weigh the visible head count against the gaps.
