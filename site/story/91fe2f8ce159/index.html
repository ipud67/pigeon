Al-Qadir Trust case: Bushra Bibi moves FCC for early hearing of sentence suspension plea

Bushra Bibi has sat in Rawalpindi’s Adiala jail since January 2025 after a court convicted her and sentenced her to seven years in the Al-Qadir Trust case. The case centers on that public money from a 2019 settlement with the UK’s National Crime Agency was transferred to the trust. Her lawyers filed an appeal seeking suspension of that sentence and she has remained behind bars while the Federal Court considered the request.

On Thursday she lodged an urgent application with the same court asking for the suspension plea to be heard at the earliest date.

The application now rests with the Federal Constitutional Court in Islamabad, which must decide when to list the matter for argument.
