IDF arrests Iran's Press TV journalist in West Bank, hands her to police, source confirms to 'Post'

The US and Israel opened strikes on Iran in late February 2026. A June memorandum lifted the dual blockades and opened a 60-day negotiating window; it collapsed on 8 July and sustained US strikes resumed. The fight has narrowed to control of the Strait of Hormuz.

On September 16 Israeli soldiers stopped a Press TV camera crew at a checkpoint near Bethlehem in the West Bank. They searched the crew, arrested correspondent Naqqa Hamed, and released the cameraman. The IDF transferred her to Israel Police rather than to the Shin Bet.
