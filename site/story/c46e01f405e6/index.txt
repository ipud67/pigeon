24:T570,Eddie Zhi carried dead lambs to the Southland Regional Council car park on Friday after the Environment Court issued interim enforcement orders on 9 September that halted his Tuturau slink skin business from receiving or processing carcasses. The orders followed seven charges against Zhi and NZAgri Development for discharges of blood, leachate and contaminants to land where they could reach water, plus failure to obey abatement notices from August and September 2025. Neighbour complaints about smells and carcass piles triggered the regional council action, with parallel charges and orders from Southland District Council.

The protest is the latest step in a compliance dispute that began with the abatement notices last year. Council staff have locked the office door during visits and declined meetings. Zhi has said he will keep protesting peacefully as his democratic right.

This pattern of public demonstrations and locked doors points to the dispute running at least until the next court appearance. It will continue until Zhi either removes the carcasses and liquids as ordered, disposes of them at approved sites, or the court varies the orders after a full hearing. Without one of those shifts the business stays shut and the charges proceed.

Next likely rung is a scheduled Environment Court date where compliance evidence is tested.
