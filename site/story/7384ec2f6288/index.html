House censures North Carolina Republican over of inappropriate behavior with staffers

House censured Rep. Chuck Edwards (R-NC) on September 1, 2026. The vote was 413-2 after the House Ethics Committee issued a report in August that detailed allegations of inappropriate behavior with female staffers in his office. The censure is the latest formal disciplinary action taken by the House against a sitting member this Congress.

Thinking ahead
The House censured Rep. Chuck Edwards on Tuesday after the Ethics Committee report detailed his alleged inappropriate behavior with female staffers. This fits the pattern of other lawmaker disciplinary votes this Congress, with both parties backing the 413-2 tally. Edwards stays in his seat for now because censure carries no automatic removal or bar from running again. Next steps hinge on whether House leadership or the Ethics panel pushes further measures like a formal expulsion vote, which would need a two-thirds majority to pass. Edwards could also face pressure from North Carolina Republicans to step down or get primaried in 2028. The situation only shifts if new evidence surfaces that prompts another Ethics review or if voters in his district signal a clear rejection at the ballot box. Without those triggers, the censure stands as the endpoint and the story fades. Horizon: 3-6 months until next election cycle pressure.
