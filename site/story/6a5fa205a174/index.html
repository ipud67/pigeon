ATLANTA
November 13, 2026 · 15:06 UTC

Chad Monroe pleaded guilty in Florida to 42 million dollars in Medicare fraud and a PPP loan scheme

Companies must list true owners on enrollment forms and cannot pay or receive kickbacks to generate orders.

Court records show he and others opened three companies—Maksix Medical Products Inc., Orthotech Atlanta LLC, and 1st Choice Medical Supply LLC—that billed Medicare, Medicare Advantage plans, and TRICARE more than $26 million for braces and cancer genetic tests that patients never wanted or received.

The government seized Monroe’s 47-foot yacht valued at over $600,000, a Cadillac Escalade, other vehicles, and more than $1 million in cash. He faces up to 10 years in prison on the count and up to 30 years on the wire-fraud count. A judge will set sentence after reviewing the U.S. Sentencing Guidelines.
