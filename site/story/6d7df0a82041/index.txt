1a:T5ca,The US has blocked Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas from entering New York for the UN General Assembly for the second year running. Last August the State Department first denied visas to 80 Palestinian officials after citing the Palestinian Authority and Palestine Liberation Organization for glorifying terrorism and trying to internationalize the conflict instead of sticking to the Oslo process commitments. Abbas spoke by video link in 2025; the General Assembly is scheduled to vote Thursday on whether the same remote arrangement will be allowed again this year.

The visa denial rests on the same stated grounds as 2025 with no public sign the Palestinian side has met the US conditions for lifting sanctions. The UN Headquarters Agreement requires the host country to grant access to diplomats yet Washington maintains exceptions for foreign policy and security reasons. Palestinian officials continue to reject the accusations and say the move blocks trust-building needed for any two-state talks.

Without a shift in the Palestinian Authority's documented positions or a US policy change the block is likely to hold through this session and the next annual cycle. The immediate procedural step is the Thursday vote on video participation. The situation changes only if the US revokes the sanctions or the Assembly finds another workaround that does not require Abbas's physical presence.
