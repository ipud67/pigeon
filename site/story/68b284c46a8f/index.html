UKRAINE
September 26, 2026 · 07:03 UTC

Ukraine’s Zelensky says he urged Trump to enlist China’s help in ending Russia’s invasion

Russia invaded Ukraine on February 24, 2022, and has held parts of the east and south since the first weeks of the fighting. Kyiv has received weapons, money and diplomatic backing from the United States and its allies while Moscow has drawn support from North Korea, Iran and economic ties with China. Donald Trump took office for a second term as president on January 20, 2025, and has pressed both sides toward a negotiated end to the war.

On September 26, 2026, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said he told Trump during their latest conversation to bring China into the talks as a way to increase pressure on Russia to stop the invasion.

The statement places Beijing at the center of the next round of diplomacy between Washington, Kyiv and Moscow. It leaves open how China would use its trade and political links with Russia to move the conflict toward a settlement.

Thinking ahead
Zelensky told reporters he pressed Trump during their call to bring China into talks aimed at stopping the Russian invasion of Ukraine. The statement comes after months of direct US-Russia contacts and separate Ukrainian-Chinese diplomatic exchanges that produced no cease-fire. Zelensky framed the ask as a practical step to widen the circle of pressure on Moscow, given China's economic ties to Russia and its public calls for a political settlement. This approach is likely to stay on the table for the next several weeks while Trump weighs options ahead of any formal talks. The next visible move would be either a direct US outreach to Beijing on Ukraine or a fresh round of Ukrainian-Chinese meetings to test what concessions China might extract from Russia. The situation would shift only if Russia and Ukraine both accept a third-party mediator with enforcement power or if battlefield positions lock into a line both sides treat as a starting point for talks. Forecast holds until a public Trump-Xi contact or a joint statement on mediation is issued. Horizon: 4-6 weeks.
