IDF strikes Hezbollah targets in Lebanon after Israeli soldiers intercept drone attack

IDF strikes hit Hezbollah targets in Lebanon on September 4 2026. Israeli soldiers intercepted a drone attack earlier that day. The strikes came after Defense Minister Israel Katz announced the IDF had cleared tunnels on Lebanon's Ali al-Taher ridge of Hezbollah fighters.

The US and Israel opened strikes on Iran in late February 2026. A June memorandum lifted the dual blockades and opened a 60-day negotiating window. It collapsed on 8 July and sustained US strikes resumed. The fight has narrowed to control of the Strait of Hormuz. Fighting in Lebanon continued as part of the weekly strike and counter-strike phases that began in March.

Thinking ahead
IDF hit Hezbollah sites in Lebanon hours after troops cleared tunnels on the Ali al-Taher ridge. The move came right after Israeli forces stopped a drone crossing from Lebanon. This fits the pattern that started in March when the Iran fight spread to weekly exchanges in Lebanon alongside the Hormuz naval clashes. The back-and-forth has run at a steady pace since July when the ceasefire collapsed and US strikes picked up again. Hezbollah has kept up cross-border attacks while Israel responds with targeted hits on tunnels, weapons stores, and launch sites. No sign yet of a ground push or major escalation in southern Lebanon. Next steps hinge on whether Hezbollah launches another barrage or if Iran directs a coordinated move through its proxies. The fight stays focused on the strait unless one side commits more forces on the ground in Lebanon. This round likely runs another three to six weeks at current intensity.
