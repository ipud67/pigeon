1b:T467,The Justice Department filed suit today in federal court against Minnesota, Minneapolis, and St. Paul. The complaint targets the state's 30-day waiting period for people who want a permit to buy a handgun or semiautomatic rifle for home use only, plus city rules in Minneapolis and St. Paul that can stretch the total delay close to 60 days. The suit claims these rules block law-abiding citizens from getting firearms for self-defense inside the home.

This is the latest step in a string of federal challenges to state waiting periods that followed the 2022 Bruen decision. The department is asking the court to block the rules while the case moves forward.

The case will now go through motions to dismiss, discovery, and likely cross-motions for summary judgment. How long it runs depends on how fast the judge sets the schedule and whether the state and cities win early dismissal. The situation changes only if the court grants or denies the block, or if the parties reach a settlement.

Next move is the state's answer to the complaint, due in coming weeks.
