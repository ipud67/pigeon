19:T497,A Massachusetts judge declared a mistrial Friday in the Lindsay Clancy case after the jury deadlocked on whether she was criminally responsible for the deaths of her three children. The deadlock came after weeks of testimony focused on her mental state at the time. Prosecutors must now decide whether to retry the case or drop the charges.

The case sits on a ladder of prior steps: indictment in 2023, not-guilty plea tied to insanity defense, months of psychiatric evaluations, and jury selection that screened for views on postpartum mental health. Those steps set the exact point where unanimous agreement on responsibility became the sticking indicator.

How much longer this runs depends on the district attorney's next filing. A retrial would restart jury selection and testimony within months. Dismissal would close it without another verdict. The observable indicator is the prosecutor's formal notice in the coming weeks.

Next move sits with the district attorney weighing the hung jury against the evidence already presented. No outside force shifts that unless new competency issues arise in Clancy's custody status.
