1a:T47c,Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said US and Chinese officials have floated a deal to cut tariffs on noncritical goods worth $30 billion. The statement came one day before President Trump hosts Chinese President Xi Jinping in Washington. Talks center on lowering duties without touching items the two sides flag as critical.

The pattern sits on top of years of tariff hikes between the two governments that started in 2018 and ratcheted up again in 2025. Each round added costs to goods flowing both ways until the current pause on new increases.

No clear signal yet on how far the cuts would go or which exact products count as noncritical. Yield curve data shows the 2s10s spread at 20 basis points and the 10-year Treasury yield at 4.96 percent as of September 21, but those figures do not tie directly to the tariff talks.

This round of talks is likely to run at least through the Xi visit and probably another 30 to 60 days of staff-level bargaining before any signed list appears. Next step is a joint working group to list the $30 billion basket and agree on the new tariff rates.
