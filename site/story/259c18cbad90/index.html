India news: PM Modi leaves for Central Asian tour

Overview
Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi is traveling to Uzbekistan for a two-day state visit, after which he will go to Kyrgyzstan for the SCO Summit. Meanwhile, hundreds of Indians are still missing in Nepal. DW has more.
Short history
PM Modi left India on August 29, 2026 for a two-day state visit to Uzbekistan. From there he heads to Kyrgyzstan for the SCO Summit. The trip is the latest in a series of Central Asian engagements that began after India joined the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation in 2017. Hundreds of Indians remain unaccounted for in Nepal following recent floods there.
Predictive model
PM Modi touched down in Uzbekistan today to start a two-day state visit before heading to Kyrgyzstan for the SCO Summit. This is the latest leg in India’s regular high-level diplomacy with Central Asian neighbors that has run on a steady schedule for years. The trip follows established protocol: bilateral talks in Tashkent, then multilateral sessions in Bishkek with the other SCO members. The tour itself ends in a few days once the summit wraps. No signs yet of any extension or early return. The only visible pressure that could cut it short or shift the agenda would be a sudden escalation around the hundreds of Indians still listed as missing in Nepal, but so far that file has stayed on a separate track. Next visible rung is the joint statements and any new connectivity or security deals expected out of the SCO meetings. If those roll out on time, the pattern simply repeats at the next SCO gathering. Horizon: wraps by early September.
