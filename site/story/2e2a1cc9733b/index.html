Celebrate International Observe the Moon Night with NASA

Humans last sent a crew around the Moon in December 1972. NASA resumed crewed lunar flight in April 2026 when four astronauts completed the Artemis II mission and circled the Moon. That flight marked the first time people had traveled that far since the Apollo era ended more than 50 years earlier.

On September 19 2026 NASA will hold its annual International Observe the Moon Night at the Goddard Space Flight Center Visitor Center in Greenbelt Maryland. The event runs from 6 p.m. to 9 p.m. EDT and will focus on the work that made Artemis II possible along with current lunar research conducted by the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter and other Goddard teams. Attendees can view an Apollo 14 Moon rock use telescopes if weather allows join hands-on activities make artwork take photos at a kiosk and talk with scientists and engineers about ongoing Moon and solar-system missions.

The celebration takes place rain or shine with free parking and no registration required though advance sign-up helps planners. It forms part of a global program that began in 2010 and now includes in-person and virtual activities at sites worldwide.

Thinking ahead
NASA runs an annual Moon Night at Goddard in Greenbelt, Maryland. This year’s event on September 19 follows the April Artemis II crew flight around the Moon, the first in more than 50 years. Visitors get telescope time if weather allows, hands-on activities, an Apollo 14 rock on display, and briefings on the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter and upcoming Artemis steps. The program has run every year since 2010 with events worldwide. Goddard’s version draws local crowds for one evening each fall. No registration is required and it goes ahead rain or shine. Turnout and clear skies will decide how many people actually view the Moon. NASA keeps the same format unless the visitor-center schedule or Artemis flight pace changes. Next rung is the 2027 Moon Night after the next Artemis launch or test milestone. Horizon: one night only, repeats September 2027.
