21:T42c,The WTC Health Program got a petition to add peripheral artery disease to its list of covered conditions for 9/11 responders and survivors. The administrator reviewed the medical literature and the petition materials and found the evidence too thin to move forward. That decision, dated September 15 2026, closes the petition without sending it to the Scientific/Technical Advisory Committee or starting any rulemaking.

This kind of denial has happened on other proposed additions when studies did not show a clear link to the dust cloud or cleanup work. The program has added conditions in the past only after stronger peer-reviewed data appeared or when new petitioner evidence changed the picture.

The next rung would be a fresh petition with different or later-published studies that meet the administrator’s threshold, or independent research that shifts the literature enough for the program to reconsider on its own. Without that, the condition stays off the list.
