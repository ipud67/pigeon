Russia and US envoys meet in US – CNN

Russia has waged war against Ukraine since its forces crossed the border in February 2022. The United States supplied weapons and to Ukraine while imposing on Russia. Donald Trump took office as president in January 2025 and named Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner as special envoys to handle direct talks with Moscow.

On 24 September 2026 Kirill Dmitriev, the Russian official who serves as Putin's envoy on economic and diplomatic matters, met Witkoff and Kushner in New York. The three men discussed possible cooperation between the United States and Russia and steps that could end the fighting in Ukraine. Dmitriev had come to the city to follow up on an earlier meeting the same envoys held with Putin in early September.

The next day US Secretary of State Marco Rubio held a separate talk in New York with Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov on the same subjects. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, also in New York for the United Nations General Assembly, said the upcoming G20 summit in Miami might host a direct meeting that includes Ukraine, the United States and Russia.

Thinking ahead
Putin's envoy Kirill Dmitriev met Trump's envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner in New York on 24 September. They talked about US-Russia cooperation and ways to end the Russia-Ukraine war. This sits one rung down from Putin's early-September meeting with the same Trump team and from the Rubio-Lavrov sit-down the day before on the same war issues. These back-channel talks have run in short bursts since the first Trump-Putin contacts after the US election. Each round ends with both sides saying they will keep talking but no joint statement or signed plan. The next procedural step is another round of envoy-level contacts or a possible Trump-Putin call before any larger meeting that includes Ukraine. The pattern holds as long as both governments keep sending the same small group of personal envoys instead of shifting to foreign-ministry working groups. A visible change would show up if Zelenskyy joins a trilateral table or if the two sides publish even a short list of agreed items. This round of envoy meetings is likely to run another 4-6 weeks before either a pause or an upgrade to higher-level contact.
