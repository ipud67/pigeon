These back-channel talks have run in short bursts since the first Trump-Putin contacts after the US election. Each round ends with both sides saying they will keep talking but no joint statement or signed plan. The pattern holds as long as both governments keep sending the same small group of personal envoys instead of shifting to foreign-ministry working groups. A visible change would show up if Zelenskyy joins a trilateral table or if the two sides publish even a short list of agreed items.
