Records Governing Off-the-Record Communications; Public Notice

The Federal Energy Commission has run a rule since 1999 that bars its staff who decide cases from talking off the record about the merits of any contested proceeding. Under that rule any such contact, if it is written, must be sent to the Secretary of the Commission, and if it is spoken the substance must be summarized in writing. The material then goes into a public non-decisional file kept separate from the official record of the case.

On September 11, 2026, the Commission placed on public inspection a new list of prohibited and exempt off-the-record communications it had received. The notice, identified as FR Doc. 2026-18770, follows the exact steps set out in 18 CFR 385.2201(b) and Order No. 607. Each item on the list can be read on the FERC website through the eLibrary system by entering the docket number.

Prohibited communications stay out of the decisional record unless the Commission itself votes to add them after parties ask for a chance to reply. Exempt communications go straight into the decisional record except when they come from a cooperating agency under the cited environmental rule. The filing gives parties the chance to serve responses on the official service list as required by Rule 2010.

Predictive model

This notice will repeat on the regular schedule as new communications arrive. It stays inside the existing 18 CFR 385.2201 process unless the Commission alters the rule or a party wins a successful motion to expand the decisional record on one item. Next rung comes when parties file responses or the Commission rules on any such requests. Horizon: routine quarterly cycle, no break visible.
