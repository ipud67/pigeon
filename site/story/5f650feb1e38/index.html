An ICE agent shot and wounded a Venezuelan DoorDash driver in Texas, his lawyer told US media

An ICE agent shot and wounded a Venezuelan man in Austin on September 21 2026. ICE has carried out immigration arrests and operations under the authority granted to the Department of Homeland Security by federal that allow agents to detain people suspected of violating immigration law. Those operations increased in the Texas capital in the weeks before the shooting.

The agent fired once and struck Wilber Rafael Garces Perez, 28, in the torso just before 1 p.m. local time. Perez was taken into ICE custody, transported to a hospital, and called his wife from there while in pain. Austin police took no part in the incident. A blue Toyota Corolla parked nearby showed damage to the passenger door. Local officials said they did not know whether Perez had been the target of the ICE action.

Protesters gathered at the scene carrying flags from Mexico and Guatemala and signs calling for ICE to leave the city. Austin Mayor Kirk Watson and Texas Congressman Greg Casar both called for an independent investigation and release of body-camera footage. The city will take part in the probe. The case follows a fatal ICE shooting in Houston months earlier and comes as federal immigration enforcement has expanded across several cities.
