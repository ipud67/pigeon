19:T48a,The Crew-12 mission wraps up after six months on the station. The four-person team of NASA astronauts Jessica Meir and Jack Hathaway, ESA astronaut Sophie Adenot, and Roscosmos cosmonaut Andrey Fedyaev completed experiments on crystal growth for drugs, cold-atom quantum studies, bone scaffolds for osteoporosis, soft-material assembly, and antibiotic-resistant bacteria behavior in microgravity. Their Dragon capsule undocks from the International Space Station on October 2 and splashes down on Earth within hours.

This rotation pattern has run on schedule since the first crewed Dragon flight in 2020. Each crew handoff follows the prior one with no gaps longer than a few weeks. The next change comes only when Crew-13 arrives at the station in late 2026 or early 2027.

The mission ends on time with no hardware faults or crew health issues reported. Ground teams will inspect the returned capsule and review experiment data over the next several months. The station stays occupied by the crew already aboard until the replacement flight docks.

The story closes with splashdown. It runs another 12 to 24 hours.
