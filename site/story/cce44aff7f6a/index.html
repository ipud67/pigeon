WEST BANK
October 18, 2026 · 12:16 UTC

European diplomats attacked by Israeli settlers in West Bank

Israeli settlers have carried out near daily attacks on Palestinians harvesting olives in the West Bank since the US and Israel began strikes on Iran in late February 2026. The fighting narrowed to control of the Strait of Hormuz after a June memorandum that lifted blockades and opened a 60-day negotiating window collapsed on 8 July. US strikes resumed and Iran moved to shut the strait in mid-July.

A delegation of diplomats from Belgium, France, Poland and the Catholic Church drove to the largely Christian village of Taybeh near Ramallah to assess conditions on the ground. Masked settlers confronted their convoy, threw stones, smashed a window on the Belgian consulate car and assaulted a French mayor wearing his tricolor sash. Video from the scene showed the man slumped beside a vehicle.

The Israel Defense Forces condemned the act of violence and opened an investigation. Belgium called for prosecution of those responsible and said it would press for European Union sanctions on the attackers and their supporters. The office of Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu described the incident as part of a coordinated campaign to smear Israel. The foreign minister said the visit had not been coordinated in advance with the military.
