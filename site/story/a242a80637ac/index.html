Maastricht University in the Netherlands suspended a Palestinian student who claimed involvement in the Oct. 7 attacks

Maastricht University in the Netherlands began hosting more than 40 students and workers from Gaza this summer after the Dutch government issued them residence visas. The program let the participants study or take jobs at Dutch schools. The university had not examined the background of each arrival beyond the visa process.

On September 28 the university barred one Palestinian student from its buildings, classes and exams. The step followed a video that spread on social media in which the student watched a Qatar television report on the October 7 2023 Hamas attack on Israel, pointed at the screen, gave a thumbs-up and said “7 October. This is me.” A university spokesman told the campus outlet Observant Online that the measure came from concern for the safety of the student, other students and staff and was not meant as punishment. The school will speak with the student but is not investigating the video or his statement.

The suspension now rests on talks between the university and the student. A lawmaker from the JA21 party has called for the student’s deportation to Israel for trial and has questioned the screening that allowed him into the country.
