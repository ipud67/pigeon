Islamabad ATC indicts Imaan, Hadi in security breach case despite objection over incomplete legal formalities

ISLAMABAD: An anti-terrorism court (ATC) on Tuesday formally lawyers Imaan Zainab Mazari-Hazir and Hadi Ali Chattha in a case pertaining to the disruption of the security route of an international cricket team and to public unrest. Judge Tahir Abbas Sipra over the proceedings, during which both accused were produced through video link. At the outset, Hadi objected to the framing of charges, stating that he had not yet been provided copies of the case documents. He argued that charges could not be framed before completion of all legal formalities.
