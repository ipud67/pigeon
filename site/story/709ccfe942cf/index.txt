This specific protest run looks likely to stay ended as long as the aid package holds and fuel supply does not tighten further. The broader Hormuz standoff shows no fresh closure moves since the UAE cut trade with Iran on August 19. Next steps hinge on whether tanker traffic stays low or US strikes hit more maritime targets.
