US agency won't comment on ongoing probes, after its head said at GOP midterm event that Ilhan Omar married her brother

The Department of Homeland Security has maintained a policy for decades that it does not discuss active investigations. That approach dates to long before the current took office in January 2025 and has applied to referrals on immigration and matters alike.

On September 11 a spokesman for the agency told JNS that no one including members of Congress stands above the law. The statement came after the DHS secretary told a Republican midterm gathering that Representative Ilhan Omar had married her brother. The agency has so far declined to confirm or deny whether any formal probe exists.

The exchange leaves the department's standard refusal in place while the secretary's public comment stands on the record. Any further action now rests on whether investigators inside the agency treat the as a referral that requires review under existing immigration statutes.
