Home invasion in Mar del Plata: five hooded men stole $20 million from a family

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineEntradera en Mar del Plata: cinco hombres encapuchados robaron $20 millones a una familia

Five men forced their way into a house on Matheu Street in the Divino Rostro neighborhood of Mar del Plata on the morning of September 23. The neighborhood sits in a residential stretch of the coastal city where families keep large amounts of cash and foreign currency at home. The men wore hoods and carried out the theft without firing shots or injuring anyone inside.

They left with the equivalent of 20 million pesos in local currency plus an unspecified amount of U.S. dollars. The group loaded the money into a car that had no license plates and drove away.

Police are searching for the vehicle and the five men. The family reported the exact amount taken once officers arrived at the scene.
