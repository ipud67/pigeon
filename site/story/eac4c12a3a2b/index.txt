20:T4b5,The FAA put out an advance notice of proposed rulemaking on September 14 2026 to gather data on moving firefighters by restricted category aircraft for ground wildfire work. This step follows the 2024 FAA Reauthorization Act that ordered the change to sections 91.313 and 119.1. The notice asks for comments on current transport resources, what blocks or helps that transport, and how costs stack up against benefits.

The process stays in the comment and data collection phase until the docket closes. FAA will then review submissions to shape a formal proposed rule. The timeline stretches as long as it takes the agency to digest the input and draft the next document; past rules of this type have taken 12 to 24 months from ANPRM to NPRM.

If comment volume stays low or shows split views on safety and cost, the FAA could extend the review or scale back the amendment. Strong agreement on workable limits could speed a narrower rule forward. Without enough clear data on operational need and aircraft availability, the agency has already flagged it may stall.

Next rung comes when the FAA either publishes a proposed rule or announces it needs more time.
