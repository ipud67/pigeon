1e:T5ab,The executive order signed September 17 2026 directs federal agencies to treat recreational saltwater fishing as a core part of ocean and coastal decisions. It builds on two earlier orders: one from July 3 2025 that set up the Make America Beautiful Again Commission and one from April 17 2025 that focused on seafood competitiveness. The new order tells the Secretary of Commerce to review National Standard Guidelines under the Magnuson-Stevens Act inside 180 days, with specific attention to Optimum Yield and Best Scientific Information Available rules so they weigh recreational economic and social factors and pull in state data faster.

Agencies must now partner with states and private operators to rebuild data systems, drop access limits that hit charter boats and coastal towns, and shift from old sampling methods that produce late or inaccurate catch numbers. The order stops at directing reviews and partnerships; actual rule changes still require the standard public-comment and approval steps under the Magnuson-Stevens Act.

This posture points to continued federal action through at least March 2027 while the Commerce review runs and agencies start data upgrades. Next visible steps will be the Secretary’s report on guideline changes plus any new state-federal data agreements that follow. The track changes only if courts block a revised guideline or Congress alters the underlying statute.
