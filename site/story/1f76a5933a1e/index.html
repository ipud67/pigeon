An 88-year-old Canberra man in Australia is likely to spend life in custody after he fatally stabbed his 78-year-old wife in 2024 and was found unfit to plead to murder

Canberra man Manfred Uhle, 88, was charged with murder after repeatedly stabbing his 78-year-old wife in 2024, but was found unfit to plead to the charge during a special court hearing.
