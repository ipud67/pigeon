17:T5ca,South Korea and the US wrapped up three days of senior defense talks in Busan this week and put out a joint statement Friday saying they will step up work to speed the handover of wartime operational control from US to South Korean command. The 29th Korea-US Integrated Defense Dialogue reviewed the conditions-based plan first set in 2014, which requires Seoul to build lead command ability, strike and air defense capacity, and a stable regional security setup before the switch. This round fits the current Seoul administration’s stated goal of completing the transfer by 2030.

The process has moved in fits and starts since the 2014 agreement, with repeated joint assessments and follow-on steps tied to each year’s Security Consultative Meeting. The latest meeting also included site visits to shipbuilding and aviation maintenance facilities to tighten industrial ties that support the military transition.

How much longer it runs will track against the three standing conditions and the pace of South Korean capability upgrades already under way. Next visible steps are further consultations laid out in the joint statement plus the next scheduled defense ministerial meeting where progress gets measured again. If capability benchmarks stay on the current schedule and the regional security environment holds, the formal OPCON shift could reach a decision point inside the current presidential term.
