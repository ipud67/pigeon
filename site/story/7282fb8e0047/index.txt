The pattern of former captives speaking out has run at a steady pace since the first releases in late 2023. It will likely continue as long as new books or interviews are scheduled. Next step is more of the same: additional survivors may publish or testify when their own timing allows. The situation shifts only when a fresh hostage deal or ground operation produces new releases or new captures.
