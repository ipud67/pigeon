It also flags rising attacks on nuclear sites, faster proliferation drivers, and an approaching wave of new nuclear-power projects that all need tighter safeguards. These items match the agenda the Agency has carried forward from earlier sessions on Syria safeguards and Ukraine plant safety. The conference runs through the week; outcomes will show up first in the resolutions Member States adopt and in any new agreements on funding or verification access.

This pattern of annual warnings plus procedural calls for cooperation has repeated for at least the last decade without a sharp break. Unless one of the named flashpoints produces a verified breach or a sudden funding collapse, the session will close with standard language and the next regular board meeting in November. Observable indicators are the resolution texts, attendance at side events on Iran and Zaporizhzhia, and whether the Technical Cooperation Fund receives new pledges by Friday.
