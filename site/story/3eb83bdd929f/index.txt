The conference runs through the week; outcomes will show up first in the resolutions Member States adopt and in any new agreements on funding or verification access.

Observable indicators are the resolution texts, attendance at side events on Iran and Zaporizhzhia, and whether the Technical Cooperation Fund receives new pledges by Friday.
