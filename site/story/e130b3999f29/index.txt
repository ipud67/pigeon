17:T468,The White House issued an executive document on September 3 2026 that sets up the United States Space Academy. This marks the first formal step after earlier agency-level proposals for a dedicated training center for space operations and personnel. The next concrete rung is the appointment of an academy director and the release of an implementation plan that lays out location, curriculum, and initial enrollment targets.

How long this runs depends on how fast Congress funds the bricks-and-mortar site and staff. If lawmakers add money in the next defense spending bill, the academy could open classrooms inside 18 to 24 months. Without that appropriation the project stays on paper and the timeline stretches past 2028. Observable indicators are the line item in the fiscal 2027 budget request and any follow-on presidential directive that names a launch date.

The situation changes only when Congress passes and the president signs dedicated funding or when a later executive order cancels the academy outright.
