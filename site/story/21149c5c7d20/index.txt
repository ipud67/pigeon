Chanel pieces and Chrome Hearts jewellery also pulled multiple bids each and cleared several times above their low estimates.

This sits on the ladder that began with the 2023 seizures tied to the laundering ring and moved through the September 17 property auction that drew zero buyers. The next procedural rung is the second tranche of 286 pieces of fine jewellery, which stays open until September 27. The two sales together were projected to pull S$2.9 million to S$3.9 million before buyer’s premium.

Strong bidding on small portable items points to continued interest in the remaining jewellery lots. If the September 27 close matches or beats the handbag totals, the full auction run will finish above the low end of the combined estimate. Thin data on bidder profiles and post-premium net proceeds leaves the exact final haul uncertain.
