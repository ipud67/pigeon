To kick off the G20 Trade Ministerial, Ambassador Greer joined the G20 Trade Ministers for a welcome reception hosted by Business Roundtable and National Association of Manufacturers, whose advocacy continues to support American businesses and expand opportunities for American workers. Under President Trump’s leadership, the U.S. steel industry is undergoing a renaissance on the strength of tariffs, trade deals, favorable tax treatment, improved energy policy, and the American entrepreneurial spirit. On the sidelines of G20 Trade Ministerial, Ambassador Greer chaired the Ministerial Meeting of the Global Forum on Steel Excess Capacity, which reached consensus on a comprehensive framework for joint action (“the Milwaukee Framework”). Walking the factory floor with the G20 Trade Ministers, Ambassador Greer toured Rockwell Automation and met with blue-collar workers and industry leaders to discuss how President Trump’s trade policies are supporting jobs and strengthening our supply chain resilience. Watch Ambassador Greer’s interview with Fox Business’s Edward Lawrence from Rockwell’s headquarters here. At the Milwaukee Art Museum, Ambassador Greer and Secretary of Agriculture Brooke Rollins hosted a welcome dinner to share a bounty of American-grown food with the G20 Trade Ministers. As a direct result of President Trump’s trade actions, our agricultural trade deficit is trending in the right direction and the trade deals secured are unlocking foreign market access for U.S. agricultural exports. To conclude the ministerial meetings about the future of trade, Secretary of Transportation Sean Duffy and Harley-Davidson President and CEO Artie Starrs joined Ambassador Greer to tour one of America’s most iconic brands, Harley-Davidson, underscoring the ingenuity of American innovation.
