Israeli-led firm developing AI-guided cardiac sonography raises $24 million to expand in US market

An Israeli company built an AI system that guides ultrasound probes to produce diagnostic cardiac images even when the operator lacks specialized training. UltraSight received FDA clearance for its Echosystem platform and placed it in more than ten commercial intensive-care sites.

On 23 September 2026 the company announced it raised 24 million dollars in Series B2 financing led by Alive HealthTech Growth Fund with participation from Deep Insight, Star51 Capital, Connecticut Innovations, eHealth Ventures, Mayo Clinic Ventures, NYU, and Iron Nation. The round will fund expansion into the US market and development of new workflows for intensive-care units and circulatory-support settings.

The fresh capital lets UltraSight scale its existing installations, broaden the cleared indications for its AI-guided cardiac sonography, and pursue deeper hospital partnerships while the firm operates inside a market limited by shortages of trained sonographers.
