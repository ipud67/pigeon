MICHIGAN
September 17, 2026 · 21:10 UTC

F-16 from Texas crashes in Michigan, order issued

An F-16 fighter jet from a Texas-based unit flew training missions over northern Michigan for years under Air Force arrangements that placed transient aircraft at civilian airfields when needed.

On September 17 the jet lost power or control during one such flight. The pilot ejected by parachute and reached the ground safely while the aircraft continued on its path and struck the earth in Blair Township near Traverse City.

The impact scattered debris across private land and triggered an immediate evacuation order for nearby homes. Local officials cordoned the site, confirmed the pilot's survival, and began coordination with Air Force recovery teams to secure the wreckage and determine the exact mechanical sequence that brought the jet down.
