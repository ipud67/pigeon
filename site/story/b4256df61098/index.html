Man wounded in 2024 Trump assassination attempt has died

Translated from Dutch · read the original →
The original headlineMan die werd geraakt bij aanslag op Trump in 2024 overleden

The July 13, 2024, rally in Butler, Pennsylvania, drew former President Trump to an open-air stage. A gunman opened fire from a rooftop 130 yards away, striking Trump in the ear, killing one attendee, and wounding two others before security forces shot the gunman dead.

James Copenhaver, 76, one of those two men hit in the arm and abdomen that day, died on September 25, 2026. He had lived with permanent injury from the shooting and walked with a cane in the two years since.

The death leaves one survivor from the original wounded pair still alive. The gunman’s body was cremated earlier this month after an anonymous donor paid for the procedure.

Thinking ahead
The 2024 Butler rally shooting left one spectator dead at the scene and three others hit by gunfire. James Copenhaver, 76, was one of those three. He took rounds to the arm and abdomen, needed a cane afterward, and died on September 25 2026. This raises the confirmed death toll from that specific attack to two. Pennsylvania state police and the FBI have not released any update on the ongoing investigation into the gunman’s planning or any accomplices. Federal prosecutors charged the gunman with attempted assassination of a presidential candidate and related counts. Copenhaver’s death could shift the case toward additional homicide charges if the medical link holds in court filings. No new arrests have been announced. The situation stays frozen until the Justice Department files any superseding indictment or the trial begins. Without fresh evidence or a plea, the procedural path runs through standard federal court scheduling in the Western District of Pennsylvania. Next rung likely appears in a docket entry or DOJ statement within weeks. Horizon: 3 to 6 months until trial or plea.
