Russian forces struck a business center in Odesa, Ukraine, and injured one person

Russian forces have struck cities across Ukraine with missiles and drones since the full-scale invasion began in February 2022. Odesa, a major Black Sea port in southern Ukraine, has come under repeated attack because it handles much of the country’s grain exports and sits within range of Russian naval and air assets in occupied Crimea.

On October 1 2026, a Russian strike hit a business center in Odesa. Oleh Kiper, head of the Odesa Regional Military , said the attack damaged roofs and facades of two buildings, blew out windows, and partly destroyed walls on the fifth floor. One person was injured.

The regional administration has not said what type of weapon struck the site or whether any military targets were nearby. Rescue crews cleared debris and boarded up broken windows while officials structural damage to the buildings.
