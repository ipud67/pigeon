1c:T464,The Trump administration rolled out a visa policy change on September 23 that blocks entry for foreigners whose main reason for coming is to have a baby on U.S. soil and claim birthright citizenship for the child. Secretary of State Marco Rubio announced the tighter screening rules for tourist and other nonimmigrant visas, adding questions and checks aimed at spotting birth tourism plans. This builds on earlier Trump-era efforts from his first term that tried to limit the practice through existing immigration rules.

The restriction sits inside executive authority over visa issuance. It can be tightened further by regulation or loosened by the next administration. Court challenges would need to show the rules violate statute or the Constitution; none have reached that point yet.

The policy will likely stay in place through the current term unless a future president reverses it or a judge blocks enforcement on procedural grounds. Next step is routine State Department updates to consular training and visa forms.
