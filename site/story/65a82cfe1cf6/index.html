Germany news: Police find arms depot near highway

Police in western Germany discovered a large cache of weapons stored in a hidden depot beside a highway in Rhineland-Palatinate on September 15. The find included rifles, pistols and other firearms. Officers removed the arms for examination and began tracing how they reached the site and who placed them there.

The discovery came on the same day that two men, one Eritrean and one German, appeared before federal judges in Karlsruhe after their arrest. Prosecutors charged both with belonging to N'Hamedu Germany, a group formed to overthrow Eritrea's government. The men stand accused of taking part in violent attacks on three community events in Giessen in 2022 and 2023 and in Stuttgart in 2023, during which members treated attacks on police guarding the gatherings as acceptable.

Federal prosecutors now hold the weapons for forensic tests while the court decides on pretrial for the two suspects. The separate trip by Defense Minister Pistorius to Washington for talks with his American ran on its own schedule.
