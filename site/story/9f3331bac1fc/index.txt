19:T40e,The UN General Assembly voted 164-1 with six abstentions on September 4 to drop the Mercator projection in favor of a map that shows Africa and other southern landmasses at their actual scale. The resolution came from African sponsors with French co-sponsorship and marks the first formal UN shift away from the 16th-century Flemish chart that had been standard in its offices and materials. The US cast the lone no vote. 

The change will roll out first in UN publications and training materials. Further adoption depends on whether member states update their own school curricula and official atlases; past map debates have taken years to fade from classrooms even after scientific agreement. No procedural deadline exists in the resolution, so the practical timeline hinges on national education ministries and cartographic suppliers.

This sits outside the Gulf realignment and US-Iran conflict ladders. 

Next visible step is the UN secretariat issuing the replacement maps.
