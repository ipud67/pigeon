22:T482,Oregon lost an appeals court round more than a year ago on the First Amendment claim. The state then signed a stipulated order on September 14 that reinstates Jessica Bates' adoption application and blocks officials from denying it over her stated beliefs on marriage and biological sex. The order also stops any requirement that she use specific pronouns or language on gender identity and bars categorical disqualification based on those views.

The state kept narrow ground to weigh an applicant's limits on meeting a specific child's needs, even if those limits come from religion. Oregon further agreed to pay the prevailing side's legal fees. Similar settlements happened in Vermont this year before its own appeals court issued a ruling.

This case now sits closed by consent judgment. Future change would need either a new state policy that tests the order's boundaries and draws fresh litigation or a higher court reversing the underlying appeals precedent. Without one of those observable moves the judgment locks the current terms in place.
