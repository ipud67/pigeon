1a:T419,The Trump administration extended TPS for El Salvador on September 10 while it ended the program for 13 other countries. This keeps legal work and deportation protections in place for about 250,000 Salvadorans already here. The decision follows earlier reviews that cut TPS for Nicaragua, Honduras, and others in the first Trump term and again this cycle.

DHS has not set a new expiration date or start a fresh review process. The extension holds until officials finish country-condition checks on crime rates, gang activity, and earthquake recovery in El Salvador. A lapse would require the secretary to publish a formal notice ending the designation and giving 60 days notice.

The next clear step is a formal DHS decision on whether to terminate or redesignate TPS for El Salvador, expected in the next review cycle. Indicators pointing to change would be a published Federal Register notice citing improved conditions or a court order blocking any termination.
