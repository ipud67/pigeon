High Court declares Community Groups Registration Act unconstitutional

Kenya's 2010 constitution required parliament to conduct meaningful public participation before passing any new law. Parliament passed the Community Groups Registration Act in 2022 anyway. The law gave the registrar of societies power to register, refuse, or deregister community-based groups and required those groups to submit annual reports on membership and finances.

A went to the High Court and argued the law had been without the required public input. On September 10, 2026, Justice Lawrence Mugambi heard the case and struck down the entire .

The Act no longer exists. Community groups that operated under its registration rules now fall back on older legal frameworks until parliament drafts a replacement that meets the test for public participation.
