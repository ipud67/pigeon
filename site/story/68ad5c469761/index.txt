1d:T495,A federal judge in Boston blocked the Trump administration's rule that would have capped stays for foreign students on F visas and exchange visitors on J visas at four years and set an initial 240-day limit for most journalists on I visas with extensions of up to 240 days. The order came one day before the Department of Homeland Security planned to start enforcement in September 2026. The rule grew out of the administration's July announcement aimed at what it described as visa abuse among students, exchange visitors, and media.

The block rests on a single court filing and will hold until the government either wins on appeal or the case settles. Next step is almost certainly a notice of appeal to the 1st Circuit, where briefing and oral arguments would stretch into 2027 unless the parties move faster. The administration could also rewrite the rule to address the judge's stated defects, which would restart the clock on public comment and legal challenges.

Indicators are thin beyond the existence of the injunction and the prior July announcement. No public signals yet on whether the government will appeal or revise.
