21:T408,Senator Grassley sent a public message to President Trump on September 20 urging an embargo on diesel exports. The call mirrors 1970s actions where presidents blocked farm product shipments to hold down domestic food costs. Grassley represents Iowa, a farm-heavy state where diesel prices hit farmers hard.

The next procedural step sits with the executive branch. Trump would need to direct the Commerce Department or Energy Department to restrict exports under existing trade authority. Congress could pass a law to force the embargo or block it, but that takes votes in both chambers and a signature or veto override.

The situation changes only if the White House acts on the request, ignores it, or faces enough pressure from other farm-state senators and House members to move. Without visible follow-through from Trump or a surge in allied calls, the public urging stays a one-off signal.
