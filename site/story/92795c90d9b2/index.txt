21:T56f,The UN Independent International Fact-Finding Mission on Iran, set up by the Human Rights Council in 2022, released its latest report Thursday. It says it found reasonable grounds that two US airstrikes on February 28 hit a school in Minab and a sports center in Lamerd, both in Iran, and that the strikes were indiscriminate. Iranian officials put the Minab school toll at over 150 dead, including about 120 children; the Lamerd strike killed and wounded 22 civilians. The mission also listed crimes against humanity by the Iranian government in its crackdown on unrest that started in late December.

The report goes to the 47-member Human Rights Council on Monday. It carries no legal force but adds to records that could feed future cases at international courts. The Pentagon said its own probe continues and has no comment yet. The White House called the findings unserious and pointed to Iranian actions instead.

This round of UN documentation on the war that began in February will likely run for months more as the mission keeps collecting evidence and the council debates it. Next step is the Monday presentation, then possible referral to other UN bodies or courts if enough member states push it. The file grows only if fresh witness statements or satellite data come in; without that the report sits as one more entry in the stack.
