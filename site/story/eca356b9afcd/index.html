Senate ratifies Julio Velarde to head the BCR for another five years

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineSenado ratifica a Julio Velarde al frente del BCR por otros cinco años

The Central Reserve Bank of Peru has operated under a board president appointed by the president and ratified by the for a fixed five-year constitutional term since the return to in 2001. Julio Velarde first took the post in 2007 and has held it through three prior , each time steering the bank through shifts in policy and commodity prices that have defined Peru's economy for two decades.

On September 17 the Senate plenary passed the legislative resolution that ratified Velarde for a fourth consecutive term.

The vote extends his leadership of the board until 2031 and keeps in place the monetary framework the bank has followed without interruption since his first confirmation.
