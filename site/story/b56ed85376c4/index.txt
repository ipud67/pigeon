The grant slots into an existing pattern of Beijing stepping in after Pacific island disasters with earmarked project money rather than loose cash.

The money is already assigned to specific purchases and field operations, so spending will start inside weeks once procurement clears. Napat’s office says it is coordinating with line agencies on the ground, which points to the next visible step being arrival of the drilling rig and first relief shipments on Ambae plus updated tallies from the ferry search. Further grants or joint projects would require another formal handover and public statement from either side.
