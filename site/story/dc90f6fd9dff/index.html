Malaysia’s ex-PM Najib remains in prison pending $15.6m payment

Najib Razak has served time in Kajang Prison since August 23 2022 after a court convicted him of misappropriating 42 million ringgit from SRC International and sentenced him to a term that included a 50 million ringgit fine. The formed part of the long-running 1MDB graft cases that began with investigations in 2015 and produced multiple charges against the former prime minister. A board that reports to the king reviews applications for early release or sentence changes once a prisoner has served a minimum portion of the term.

On September 18 2026 the Federal Territories Pardons Board chaired by Sultan Ibrahim Sultan Iskandar granted Najib a conditional pardon that substituted house arrest for the remainder of his prison term once he meets the attached requirements. The board set a payment deadline on the full 50 million ringgit fine before any transfer from Kajang could occur.

Najib stays inside the prison while his lawyers examine the pardon text and the practical steps needed to lift government freezing orders on his listed assets bank accounts and properties. The statement from his firm Shafee & Co said the payment condition rests partly on assets not under his direct control and that he is taking further legal advice on how to satisfy it.
