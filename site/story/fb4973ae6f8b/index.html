Netanyahu accuses Mamdani of ‘supporting’ Hamas ahead of UN visit: ‘Shame on you’

Israeli and US strikes on Iran that began in late February 2026 settled into weekly exchanges after a June memorandum lifted the dual blockades on the Strait of Hormuz and opened a 60-day negotiating window. That window collapsed on 8 July, the US resumed sustained nightly strikes, and the fight narrowed to control of the strait itself.

On 22 September 2026, ahead of a United Nations General Assembly session in New York, Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu posted a video on X that accused the city's mayor, Zohran Mamdani, of supporting Hamas and ended with the words "Shame on you, Mr. Mamdani."

The exchange comes while US and Israeli forces continue operations against Iranian and logistics targets in and near the strait, with Iran maintaining efforts to restrict tanker traffic that bypasses its approved routes.
