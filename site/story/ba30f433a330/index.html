Social Adult Day Care Program Director Pleads Guilty to $127M Medicare and Medicaid Fraud Scheme

Daniel Lee, 57, of Flushing, served as program director at two such centers in the same borough, Happy Life Inc. and Royal Adult Day Care Center.

Court records show that for roughly ten years the centers paid cash kickbacks to beneficiaries to sign up, then billed the programs for day care services never furnished, including on dates when recipients were documented as hospitalized. A related pharmacy submitted claims for medically unnecessary drugs after similar payments to beneficiaries. Agents recovered more than $100,000 in cash from one center and seized about $900,000 in proceeds from bank accounts.

Lee now awaits sentencing on March 2, 2027. The maximum term on the counts is 30 years. The case was brought by the Health Care Section of the Justice Department’s newly created Fraud Division and the Eastern District of New York.

Daniel Lee, program director at two Flushing adult day care centers, entered a guilty plea Monday to conspiracy to commit health care fraud and conspiracy to commit money laundering. The scheme ran for roughly ten years and involved cash kickbacks to patients to sign up plus bills for services and drugs that were never delivered, including claims filed while patients were in the hospital. Agents seized over $100,000 in cash from one center and about $900,000 in related bank accounts. Sentencing is set for March 2, 2027. The statutory maximum is 30 years but the actual term will be set by the judge after review of the sentencing guidelines and other factors listed in the plea. The case stays on the calendar until that hearing. It moves forward only if the judge accepts the plea terms and sets a final loss amount; any appeal or new evidence on additional participants could push the timeline out.
