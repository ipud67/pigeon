Pakistan arrested Faheem Akram whom the US sought in a 126 million dollar postage fraud case

Pakistan's Federal Investigation Agency runs a cybercrime wing that pursues online fraud cases with international reach. A Florida-based grand jury returned an against a 33-year-old man from Khanewal last month on one count of to the United States and to make and sell postage stamps, five counts of making and selling counterfeit postage stamp labels, and four counts of wire fraud.

Officers shut down the site.

The arrest hands US prosecutors a defendant who now faces a maximum of five years on the conspiracy and counterfeit counts and twenty years on each wire fraud count if convicted in Florida federal court. The case turns on whether Pakistan will extradite him to stand trial.
