Quarter of parliamentary questions partly written with AI, number is rising

Translated from Dutch · read the original →
The original headlineKwart Kamervragen met hulp van AI geschreven, aantal neemt toe

Dutch members of parliament have asked written questions of government ministers since the 1840s. The questions arrive as formal documents that set out the facts the MP wants examined and the exact replies required. Staff draft most of them, though the final text carries the signature of the elected representative.

On 14 September 2026 NOS published its review of 2,500 such documents filed over recent years. The outlet found that at least one quarter of the papers contained passages generated at least in part by large language models such as ChatGPT or Claude. Some documents showed only a handful of AI sentences; others carried complete numbered lists produced by the tools.

The proportion of documents that include AI text has grown steadily across the period examined. The research stopped at the latest available filings, so the exact share in the current session remains unknown.
