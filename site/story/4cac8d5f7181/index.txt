The claims target the Department of Homeland Security, ICE, Customs and Border Protection, the FBI, and the Departments of Justice and Labor. DHS has described the operation as part of a criminal probe into unlawful employment practices and said it remains focused on accountability under existing law. The process now sits with those agencies, which must respond or allow the claims to proceed to court. No court filings have been entered yet, and Hyundai has not commented.

This legal track will likely run for months while agencies review the paperwork and decide on responses. It moves forward only if the claims clear administrative hurdles and reach actual lawsuits. The situation stays on this path unless the agencies settle, the workers drop the claims, or a court sets a schedule that forces quicker action.
