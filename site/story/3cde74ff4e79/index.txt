1a:T4e0,The National Environmental Policy Act of 1969 required federal agencies to prepare environmental impact statements for major actions. In 2020 the Council on Environmental Quality issued new implementing regulations that narrowed the scope of reviews. Congress repealed those CEQ regulations in the Fiscal Responsibility Act of 2023 and passed the BUILDER Act, which set new deadlines, limited the range of alternatives agencies must study, and capped the length of environmental documents. The 2021 Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act added further rules on concurrent reviews and lead-agency authority. The Supreme Court’s 2025 decision in Seven County Infrastructure Coalition clarified that agencies do not need to study effects beyond the project’s immediate geographic and temporal footprint.

On July 3, 2025 the Federal Highway Administration, Federal Railroad Administration, and Federal Transit Administration published an interim final rule that updated their own NEPA procedures to match the new statute and court ruling. The rule gave the public 30 days to comment.
