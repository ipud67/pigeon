The current alignment holds as long as Democratic leadership keeps the caucus behind the present-or-table strategy and Green lacks the 218 votes for discharge. A shift would show in a spike of co-sponsors on a new filing or a rules vote that breaks the present bloc.

This episode sits on the ladder of earlier House attempts that reached the floor only to be tabled by cross-party majorities when investigative steps were absent.
