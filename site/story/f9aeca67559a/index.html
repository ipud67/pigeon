3 stabbed at New York college; 2 suspects in custody

Stabbings have occurred on U.S. college campuses for years, often involving disputes between individuals that turn violent in public spaces. New York state law treats such attacks as under the code for assault with a deadly weapon, giving local police and campus security the power to detain suspects on scene and hold them for in county court.

On September 16, three people suffered stab wounds at SUNY Westchester Community College in Valhalla during an incident on the grounds north of New York City. Officers took two suspects into at the site before they could leave the area.

The three victims received treatment for their injuries. The two suspects remained in police custody pending formal charges.
