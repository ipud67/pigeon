PAKISTAN
September 11, 2026 · 13:06 UTC

PMD forecasts fresh spell of rain across country from Sept 12-17

Pakistan has tracked monsoon rains and westerly weather systems each summer since the rains returned in force after the 2010 floods. The Pakistan Meteorological Department releases daily updates on moisture currents moving in from the Arabian Sea and disturbances crossing from the west. Those updates set the window for when rain is expected in the northern hills and then push south across the plains.

On September 11 the department stated that fresh monsoon currents had begun entering the upper parts of the country. A westerly wave is set to follow over the weekend and reach peak strength on September 14. The forecast covers the period from September 12 to 17 and includes a drop in daytime temperatures across the affected zones.

The new moisture will move through the upper regions first and spread southward while the westerly wave remains active. The forecast now turns on the exact track and strength of that wave as it crosses the country through the middle of next week.

Predictive model
PMD issued the forecast on September 11 for rain to start the next day and run through September 17. The department cited monsoon currents already moving into upper Pakistan plus a westerly wave expected to arrive over the weekend and peak around September 14. That sets the current expected window at six days of wet weather with cooler temperatures. The spell ends when the wave moves out and currents weaken, which the forecast pins to September 18 onward unless a new system builds. Next observable step is daily updates from PMD that either confirm the rain on schedule or adjust the end date if the wave stalls. Likely to run the full six days as stated.
