Lithuania police open hate-incitement probe after pig’s head displayed at Kaunas mosque

Lithuania has a small Muslim community that uses a mosque in Kaunas for Friday prayers. Police there handle public assemblies under rules that limit noise, slogans and objects at such gatherings. On September 11 officers opened cases after reports of a biker group that gathered in Ramybės Park next to the mosque while prayers took place.

On September 15 Kaunas County Police upgraded the case to a pretrial criminal investigation for possible against a group and interference with religious rites. Officers reviewed video that showed the bikers playing loud music, revving engines, chanting nationalist slogans and holding up a pig’s head they had carried to the site. No physical contact occurred between the groups and no one required medical help.

The investigation has identified the person who held the pig’s head and the identities of others in the group but has named no formal suspects yet. Police chose not to intervene on the spot to avoid raising tensions while they recorded the event. The prosecutor’s office will decide on victim status as the file moves forward.
