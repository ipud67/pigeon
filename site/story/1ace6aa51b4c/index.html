Talarico opposes trans athletes in girls’ sports ‘if it endangers safety or fairness’

Texas law since 2021 has barred public school athletes from competing in sports categories that do not match the sex listed on their birth certificates. The measure gave school districts and the University Interscholastic League authority to verify birth records and to reassign any participant whose record showed a different sex at birth. Supporters cited physical differences in strength and speed that appear after puberty. Opponents filed lawsuits claiming the rule violated equal-protection guarantees and Title IX.

On September 10, 2026, Democratic state candidate James Talarico said in an interview that he opposes allowing transgender athletes to compete in girls’ sports when it endangers safety or fairness. He stopped short of a blanket ban and noted that every sport sets its own rules on eligibility and equipment.

The statement places Talarico’s position inside the standing state rather than against it. Campaign officials in both parties now treat the issue as settled law in Texas, and the next test of that law will come in the November election for the seat Talarico is seeking.
