18:T506,College coaches showed up on Capitol Hill on September 14 to back the Protect College Sports Act pushed by Sen. Ted Cruz and Sen. Maria Cantwell. The bill already cleared the Senate Commerce Committee in June after picking up endorsements from 24 conferences, the NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL, their players unions, and the U.S. Olympic Committee. It sets federal rules on NIL pay, transfers, eligibility, athlete health, and scholarships while shielding schools from some antitrust suits and creating a study commission.

The next procedural step is a full Senate vote expected before the end of September. Passage would need 60 votes to clear a filibuster and then House action, where no companion bill has moved yet. Observable indicators are the bipartisan sponsors, the coach turnout, and the list of big-league backers, all of which signal momentum inside the Commerce Committee but thin public tracking of floor support.

Critics on the athlete side keep pointing to gaps on revenue sharing and women’s sports protections, which could stall it if amendments surface. Without a clear whip count or House movement the picture stays procedural.

This stays alive through the current session and likely dies or gets reworked next Congress if it misses the September vote.
