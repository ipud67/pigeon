1b:T44f,The Alien Terrorist Removal Court, set up in 1996, just handed down its first deportation order in 30 years against an Afghan man the Justice Department said plotted an Election Day attack. The order marks the first time the court has completed a full case since it was created. Next step is enforcement of the removal, which now sits with immigration officers unless the man files an appeal that succeeds or the government halts the process.

The court was built for secret proceedings on national-security deportation cases that regular courts cannot handle. This ruling shows the panel can still move a case from filing to final order when the government brings one forward. For the situation to change, either Congress would have to rewrite the statute or the executive branch would have to stop sending cases to the court.

The deportation itself is likely to go forward on the current order. How many more cases reach this point will depend on how often the Justice Department chooses to use the court again.
