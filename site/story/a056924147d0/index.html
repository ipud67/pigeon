'Provocative and blatant violation': Syria condemns Netanyahu's entry into its territory

Israeli forces moved into a buffer zone between Israel and Syria in 1974 under a United Nations arrangement that kept the two armies apart. That zone stayed in place until the government of Bashar al-Assad fell in December 2024. After the fall Israel kept troops in the area and called it a security zone.
