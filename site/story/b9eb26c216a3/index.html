In Taipei Taiwan President Lai Ching-te called for deeper cooperation with Eswatini as he received King Mswati III at a military ceremony on Friday

Taipei, Oct. 9 (CNA) President Lai Ching-te (賴清德) called for deeper cooperation with Eswatini, as he received the country's leader, King Mswati III, at a military ceremony in Taipei Friday.
