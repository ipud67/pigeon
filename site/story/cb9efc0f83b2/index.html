Beni: 28 people arrested during a cordon operation in the Malepe district

Translated from French · read the original →
The original headlineBeni : 28 personnes interpellées lors d’une opération de bouclage dans le quartier Malepe

Security forces in Beni have run regular cordon-and-search operations in North Kivu for years to control movement and seize weapons or suspects in districts hit by armed-group activity. The city sits in the east of the Democratic Republic of Congo where several networks have operated since the early 2000s. Malepe district in the Beu commune has seen repeated break-ins, robberies and shootings over the past months.

On Saturday 19 September 2026 troops sealed off Malepe and moved house to house. They took 28 people into , among them two soldiers and seven women. The sweep was ordered after local reports of rising theft and attacks in that quarter of the city.

The 28 remain in custody while authorities check their identities and any links to the crimes reported in the district. The operation has cleared the immediate streets but leaves open how the forces will hold the ground once the cordon lifts.
