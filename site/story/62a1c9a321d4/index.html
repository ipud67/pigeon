Lilly says Foundayo has captured over 30% of new US patients on oral weight-loss drugs

Eli Lilly brought the first pill version of its weight-loss drug to the US market in 2024 after regulators approved orforglipron for daily oral use. The company already sold the injected form under the name Zepbound and held a lead position against rival Novo Nordisk whose injected Wegovy had arrived earlier. Both drugs belong to the GLP-1 class that slows digestion and reduces hunger.

On September 14 2026 Lilly reported that Foundayo a new oral competitor from another developer had taken more than 30 percent of all new US patients starting oral weight-loss medicines in recent months.

The shift leaves Lilly evaluating adjustments to its own oral rollout and pricing while the overall market for these treatments continues to expand.

Thinking ahead
Lilly reported that Foundayo has taken over 30 percent of new US patients starting oral weight-loss drugs. The figure comes straight from the company's own update on market share in the oral segment. No court filings, regulatory filings, or competitor counters have surfaced yet to test the claim. This share level has held steady enough in recent quarters that Lilly keeps citing it in earnings calls. Next move likely sits with Foundayo's sales data in the coming quarter or with payer formulary decisions that could shift patient flow. The number would change only if Lilly revises it downward in a later report or if a rival posts verified script data that shows a different split. The situation runs at least through the next earnings cycle.
