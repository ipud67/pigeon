24:T419,LA County Superior Court is offering full-time staff with five or more years on the job a $35,000 one-time payment to resign, with the window opening September 21. The move comes as county officials face a budget gap that has already forced cuts elsewhere in operations. No prior buyout program of this size appears in county court records for at least the past decade.

The offer targets experienced workers whose salaries and benefits make up a large share of the court’s fixed costs. Acceptance rates will show up in headcount reports due later this year. If enough staff take the deal, the county gains immediate payroll relief but loses institutional knowledge that takes years to replace through new hires.

Treasury yields show no direct read on local court budgets, so none are used here.

This runs at least through the first quarter of 2027 while the county closes its current gap. Next measurable step is the resignation tally and any follow-on hiring freeze or further cuts announced in the spring budget cycle.
