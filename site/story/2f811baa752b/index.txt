City Hall states the timeline matches what happened under prior mayors, with Bill de Blasio receiving his clearance nearly two years in. Multiple senior officials at City Hall and the NYPD already hold clearances and continue to receive non-classified briefings on investigations. 

The next concrete step is completion of the background review by federal agencies, after which clearance can be granted or denied based on standard criteria. The situation changes only if the review concludes with approval, or if public pressure or a specific incident forces an earlier decision from the administration. 

This sits on the ladder of Mamdani's public statements about protests during the upcoming UN General Assembly visit by Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu on Sept. 24 and the recent dispute over withheld federal counterterrorism funds.

It will likely drag on for several more months along the normal pace for these reviews.
