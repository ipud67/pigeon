19:T444,More than 800 people from the book trade signed a petition to pull Argentine writer Ariana Harwicz from the CULTUR_ALH festival in Granada after she denied the genocide of Palestine. The petition frames her removal as a stand against antisemitism and censorship. Festival organizers have not yet said what they will do.

The drive sits inside a pattern of open letters in literary circles that pressure venues to drop speakers over statements on Israel and Palestine. Past cases show festivals sometimes cancel the invite, sometimes keep it and add counter-programmes, and sometimes let the event run while the petition circulates. The next visible step is a public reply from the Granada organisers or from Harwicz herself.

How long this particular pressure lasts depends on whether the festival announces a decision and whether either side escalates with more letters or counter-petitions. The situation changes if the festival sticks to its original line-up, if Harwicz withdraws, or if a court or sponsor steps in with a binding rule.
