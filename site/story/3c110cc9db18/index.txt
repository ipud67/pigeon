Russian losses reached about 800 in the latest stage and over 3,000 across both stages according to the corps command. The first stage on 15 September had already cleared 75 square kilometers and taken 10 more. Operation Vivaldi remains active with no announced end date or next target listed.  

The advance sits on open ground north of Pokrovsk where Russian supply lines run east-west along a few roads. Further movement depends on whether the corps can keep its rate of ground gain against whatever Russian reinforcements arrive from the east. If Russian units stay pinned to static positions without fresh manpower or artillery stocks the Ukrainian push can continue at current pace. Thin public data on current Russian reinforcement levels and exact Ukrainian supply status leaves the duration unclear.
