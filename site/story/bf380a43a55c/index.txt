1c:T496,The Security Council voted 15-0 on 11 September 2026 to roll over the Sudan sanctions regime for one month under resolution 2828. This keeps the targeted sanctions, arms embargo, and Panel of Experts in place until early November while members negotiate possible expansion. The one-month window follows the year-long renewal passed in September 2025 and comes amid the war now in its fourth year. US diplomats pushed for wider scope on the embargo and new listings for sexual violence, kidnapping, and attacks on aid workers. Sudan’s delegate rejected any broadening and called the embargo a violation of self-defense rights.

The short rollover signals talks are stuck but not broken. Council members show broad support for change yet some prefer no action at all. That split produced the technical extension instead of a longer deal. No new listings or territorial widening took effect today.

This setup is likely to hold for the next four weeks. After that the Council will either pass an expanded package or roll over again. Observable indicators point to continued deadlock rather than quick agreement.
