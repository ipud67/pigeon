The lawsuit has been active for months with prior motions and filings already on record. This decision simply clears the dismissal hurdle and sets the stage for discovery and summary judgment briefing. How long it runs depends on how fast the parties exchange documents and whether DHS files another procedural challenge.

Next measurable steps are the deadline for DHS to file its answer and the date the judge sets for the first status conference. Settlement talks could surface if the government sees risk in a class-wide ruling on parole or work authorization. A shift would show up first in a joint motion to pause the case or in a clear schedule order from the bench.
