1d:T4b2,The NRC has spent years renewing and tweaking the NUHOMS cask system under CoC 1004. This proposed rule adds Renewed Amendment 19, which green-lights a lighter 61BTH basket built with staggered plates already cleared in Amendment 18 and in the separate EOS casks. The change mainly cuts weight, eases welding, and cleans up wording while keeping the same aging-management rules that kicked in after the last renewal.

Next step is the standard public-comment window on the proposed rule. After comments close, the NRC staff will fold in any fixes and issue a final rule that lists the new amendment in the approved-cask table. Once that final rule is published, utilities can load the updated baskets without extra licensing.

The timeline hinges on how many substantive comments arrive. Thin comment traffic usually means the final rule drops in four to six months. Heavy pushback on the basket design or aging program could stretch it past a year and force another round of review.

If the final rule publishes unchanged, the 61BTH basket enters the approved list and stays there until the next renewal cycle or a new amendment request.
