The Nuclear Commission proposed adding Renewed No. 19 to the TN Americas NUHOMS spent fuel storage cask certificate

“List of approved spent fuel storage casks”
Overview
Proposed Rule. The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is proposing to amend its spent fuel regulations by revising the TN Americas, LLC Standardized NUHOMS[supreg] Horizontal Modular Storage System for Irradiated Nuclear Fuel listing within the "List of approved spent fuel storage casks" to include Renewed Amendment No. 19 to Certificate of Compliance (CoC) No. 1004. The NRC is referring to this amendment as "Renewed Amendment No. 19" because it was submitted after the renewal of the TN Americas, LLC Standardized NUHOMS Horizontal Modular Storage System for Irradiated Nuclear Fuel Certificate of Compliance No. 1004 and, therefore, subject to the Aging Management Program requirements of the renewed certificate. Renewed Amendment No. 19 would amend the certificate of compliance to provide for a 61BTH improved basket design using staggered plates similar the 24PTH Type 3 basket approved in CoC 1004 Amendment 18 and similar to the EOS 37PTH and 89BTH baskets approved in CoC 1042. This would simplify construction, reduce weight and improve fabricability. Additional changes are proposed to address editorial corrections, consistency, and terminology clarifications. (Nuclear Regulatory Commission)
Short history
The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission first listed the TN Americas Standardized NUHOMS Horizontal Modular Storage System for Irradiated Nuclear Fuel as Certificate of Compliance No. 1004 in the approved spent fuel storage casks list in 1995. The agency renewed the CoC in 2020 and required any later changes to follow the renewed certificate’s Aging Management Program rules. On August 26 2026 the NRC published a proposed rule that would add Renewed Amendment No. 19 to the list. That amendment would approve a new 61BTH improved basket design that uses staggered plates like those already allowed in Amendment 18 of the same CoC and in CoC 1042. It would also make editorial corrections, consistency updates, and terminology changes. The rule has not taken final effect.
Predictive model
The NRC has spent years renewing and tweaking the NUHOMS cask system under CoC 1004. This proposed rule adds Renewed Amendment 19, which green-lights a lighter 61BTH basket built with staggered plates already cleared in Amendment 18 and in the separate EOS casks. The change mainly cuts weight, eases welding, and cleans up wording while keeping the same aging-management rules that kicked in after the last renewal. Next step is the standard public-comment window on the proposed rule. After comments close, the NRC staff will fold in any fixes and issue a final rule that lists the new amendment in the approved-cask table. Once that final rule is published, utilities can load the updated baskets without extra licensing. The timeline hinges on how many substantive comments arrive. Thin comment traffic usually means the final rule drops in four to six months. Heavy pushback on the basket design or aging program could stretch it past a year and force another round of review. If the final rule publishes unchanged, the 61BTH basket enters the approved list and stays there until the next renewal cycle or a new amendment request. Horizon: final rule likely by mid-2027 barring major comments.
