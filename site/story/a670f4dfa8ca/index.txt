17:T414,The US has sent an official invite to Putin for the G20 summit set in Miami this December. Secretary of State Marco Rubio confirmed the step on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly on September 23. This fits the pattern of direct leader-to-leader contacts that started with the first Trump term and resumed after the 2024 election.

The invite now sits with the Kremlin. Putin has attended past G20s when the calendar and politics lined up, though he skipped some after 2022. The next visible rung is whether the Kremlin accepts or sends a deputy, a decision usually announced inside two to three weeks of an invite.

The summit itself runs on a fixed December date. No procedural hurdle blocks attendance unless another country files a formal objection under G20 rules or US Secret Service logistics shift. That keeps the current track intact for now.

This looks set to run another eight to ten weeks until the summit opens. It heads next to a Kremlin yes or no, then to travel logistics if accepted.
