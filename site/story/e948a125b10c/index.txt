1a:T45d,The CFTC put out a proposed rule on August 26 2026 that would drop the order-book mandate for permitted transactions on swap execution facilities. This is the latest step in a long-running tweak of SEF rules that started after Dodd-Frank and have been adjusted several times since. Industry comments will come in over the next 60 to 90 days, then the agency staff will review them and decide whether to send a final version to the five commissioners for a vote.

How long this stays in limbo depends on how many comment letters hit the docket and whether the current commission lineup stays intact. If the proposal draws heavy pushback from SEFs or clearinghouses, staff could rewrite chunks and push the final vote into 2027. If comments are light or mostly supportive, a final rule could clear the commission by late spring 2027 and take effect six to nine months later.

Next rung is the end of the public-comment window, followed by any staff revisions. The only thing that would kill the change outright is three commissioners voting no at the final meeting.
