US Treasury Secretary called on Russian minister to end war against Ukraine at G20 meeting

Overview
Scott Bessent told Anton Siluanov the war needs to end and that the US wants a peaceful outcome, his deputy for international affairs said after the G20 talks
Short history
The war in Ukraine began when Russian forces invaded on February 24, 2022. The United States and its allies responded with on Russian banks and officials, plus military aid to Ukraine that reached over $60 billion by 2025. On September 1, 2026, at the G20 meeting, US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told Russian Finance Minister Anton Siluanov that the war must end and that the US wants a peaceful outcome, according to Bessent’s deputy for international affairs.
Predictive model
The US Treasury Secretary's direct call on his Russian counterpart to end the war in Ukraine is the latest rung in a ladder of steady US pressure that has run since early 2022. Scott Bessent delivered the message at the G20 meeting on September 1 2026, with his deputy confirming afterward that Washington still wants a peaceful outcome. No immediate Russian response has been reported. Treasury yields sit at 3.91% on the 3-month bill, 4.34% on the 2-year, 4.75% on the 10-year and 5.25% on the 30-year as of August 31, with the 2s10s spread at 41 basis points. Those levels show no visible move tied to this specific diplomatic exchange. This round of talks is unlikely to end the war soon because neither side has changed its core demands. It may lead to more back-channel contacts or another round of public statements before winter, but only if one government shows a concrete concession first. Indicators remain too thin for any tighter timeline. Horizon estimate: 3-9 months of continued diplomatic prodding with no breakthrough yet visible.
