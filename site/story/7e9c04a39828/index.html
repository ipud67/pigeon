Republicans in Texas honor Charlie Kirk during midterm convention

Republicans in Texas have held midterm conventions every two years since the state party began using them to set platform priorities and rally activists ahead of the next election cycle. The gatherings bring together delegates from across the state to pass resolutions, hear from speakers, and recognize figures in the movement.

On September 10, 2026, organizers at the convention in Texas played a tribute video for Charlie Kirk. The video honored the conservative activist who was assassinated at a Turning Point USA rally in Utah the previous year.

The convention now continues with its scheduled business of adopting the party platform and hearing from current candidates. The tribute marked a pause in the proceedings to recognize Kirk's prior role in conservative organizing.
