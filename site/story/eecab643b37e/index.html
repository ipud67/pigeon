Germany’s government says Bundestag modernizes design law

The original headlineBundestag modernisiert das Designrecht

The Bundestag adopted the federal government's bill to modernize design law on Thursday, 24 September 2026, after a 20-minute debate. The CDU/CSU and SPD voted in favor; the opposition factions AfD, Bündnis 90/Die Grünen and Die Linke . The bill implements binding requirements of an EU directive from October 2024 on the legal protection of designs and makes procedures
