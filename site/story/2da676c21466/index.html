Claudia Sheinbaum and Donald Trump spoke by phone about the trade agreement, US press reveals

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineClaudia Sheinbaum y Donald Trump hablaron por teléfono sobre el acuerdo comercial, destapa prensa de EEUU

Mexico and the United States have operated under a single trade agreement since 2020. The replaced the 1994 NAFTA deal and set rules on tariffs, supply chains, and dispute settlement between the three North American partners. It requires reviews every six years and faces its first formal review in 2026.

On September 16 the Mexican president Claudia Sheinbaum and the US president Donald Trump held a phone call. US news outlets reported the conversation the next day. The two leaders discussed the trade agreement directly.

The call marks the first publicly confirmed contact between the two leaders since Trump took office this year. It leaves the 2026 review schedule on track while both governments prepare their opening positions on changes to the pact.
