1b:T5e9,The US Treasury sanctioned BitBank, a crypto exchange run by Babak Zanjani, along with its software developer Pishtaz Simorgh and three of his associates. Treasury said the platform routed hundreds of millions in payments to the IRGC and to the new Hormuz Safe Marine Services Authority that charges ships for safe passage through the strait. This marks the latest step in the US “Operation Economic Outcast” campaign that has already hit Zanjani’s Zedcex, Zedxion, Dot One conglomerate and related firms in Turkey and the UAE since January.

Zanjani’s network has operated as both open commercial projects and hidden channels for sanctions evasion and IRGC support since he re-emerged in 2025 after his death sentence was commuted. The sanctions tighten the financial side of the narrow fight over Strait of Hormuz control that resumed after the July collapse of the Islamabad Memorandum.

The move adds one more layer of pressure on the payment infrastructure Iran uses to monetize tanker transits. Further designations of similar digital-asset nodes or front companies remain the procedural next step unless Iran and the US reach a new agreement that reopens verified, unblocked shipping lanes through the strait. No such talks are on record.

This sanctions effort is likely to continue for months. Next it will probably expand to any new exchanges or wallets that replace BitBank in the Hormuz payment chain.
