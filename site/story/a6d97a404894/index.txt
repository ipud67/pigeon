Iran’s own exports stay below the pre-war average because of US policy, while seven more vessel attacks have hit near Hormuz including the Kazimah III tanker struck on October 1.

This pattern has run since the July 12 IRGC push to shut the strait and the collapse of the June Islamabad Memorandum. The shift to bypass routes has held steady for three months now. It will likely continue as long as tanker operators keep the new paths open and Gulf producers maintain the extra pipeline and transfer volumes.

Next moves depend on whether the volume of bypass traffic stays above 40% or if renewed attacks force more ships back through Hormuz. No market figures from the supplied Treasury yields and spreads bear directly on the export recovery.
