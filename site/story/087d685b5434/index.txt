Baku has sent letters to US and EU leaders between late 2023 and early 2025 backing Usmanov as a philanthropist who built ties between the West and the Turkic world. France's foreign ministry called the topic a national security matter and gave no direct comment on Azerbaijan's involvement.

Next step is likely continued quiet talks between Paris and Baku over the detained French nationals, since the leverage claim remains on the table. Any shift would require either the release or trial outcome for the French citizens, or an EU decision on Usmanov's sanctions status that both sides accept.
