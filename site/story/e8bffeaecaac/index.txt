29:T5eb,Russia began voting last month in the parts of Donetsk, Luhansk, Kherson and Zaporizhzhia it seized and declared annexed in September 2022. The balloting for the State Duma, Russia's lower house of parliament, runs through Sunday and marks the first time Moscow has included those districts in a nationwide parliamentary election. Armed soldiers stood watch at some polling stations in the Russian-held section of Donetsk, and officials extended early voting there citing security.

Kyiv has called the process illegal, warned that helping organize it can bring criminal charges, and urged residents to stay away. A coalition of nine Ukrainian human rights groups repeated that call, saying the vote tries to give a legal cover to the occupation.

The vote itself changes no front-line positions. It adds another administrative layer that Russia treats as settled inside its system while Ukraine and most governments treat it as void. The next procedural step is the counting and certification of Duma seats from those districts, which will sit in the new parliament that convenes after the full results are published.

How long this runs depends on who holds the ground. As long as Russian forces keep the districts, Moscow will keep running its elections there. Any shift would require Ukrainian troops to retake and hold the territory or a negotiated line that removes it from Russian control.
