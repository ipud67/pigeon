Police in Smyrna, Georgia, charged an 18-year-old neighbor with murder after they found a dismembered DoorDash driver in garbage bags in a car trunk this week

Police in Smyrna, Georgia, have long responded to reports of missing delivery drivers in the Atlanta suburbs after customers or employers flag late arrivals. DoorDash contracts with independent drivers who use their own cars to deliver food from local restaurants to homes and businesses across Cobb County. Those drivers operate without direct employer oversight once they leave the pickup point.

On September 24 officers searched an apartment complex after a tip and found a woman's dismembered remains sealed in garbage bags inside the trunk of a car. The next day they identified the victim as 33-year-old DoorDash driver Jamilah Lewis, who had been reported missing after she failed to complete a delivery run. On September 26 detectives charged her neighbor, 42-year-old Michael McCann, with murder and with evidence.

The case now rests on forensic tests from the car and the apartment where McCann lived, along with digital records from Lewis's phone that placed her at the complex on the day she disappeared. Cobb County prosecutors must present the evidence to a grand jury before the case can move to trial.
