CANADA
September 30, 2026 · 13:00 UTC

Canada’s government says Prime Minister Carney marked National Day for Truth and Reconciliation with First Nations, Inuit and Metis people in Canada on Thursday

“Nogom wedi e-kijigik Tebwendjageh mamowi kwek ijatcagewin kijigan mamowi Misko-ozawa abagiyan kijigan ka ijinkadek, kiga mokowendjigemin kagi swabwikidimowatc Canada Mehkodowkoneh kinamageh migwamik mekodekone oga kinamage migwam. Kakina abinodjicik agwa wikat kagi pigiwenaganiwatc, acitc kiga widjigabwi-kidowanan ododem-wan ka miwodondjin weni. Ocimec apita minigik awik e-madizitc, ocimec 150,000 Anishnabek First Nations, Tokluk Inuit mamowi Apitwisik, Metis abinodjicik ki pikiwonagini-ok ododemwan mamowi, kidja madjinjowaganitc mekodekone kidja kakina-mowadjin, residential school. Ogimanodagin odinakonagewin kidja kasibiwadjin Anishnaben odicgegewin, otebwetimowin mamowi kakina e-ija madisitc. Federal ogimaetcnodagin kagi kijendik weni kidja inakonigetc, Canada obamiwodon weni nogom minigik kagi nekadjiwadjin anishnaben. Ocimec 6,600 minigik ticiwok kagi pi-nigangabwitc kidja tibadjimotc adi ka ijiwebziwatc, kidja tibadidik minigik misowatc kiabitc nogom e-ija wisgendik anishnabe, ozam kinesh Canada ki abinabi win dic odictcigewin. Kakina e-ija kendik anishnabe otebwetimowin ogi abidjiton kidja nodimandjin Canada ogimanodagin-nin, kidja mokowindjin acitc kidja wabidik adi ka ijitcigetc. Kawin eta kidja mokowendjigewik kimisnamowasi nan anishnabek Kimisnamowanan kegon kidja ijitcige-ik Kida nagon mikan adi ke-ijidabitc weni odimtawini Ogimanodagin mamowi anishnabe ijitchigeh kidowin ndemonanon 94 odja. e-mamowi widjigabwikidowa-ik First Nations Anishnabek, Inuit Tokluk mamowi Metis Apitwisik, ogimanodagin odimta kidja kwek ijitcigetc nogom mickikini-migwamik mamowi kinamage-migwamik, kidja kicka kopi-odik anishnabe ijigejewini mamowi e-ija madizindjin, kidja ojitok tecigotc kidja minowacin nik kewin anishnabe onibim kakina mamowi 0-migwan-nin. Niga nigansidonanan kagi ndendagok kidja nigancimagani-atc ikwe-ok mamowi ikwesisik kagi nisagani-atc, mamowi 2SLGBTQI+ pemadisidjik, mamowi kidja abikik onakonige misinigin United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples Act.
