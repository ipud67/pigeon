Susie Wiles: ‘I am cancer free’

Susie Wiles took the job as White House chief of staff in January 2025 after Donald Trump returned to the presidency. She had held senior roles in his earlier campaigns and White House before that.

On Wednesday she posted on X that her latest pathology report from the Mayo Clinic came back clear. She had announced in the spring that doctors found early-stage breast cancer and she would begin treatment while keeping her duties.

The results mean the cancer that was removed earlier this year has not returned in the tested tissue. Wiles thanked her medical team, family and staff for their support during the process.

Thinking ahead
Susie Wiles announced her early-stage breast cancer diagnosis earlier this year. She posted on X Wednesday that pathology from a Mayo Clinic visit this week came back clear and she is now cancer free. The update marks the end of her reported treatment cycle. This kind of post-treatment clearance usually holds for the first few years if no new symptoms appear. Next step is routine follow-up scans and bloodwork on a schedule set by her doctors. The situation changes only if a future test shows recurrence or she reports new symptoms. The announcement itself ends the active phase of this story. Horizon: quiet for at least 12 months barring new test results.
