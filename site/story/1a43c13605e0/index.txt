The file moves forward only when prosecutors file formal charges; until then the suspects stay in custody under standard Turkish pre-trial rules. No public link has been shown between the money trail and any party to the Hormuz blockade.  

The situation stays inside the Turkish justice system until either a Turkish court convicts or an extradition request is granted. If the money trail leads to accounts in third countries, those states would have to open their own probes for the case to widen.
