GLOUCESTERSHIRE
September 27, 2026 · 21:36 UTC

Men accused of planning terrorist attack near RAF base used to strike Iran

British and American forces have used RAF Fairford in Gloucestershire since the opening weeks of the war with Iran that began on 28 February 2026. The base houses the headquarters of the US Air Force’s 501st Combat Support Wing and the 420th Air Base Squadron. It has served as a launch point for strikes that continued after the collapse of the Islamabad on 8 July.

On 27 September 2026 police received a call at 12:45 a.m. local time about three suspicious vans near the base perimeter in the village of Whelford. Officers stopped the vehicles, arrested five men on suspicion of offences under the Explosives Act, and later re-arrested them under the Terrorism Act 2000. A 400-metre cordon was placed around the vans while an Army Explosive Ordnance Disposal robot examined their contents. Residents in dozens of nearby homes were evacuated to a leisure centre and barred from returning overnight.

The five men remained in custody late on 27 September. Metropolitan Police counter-terrorism officers are leading the inquiry with local forces and have released no names, nationalities or further details about what the vans carried. The investigation continues inside the cordon.
