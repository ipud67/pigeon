21:T4ae,Trump rejected an Iranian proposal sent through Qatari mediators that would reopen the Strait of Hormuz on the seventh day of a truce and halt fighting across the Middle East including Lebanon. The plan matched terms from the June Islamabad Memorandum that both sides signed then abandoned in July. Trump said the immediate opening of the strait was not acceptable because Iran gets its money from traffic there while US ports remain under blockade.

The fight has stayed narrowed to control of the 21-mile strait since the July 12 IRGC move to shut it and the resumption of nightly US strikes. Iranian ports sit under US blockade and Hormuz crossings have stayed low. The proposal came at the UN General Assembly this week with coordination from Iran's supreme leader and followed a meeting between Iran's foreign minister and Trump's envoy.

This standoff is likely to run at least through the November US midterm elections. Trump told staff a renewed bombing campaign is the probable next step after that vote. The situation changes only if one side accepts the other's core demand on sanctions, assets, or immediate strait access.
