Catholic teaching has held the same line for decades: life runs from conception to natural death and no one may shorten it on purpose. The French bishops fought the bill through every stage of debate and the pope repeated that stand in front of sick pilgrims and health workers. The French law still needs its final regulatory steps before full rollout. Church officials and some disability groups keep pressing lawmakers on safeguards and conscience protections for doctors. No schedule has been set for any parliamentary review or court test of the new rules.

The statement keeps the Catholic position fixed and public. Further movement depends on how strictly France writes the final decrees and whether Catholic health facilities or individual caregivers test the law in practice.
