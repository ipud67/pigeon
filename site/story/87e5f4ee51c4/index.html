Conditional release for 32 crew members of two Ecuadorian boats destroyed by the US

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineLibertad condicional para 32 tripulantes de dos barcos ecuatorianos destruidos por EE.UU.

The United States has long maintained a drug program in the eastern Pacific that lets its forces board, search and destroy stateless or suspect vessels used to move cocaine north from South America. Ecuadorian-flagged boats fall under that when they operate without proper papers or when crews refuse inspection.

On an undisclosed date before September 2026 two such Ecuadorian vessels were intercepted, boarded and then deliberately sunk by US authorities during an operation. Thirty-two crew members taken off the boats were brought to the United States and charged with drug-trafficking offenses in federal court.

A federal judge granted the 32 men conditional release on September 9. The order allows them to return home under supervision and travel restrictions while their cases continue. The decision turns on the next court date and the conditions each man must meet to remain out of .
