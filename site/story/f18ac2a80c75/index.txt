Ukrainian air defenses intercept some but not all incoming weapons, and residential buildings get struck when interceptors miss or debris falls. The next phase depends on whether Russian launch rates stay at current levels or drop after any ceasefire talks gain traction.

No court filing or procedural deadline is attached to this single strike. The situation changes only if missile attack frequency falls for a sustained period or a formal truce halts launches.
