Regional and Municipal Elections 2026: ONPE to begin national distribution of electoral materials this Friday

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineElecciones Regionales y Municipales 2026: ONPE iniciará este viernes distribución nacional de material electoral

Peru holds regional and elections every four years. The last ones took place in 2022. The National Office of Processes, known as ONPE, runs the logistics for voting across the country. It prints ballots and tally sheets in a central plant south of Lima, then sends them to 125 offices that cover every province and district.

On Friday, September 19, 2026, ONPE will start moving those finished materials out of its main warehouse in Lurín. The first shipments will go to the decentralized offices so workers there can prepare polling stations in time for the October vote.

The distribution marks the point where the printed packets leave the factory floor and enter the national delivery chain. ONPE has already completed printing and is now in the final assembly stage before trucks begin rolling. The schedule now turns on whether every office receives its full without delay.
