SANTIAGO DE CUBA
September 15, 2026 · 14:01 UTC

After flight of its foreign partners, Islazul seeks private investors inside Cuba

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineTras la huida de sus socios extranjeros, Islazul busca inversores privados dentro de Cuba

Cuba's state tourism company Islazul ran its hotels and restaurants for decades with contracts that brought in foreign operators from Europe and Canada. Those deals supplied capital, staff training and steady foreign visitors until the partners began pulling out one by one in the past three years.

On 15 September 2026 Islazul published a tender in Santiago de Cuba for private Cuban investors to take over roughly ten gastronomic spaces that the foreign firms had vacated. The offer gives local businesses the right to lease the sites, equip them and run them under Islazul branding for a set term and fee.

The tender marks the first time the state firm has opened these particular contracts to domestic capital on the island. Whether enough Cuban investors step forward will decide how many of the spaces reopen and how quickly the company can replace the lost foreign management.
