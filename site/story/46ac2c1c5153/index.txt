The pact will hold in its current three-country form for the next 12 to 18 months unless one of the partners publicly tables a new membership list or a joint military exercise schedule that names additional states. Observable indicators are statements from the FO, Saudi foreign ministry readouts, and Turkiye’s defence communiques. Any shift would show first in a joint communique that drops the “not on the cards” language or in an announced headquarters expansion beyond the current planning cell in Makkah.

Next step is routine follow-up talks scheduled for Riyadh in November to set annual deterrence drills. No new members join without unanimous public approval from all three capitals.
