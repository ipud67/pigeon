Seven cocaine bars disappear from Suva High Court exhibit room - Fiji Police

Police in Fiji keep drug evidence from big cases inside a locked room at the Suva High Court. The room holds exhibits that prosecutors plan to use at trial. On Tuesday a team from the Criminal Investigations Department, senior court officials, a prosecutor from the Director of Public office, and a crime-scene officer opened the room and counted the items in front of each other.

They found seven bars of cocaine gone from a set of 39 exhibits that had been stored there. Police assistant commissioner Loraini Seru said the bars disappeared sometime after the last check. The CID team started a formal investigation the same day to learn how the bars left the room.

The missing bars now sit inside an active criminal inquiry that runs alongside the original drug case still waiting for trial. Police have not said when the last full inventory took place or whether the room had been opened in between. Commissioner Krissy Barrett noted that Pacific forces have seized 17 tonnes of cocaine and other drugs since January, but the seven bars remain unaccounted for inside the Suva courthouse.

The inquiry will run as long as the CID team keeps pulling records, logs, and statements from everyone who had access to the room since the exhibits arrived. It ends when they either locate the bars, charge someone, or close the file without charges.
