New Jersey mom charged with obstruction after 'missing' son found hiding in her crawl space

A New Jersey mother reported her teenage son missing to East Brunswick police on September 19. Officers arrived at the home to search for the boy after the report. They asked the mother if the teen was inside.

She told them he was not there. Police then found the juvenile hiding in a crawl space in the basement of the house.

The mother now faces a charge of obstruction for the false statement to officers. The teen was located unharmed during the search that same day. The case remains with local authorities.
