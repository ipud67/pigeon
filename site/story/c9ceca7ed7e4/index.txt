19:T53e,A Maltese court acquitted Yorgen Fenech on Wednesday of masterminding the 2017 car-bomb murder of journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia. The ruling sits on the ladder that began with her killing, the quick arrest of three hitmen, years of leaks tying Fenech to the plot through his company 17 Black, his 2019 arrest, and the long-running trial that ended this week. Her family immediately called the verdict a denial of justice and said they would push for every available appeal route.

The case now moves to the appeals court. Prosecutors must file within days if they want to challenge the acquittal on points of law or fresh evidence. Without a successful appeal or a separate prosecution on linked charges, Fenech walks free on this count.

How long this drags on depends on how fast the appeals judges schedule hearings and whether prosecutors turn up new witnesses or documents that the first trial missed. Right now the observable indicator is the family’s public vow to keep fighting and the attorney general’s office staying silent on next moves.

If prosecutors file a strong appeal with concrete new proof, the case could head back for retrial in 12 to 18 months. If they don’t, or if the appeal fails, the matter ends here.
