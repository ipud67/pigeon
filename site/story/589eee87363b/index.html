Yemeni government troops withdraw from area near key strait

Translated from Dutch · read the original →
The original headlineRegeringstroepen Jemen trekken zich terug uit gebied bij belangrijke zeestraat

Yemen's civil war began in 2014 when the Houthis the government from Sanaa. An informal four-year ceasefire started to unravel in July 2026 as the US-Iran conflict narrowed to control of the Strait of Hormuz and shipping routes through the Red Sea.

Yemeni pro-government forces backed by Saudi Arabia withdrew troops from positions around Bab al-Mandab Strait and Dhubab Airport this month. The move ended a six-week counteroffensive that had failed to drive the Houthis from the western coastline or recapture the port city of Mocha. The Southern Armed Forces shifted those units to reinforce lines around the capital of Aden after repeated Houthi attacks there including an October strike on the presidential palace.

The redeployment leaves the Houthis in control of the evacuated overlooking the strait.
