Italy’s government says Italian PM Giorgia Meloni to visit Carabinieri Forest, Environmental and Agri-food Unit Command (CUFAA)

Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni will visit the Comando Unità Forestali, Ambientali e Agroalimentari dell'Arma dei Carabinieri (CUFAA) on Monday 16 November in Naples at 3:00 p.m.
