24:T55e,Trump announced the tariff removal on Irish whiskey at the trophy presentation for the Irish Open at his Doonbeg golf resort on the second day of a two-day Ireland visit. The move ends the 15% tariff that has applied to Republic of Ireland distilled whiskey entering the US while UK-made whiskey has faced none since the May cut. It follows his earlier remarks in the same trip backing a united Ireland as one of the natural next steps for the island.

The tariff gap had created uneven costs for all-island supply chains where Northern Ireland brands bottle at the Great Northern Distillery in Dundalk and get classed as EU production. Lifting the Republic tariff levels that field. No clear signal yet on when the change takes effect or how it gets written into trade rules.

This sits on the ladder of the May UK whiskey tariff removal and the weekend meetings with the Taoiseach and President where the topic came up directly. Treasury yields show no visible shift tied to the announcement: 2-yr at 4.65 percent, 10-yr at 4.97 percent, 2s10s spread at 32 basis points as of September 14.

How long it lasts depends on whether the next administration keeps the cut or rolls it back in a broader EU trade reset. Next rung is likely the formal order or regulatory filing that makes the zero tariff stick.
