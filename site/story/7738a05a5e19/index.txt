The university stated it will not divest from Israel and is barred by New York Executive Order 157, signed in 2016, which directs state entities to avoid funds tied to BDS activity against Israel.

The legal fight began with a March 2024 Freedom of Information Law request by a CUNY Law student tied to Students for Justice in Palestine. CUNY has repeated its opposition to divestment in statements to JNS. The order remains in force with no recorded challenge or repeal. Activists would need a new lawsuit that overturns the order or changes state law for the position to shift. No such case has been filed.

This stance holds steady. Next step is routine portfolio updates with no divestment expected.
