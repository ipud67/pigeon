The device was fixed inside a loader rickshaw that exploded on the link road to Khyber district, blowing out the outer wall of the Sra Khawra post in Mattani jurisdiction. Police have started collecting fragments and questioning locals along the route.

This kind of low-tech VBIED against fixed police points has run in the region for years. Each new blast usually triggers a few days of cordon searches, extra checkpoints, and claims of responsibility from local networks. The pattern holds until security forces roll up a specific cell or the groups shift to softer targets.

Next step is standard: bomb-squad forensics on the rickshaw parts, sweeps for secondary devices, and a look at recent theft reports for similar vehicles. The cycle continues unless officials lock down loader traffic on that stretch or pick up the bomb-maker through tip-offs or raids.
