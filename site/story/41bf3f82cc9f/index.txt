One man left the country with no plans to return. The other received a state pardon that ended his deportability and mandatory detention under Section 1226(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Act. The government had asked the court to decide when due process requires a bond hearing for people held this way and what showing the government must make to keep holding them. Both sides now agree the specific claims are moot. The Justice Department said in its letter it plans to bring the same legal question back in a different case soon.

This particular docket entry ends here. The underlying dispute over bond hearings for long mandatory detention will surface again only when the government files and the court grants a new petition with a live set of facts. No schedule or vehicle exists yet.
