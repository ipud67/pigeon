Wineries in Aomori, northeastern Japan, form group to seek recognition

Wineries in Aomori Prefecture began producing wine in the 1970s after the national government lifted postwar restrictions on grape cultivation outside traditional regions. Local growers planted hardy varieties suited to the cold climate and acidic soil, and by the 2010s several dozen small operations turned out bottles sold mainly within the prefecture.

On September 13, 2026, those producers created a formal association whose rules require members to use at least 85 percent Aomori-grown grapes and to submit wines for independent tasting panels that award a regional seal.

The new group now controls the right to grant that seal and plans to present the first bottles to national distributors and export agents later this year.
