22:T454,The executive order signed on September 17 directs the Interior Secretary to open hunting in two national monuments within 90 days and sets a broader presumption that federal lands stay open for hunting unless a site-specific resource reason closes them. It also calls for new infrastructure to reach locked-up parcels and for streamlined rules on hunter training programs. This is the latest step in a sequence that began with earlier proclamations creating those monuments and later laws that tightened federal land rules. The order itself carries no enforcement deadline beyond the 90-day mark and leaves final closures to agency review of local conditions. Next measurable moves will show up in Interior Department notices listing which parcels actually open and in any legal filings challenging those notices under existing monument statutes.  

The order will run at least until a future administration revokes it or Congress passes binding new access law. Without either, the presumption-of-access language stays in force and agencies keep issuing the required reviews.
