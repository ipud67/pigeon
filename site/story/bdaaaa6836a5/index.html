Russian official to attend G20 energy meeting in Houston next week, White House official says

Russia and the United States have kept diplomatic channels open on energy policy even after the full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022. The two countries continued to exchange information at G20 energy ministerial meetings that began under previous and have run annually since.

A Russian official will sit at the table when energy ministers and their gather in Houston next week, a White House official confirmed on September 11. The meeting is one of several technical sessions the G20 energy track holds outside the main leaders' summit.

The session will go forward with the Russian delegate in attendance, the first such gathering on American soil since the invasion. The agenda now turns on whether any joint statements emerge or whether the participants simply record their separate positions.
