Ogun releases N6.75bn for gratuity, additional pension benefits

Ogun State government has paid pensions and to retired civil servants for years under a standing arrangement that sets aside funds each budget cycle for those who reach retirement age. The process requires the state to clear both the one-time gratuity lump sum and any additional monthly pension top-ups once retirees meet the qualifying service terms set by the civil service rules.

On September 11, 2026, the Head of Service announced the release of N6.75 billion in fresh payments. The sum broke into N2.751 billion paid out as gratuity to 432 retired workers and N4 billion sent for additional pension benefits covering every retiree from July 2025 through the present date.

The new money moves the accounts current for the listed beneficiaries and brings the July 2025 cohort fully into payment. The state payroll system now turns on clearing the next batch of retirees as they hit their eligibility window and maintaining the flow of the additional pension top-ups on schedule.

Thinking ahead
Ogun State just paid out N6.75 billion in retirement money. The Head of Service released N2.751 billion to clear gratuity for 432 people and another N4 billion to cover extra pension payments for retirees from July 2025 until now. This fits the pattern of the state government releasing batches of owed retirement funds on a regular schedule. The next payments will depend on when the next batch of verified retirees is processed and when the state allocates fresh cash. The flow continues as long as the government keeps listing eligible names and releasing budgeted money on time. It slows or stops only if the approval list shrinks, verification bottlenecks appear, or the state diverts the budgeted line item to other spending. Three-to-six months of similar payouts at this scale look likely unless the retiree queue visibly shortens on the next release.
