FRANCE
October 24, 2026 · 21:14 UTC

French police arrested nearly 1000 people in protests across France on Saturday

Protests over school funding, living costs and public services have run in France since mid-September. Student demands for more money and better conditions grew into wider demonstrations. President Emmanuel Macron said this week that protests illegitimate would be prohibited. Parisian police authorized only a union march for Saturday and turned down requests from the main student union and the Yellow Vest movement.

On October 24 authorities counted 410,000 people in the streets while the General of Labour counted 800,000. Nearly 1,000 arrests took place nationwide. Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez said most marches passed without major incident, with less damage to businesses and no targeted attacks on public buildings.

Tens of thousands of officers were on duty. Several museums closed for the day. The protests now turn on which routes authorities will approve and how police will handle crowds that form outside those routes.
