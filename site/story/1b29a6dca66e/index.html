South Africa’s government says President Ramaphosa appoints Director of Public Prosecutions for the Western Cape

The National Prosecuting Authority runs criminal cases in South Africa's divisions under the National Prosecuting Authority Act of 1998. Section 13(1)(a) of that act gives the president power to name a Director of Public Prosecutions for each division after talking to the justice minister and the national director. The Western Cape post had been vacant or acting for some time before this step.

On 10 September 2026 President Cyril Ramaphosa named Adrian Carl Mopp to the job. Mopp had worked inside the NPA since the 1990s, starting as an admitted advocate in 1993. He ran the Directorate of Special Operations in the Western Cape from 2004 to 2009, moved to the Asset Unit, and served as Director of Public Prosecutions in the same division from 2009 onward.

Mopp now holds the full authority to direct prosecutions, manage senior prosecutors, and oversee the division's operations from Cape Town. The appointment places a 30-year insider in the top provincial seat and closes the immediate vacancy created by the prior departure.
