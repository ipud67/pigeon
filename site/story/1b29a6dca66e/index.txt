The move followed talks with Justice Minister Mmamoloko Kubayi and National DPP Andy Mothibi and rests on his 30-plus years in prosecutorial management.

This appointment fills the post and locks in the current leadership track for at least the standard five-year term unless Mopp steps down early or faces removal proceedings. The next observable step is Mopp taking operational charge of the Western Cape NPA division and any public statement from him on priorities. Change would require either a new presidential appointment triggered by vacancy or a court challenge to the process that succeeds on procedural grounds.
