WASHINGTON
October 26, 2026 · 23:05 UTC

William Daroff, CEO of the Conference of Presidents of Major American Jewish Organizations, said Jews will watch Israeli election results at a Washington watch party

US and Israeli strikes on Iran that began in late February 2026 have narrowed to a fight for control of the Strait of Hormuz. A June memorandum that lifted the dual blockades and opened a 60-day window collapsed on 8 July, after which US strikes resumed and Iran moved to shut the strait.

Jewish organizational leaders in the United States planned to track the Israeli elections from a watch party in downtown Washington hosted by the American Zionist Movement and the Jewish Federations of North America. William Daroff, CEO of the Conference of Presidents of Major American Jewish Organizations, said he would spend the day on calls and video meetings with colleagues in both countries while following exit polls and possibilities. About 65 people, including staffers and representatives from groups such as the Reform Action Center, Hadassah, the Anti-Defamation League, and the Zionist Organization of America, were expected to attend the informal gathering.

The event gave American Jewish groups a place to watch results together, exchange views on Israeli politics, and consider what a new government might mean for ties between Israel, the United States, and the Jewish diaspora while the Hormuz campaign continued.
