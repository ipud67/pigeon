A youth died in a Lahore hospital a week after two men pumped LPG into his body at a decanting shop in Kot Ishaq

GUJRAT: A youth who was subjected to brutal torture by two suspects, who had allegedly pumped liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) into his body at a decanting outlet in Kot Ishaq locality in Wanike Tarar police in Hafizabad a week ago, died in a hospital in Lahore on Monday. According to an FIR lodged with the police on Sept 14, Ghulam that his nephew Ali Hassan (18) was intercepted by the suspects – Mudassar and Majid – in a local market. He said the suspects took the youth to their LPG decanting shop, overpowered him and allegedly pumped the gas into his rectum.
