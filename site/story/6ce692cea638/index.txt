The case sits on a ladder of earlier grid attacks that began in 2024 and grew more targeted through 2025, each time leaving transformer stations or substations offline for hours. Dobrindt's statement shifts the probe from general threat assessment to a focused manhunt.

How much longer this runs depends on whether the suspect stays active or goes to ground. Police have increased patrols at key substations and begun door-to-door checks in activist circles. If they arrest the individual and seize tools or plans, the attacks stop. Without an arrest, copycats could join in and stretch the campaign into 2027.

Next step is formal charges once forensic matches clear the threshold for indictment. The situation changes only on capture or confirmed cessation of attacks.
