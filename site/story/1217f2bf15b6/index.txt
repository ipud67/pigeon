18:T472,The MHRA has tabled amendments to the Government’s Health Bill that lay groundwork for wider information sharing between regulators and set the framework for a full medical device licensing regime to replace current EU-derived rules. This is the latest step in a ladder of post-Brexit moves that began with the Medicines and Medical Devices Act 2021 and continued through consultations in 2022-2024 on reforming device oversight. Next procedural rung is debate and possible passage of the amendments during the Bill’s remaining stages in Parliament, followed by secondary legislation that would spell out the actual licensing rules. Observable indicators that this keeps moving forward are continued government backing in the Commons and Lords plus timely publication of draft regulations; any major pushback from device manufacturers or patient groups that forces significant changes would slow or alter the timetable. Without those visible blocks the amendments are likely to clear Parliament in the current session.

This runs through at least early 2026 before the licensing regime itself starts to take shape.
