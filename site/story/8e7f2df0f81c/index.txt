No prior court rulings or laws are on record here, so the budget line itself is the only clear signal that the project is moving from talk to funded trial.

How long it runs depends on whether the test produces usable case summaries, precedent checks, or ruling drafts that judges actually adopt without big errors. Early results will show up in internal court reports or small pilot data by late 2027. If the AI cuts routine paperwork without blowing up accuracy, the budget will likely grow and spread to more civil dockets. If judges reject it or errors spike, the money stops and the test stays small.

Right now the indicators are too thin to pin down an end date or next big expansion. The ¥60 million request is the only hard number on the table.
