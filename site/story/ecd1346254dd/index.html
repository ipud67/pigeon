October 26, 2026 · 18:55 UTC

Strikes on Iran in Israel remain based only on security needs, IDF chief said

The US and Israel opened strikes on Iran in late February 2026. The fight settled into weekly strike and counter-strike phases that narrowed to control of the Strait of Hormuz after the Islamabad Memorandum collapsed on 8 July.

The statement came after leaks to Channel 12 television reported that Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and IDF chief of staff General Eyal Zamir disagreed over the timing of any fresh strikes in the final days before a national election. Defence Minister Israel Katz rejected the reporting, while CNN separately reported that General Zamir told his US any pre-election strikes would delay the vote and that the White House withheld approval.

The military added that it remains on high alert across every arena in light of various warnings and the sensitivity of the current period. The statement placed the military's command decisions on one side of the reported disagreement with the government and left open when or whether further strikes would occur.
