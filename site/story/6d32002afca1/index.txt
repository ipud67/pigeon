23:T475,FleetCor, now called Corpay, reached a $100 million settlement with the FTC on an administrative complaint that followed a 2019 federal court filing. The district court ruled in 2023 that the company charged hidden fees on its fuel cards, sent invoices that did not list them, and made false claims about fuel savings, late fees, and fraud controls. An appeals court upheld the judgment against the company on every count in 2026 and kept the permanent ban on billing without express informed consent, hiding charges behind links, or deceptive advertising about the cards.

The $100 million payment goes to affected small-business customers. FleetCor and its CEO agreed not to fight the return of a court injunction against the CEO. The FTC vote was 1-0 with the chairman recused.

This closes the administrative track that ran parallel to the court case. No further payments or bans are on the table unless the company violates the order, which would trigger contempt proceedings in federal court. The next observable step is distribution of the redress fund and routine FTC compliance checks.
