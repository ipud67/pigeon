Public libraries to join daily reading hour proposed by Education Ministry

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineBibliotecas públicas se sumarán a la hora de lectura propuesta por el Minedu

The National Library System has operated public libraries across Peru for decades under the authority of the Biblioteca Nacional del Perú. These branches lend books, run community programs, and open set hours each day to local readers of all ages. Yesterday the Education Minister presented the National Education Plan that runs from 2026 through 2031.

José Antonio Chang announced a daily reading hour as part of that plan. Public libraries that belong to the National Library System will now take part in the hour, setting aside dedicated time each day for reading activities inside their buildings.

The libraries will adjust their schedules to include the fixed reading slot starting with the new education plan. The exact start date, length of the slot, and content requirements remain tied to further details the ministry has yet to release.

Thinking ahead
Public libraries in the National Library System will add a daily reading hour after the Education Minister laid out the National Education Plan 2026-2031. The plan sets the new activity for schools and now folds in the SNB network as the latest step. Libraries already run story times and book loans on their own schedules; the ministry proposal ties them to a fixed daily slot. How long it runs depends on whether the ministry issues a start date and whether SNB directors assign staff hours to it. If the plan clears the full approval process and libraries get extra budget for materials, the hour could roll out nationwide within months. Without those, it stays on paper. Next move is the ministry publishing the exact timetable and training requirements for librarians. If uptake in the first wave of libraries hits low attendance numbers, the schedule could shrink to three days a week. Horizon: 4-6 months until first libraries post the new daily hour on their doors.
