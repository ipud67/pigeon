17:T505,The United States Trade Representative asked Mexico on September 25 2026 to review labor practices at the Yokohama Tire plant in Coahuila under the USMCA Rapid Response Labor Mechanism. The request came after the Interagency Labor Committee reviewed a petition filed August 26 by the Mexican union LSOM and the International Lawyers Assisting Workers Network. The petition listed retaliation against union supporters, unequal plant access for rival unions, vote irregularities, dismissals, and failure to follow the rubber industry contrato ley. 

This is the latest use of the mechanism that lets one country trigger a fast check on the other when credible evidence of denied organizing rights appears. The US also ordered a hold on clearing Yokohama tire imports until the matter clears. Mexico now has ten days to accept the review and, if it does, forty-five days to finish it.

The case sits on top of the recent shutdown of Yokohama’s Salem, Virginia plant and layoff of nearly 600 US workers. No earlier RRM cases against Yokohama itself are listed in the record supplied.

Indicators are too thin to forecast how long the review will run or what changes it may produce. The ten-day and forty-five-day clocks are the only firm markers on the table.
