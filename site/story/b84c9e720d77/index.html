Fetterman praises Trump in bombshell GOP convention video

Pennsylvania has sent Democrats to the Senate since 1992. John Fetterman won that seat in 2022 after a campaign that centered on his support for unions and the state's steel towns. The industry has lost tens of thousands of jobs over decades to foreign competition and mill closures.

On September 9 2026 Fetterman recorded a video that ran at the Republican National Convention in Dallas. In it he credited President Trump and Republican Senate candidate Dave McCormick with steps that kept steel in place and steered federal money toward Pennsylvania mills.

The video played to delegates on the third night of the convention. It marks the first time a sitting Democratic senator has appeared in a positive light at a Republican presidential nominating event since the parties took their current shape on trade. The appearance now sits inside a Senate race in which steel policy has become a flashpoint between Fetterman and his eventual Republican opponent.

Thinking ahead
Fetterman has backed steel tariffs and mill reopenings since he ran for Senate in 2022. The video shown at the Dallas convention on September 9 lines up with that record and with his past votes for bills that kept steel protections in place. No party switch or floor speech has followed, only the taped remarks that name Trump and McCormick as allies on the issue. The clip changes nothing in the Senate calendar or in Pennsylvania filing deadlines. Fetterman still sits as a Democrat with a term that runs through 2028. What would shift the picture is either a formal party change recorded with the Senate clerk or a primary challenge backed by state party leaders who now treat the video as a breaking point. The situation holds steady until one of those two things shows up in the public record. Horizon: another 12 to 18 months of same-party status unless a filing or leadership vote appears first.
