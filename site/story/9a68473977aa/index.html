STRAIT OF HORMUZ
August 31, 2026 · 13:47 UTC

US and Iran trade strikes for first time in weeks

Overview
The attack on Larak Island in the Strait of Hormuz - the first known US strike since late July - has killed two people and injured two others.
Short history
US and Iran traded strikes in the Strait of Hormuz on 31 August 2026. A US attack on Larak Island killed two people and injured two others. It was the first known US strike since late July. The war began when the US and Israel opened strikes on Iran in late February 2026. A June lifted the dual and set a 60-day negotiating window. That deal collapsed on 8 July and sustained US strikes resumed. The fight narrowed to control of the Strait of Hormuz. On 6 July Iran struck three commercial tankers that bypassed its approved transit routes. On 12 July the IRGC moved to shut the strait and Tehran suspended the memorandum. US strikes hit Iranian surveillance sites, logistics, weapons storage, maritime facilities, an electricity plant and a desalination plant on 18 July. Crossings dropped and oil prices rose. The UAE cut trade with Iran on 19 August after accusing it of firing missiles toward its waters.
Predictive model
US hit Larak Island in the Strait of Hormuz on August 31 and killed two people there. This marks the first known US strike since late July in a fight that has narrowed to control of the strait. The pattern since the Islamabad Memorandum collapsed on July 8 has been tit-for-tat strikes and counter-strikes with Iran trying to shut the passage and the US working to keep it open. The exchange shows both sides still have the capacity and the will to hit each other directly after a quiet stretch. No new talks or cease-fire moves have surfaced. The next rung will likely be another Iranian reply aimed at shipping or US assets in the Gulf, followed by a US counter-strike on Iranian maritime or missile sites. Without a clear procedural off-ramp or visible third-party mediation, the cycle points to more weeks of the same. This round of strikes will probably run another 2-4 weeks before either side signals a pause.
