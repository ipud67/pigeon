19:T421,California has run a program since the 1990s that placed nearly one million students who speak another language at home into special classes. Schools used multiple tests and benchmarks before a student could leave those classes and join regular English instruction. On September 28 2026 Governor Newsom signed a bill that drops the extra tests and leaves only one state-approved English assessment as the exit requirement.

The law takes effect for the current school year. Districts must now update their placement and exit rules to match the single-test standard. Schools will track how many students move out of the program each year under the new rule.

The change stands until the legislature passes another bill or a court blocks it. State education officials will issue guidance on the approved test and reporting. If reclassification numbers stay flat or drop, lawmakers could face pressure to tighten the single-test rule again.

Next step is districts rewriting their local plans by spring 2027.
