No new funding pledges, repatriation agreements, or security guarantees were announced today.

This pattern has held steady for nearly a decade: annual statements, little measurable change on the ground. The situation is likely to continue at roughly the same scale for at least another 12 to 24 months unless Myanmar’s junta or Bangladesh changes its stance on returns, or a major new donor surge appears. Observable indicators to watch are any joint statement from ASEAN capitals, a concrete UNHCR-Bangladesh repatriation timetable, or a documented drop in camp violence and aid shortfalls.

Without one of those concrete shifts, the status quo of encampment and insecurity rolls forward.
