APC wins Dawakin Kudu constituency by-election in Kano

Kano State holds by-elections for vacant seats in its House of Assembly when a member dies, resigns or is removed. Dawakin Kudu is one of the 44 single-member constituencies that send representatives to the 40-seat chamber in the state capital. The Independent National Commission schedules and runs these polls under the Electoral Act that sets the vote count and rules for each local government area.

On Saturday the commission’s returning officer for Dawakin Kudu, Ibrahim Tajo-Suraj, tallied the ballots cast that day and announced the result in the constituency. Hussain Yusuf-Datti of the All Progressives Congress received 22,890 votes. The next highest total went to Habib Hamisu of the Peoples Party with 270 votes. Three other candidates took 108, 89, 53 and 39 votes respectively.

The returning officer declared Yusuf-Datti the winner after confirming he met the legal threshold and led the field. The seat now sits with the All Progressives Congress pending the window for any challenge to the result.
