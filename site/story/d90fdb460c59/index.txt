(Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services)1a:T413,The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services just put out the required Federal Register notice inviting public comments on a planned information collection under the Paperwork Reduction Act. This is the standard second-round comment period that follows the first notice; CMS has to log and respond to whatever comes in before they can send the package to OMB for final clearance. The clock on comments runs 60 days from publication, so that lands in early November 2026.  

From there the next rung is OMB review, which usually takes 30 to 90 days unless the public comments force CMS to revise the burden estimate or the forms. If the comments are light or technical, the collection gets approved on schedule. If they raise serious utility or burden problems that CMS cannot fix quickly, the agency may have to republish a revised notice and start the comment period over.  

No big shift in policy or funding is visible in the notice itself; this is routine paperwork renewal.
