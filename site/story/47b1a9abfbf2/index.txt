19:T404,The Italian government issued decree-law 187 on 10 September 2026 to keep the 17-cent diesel excise cut in force until 17 September, add tax credits for haulage companies, and fast-track permits on key energy works by naming special commissioners. This is the latest step in a series of short-term fuel relief measures that have been renewed every few weeks or months since the price spikes began in 2022. The decree now goes to parliament for conversion into law within 60 days or it lapses.  

The measure will run at least until mid-September and probably longer if parliament approves it. Next visible step is the start of debate in the Chamber of Deputies, where the governing majority holds the numbers to pass it. Any change would require either the government to let the cut expire without renewal or parliament to vote it down, neither of which is on the table right now.  

Gap in indicators: no public schedule yet for the exact parliamentary calendar on this bill.
