Mountain of garbage forces Havana-Guantánamo train to stop in Las Tunas

Translated from Spanish · read the original →
The original headlineUna montaña de basura obliga a detener el tren Habana-Guantánamo en Las Tunas

Cuba’s rail network runs on aging tracks and outdated rolling stock that break down often and receive little maintenance. The single-line route from Havana to Guantánamo carries passengers and limited freight through the eastern provinces, where towns have few other transport options.

On September 17 the train came to a halt outside Las Tunas when it met a large pile of garbage dumped across the rails. Crew and passengers left the cars, walked to the obstruction, and moved the trash by hand until the line was clear enough for the train to pass.

The convoy resumed its journey after the manual clearance. The incident left the rails open again but highlighted the everyday obstacles that slow rail traffic on the island’s main east-west corridor.
