1f:T4d4,A federal judge in Texas issued an injunction Friday that stops border wall work in the Big Bend sector of the national park. The order bars the administration from using waivers or starting any ground-disturbing activity until the full case ends. Plaintiffs, including a river guide and a church group, argued the waivers exceeded authority and would permanently damage park land that cannot be fixed with money later.

This is the latest step in a ladder of legal challenges that began when the administration moved to restart construction after earlier pauses. The judge found the plaintiffs showed a strong chance of winning on at least one claim, which triggers the block.

The case now sits in discovery and briefing. It continues until a final ruling or until an appeals court lifts the injunction. The block holds unless the administration wins on the merits or negotiates a settlement that satisfies the core claims about waivers and park impact.

Next likely move is motions to dismiss or for summary judgment once records are exchanged. Any shift would require the district court to rule for the government or the Fifth Circuit to overturn the injunction on appeal.
