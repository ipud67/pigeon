21:T423,The GAO report tallied $9.5 billion spent on paid administrative leave across federal agencies in 2025, a jump tied to the buyout offers rolled out under the Department of Government Efficiency. Paid leave usage had already climbed 435 percent between 2023 and 2025 as agencies shifted workers to administrative status instead of immediate separation. Congress set the buyout rules in statute years earlier; the report simply records the cost once the program ran.

Next moves sit with the appropriations committees. Lawmakers review the GAO figures during the next budget cycle and can tighten leave caps or rewrite buyout payout formulas in the fiscal 2027 spending bills. Without those changes the pattern holds: agencies keep the same authority, costs stay high, and future GAO updates will log similar or larger totals.

The situation shifts only when statute changes. Until then the $9.5 billion line item repeats each year the buyouts continue at current scale.
