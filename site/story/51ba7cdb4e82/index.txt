19:T45b,Ukrainian forces have knocked out more than 3,000 Russian unmanned ground vehicles with drones since January, with monthly counts climbing from 460 in January to a peak of 511 in August and nearly 200 more in the first half of September. The Defence Ministry tracks these strikes through the Delta battlefield system and began listing them in daily General Staff reports back in May. The numbers show a steady rise in UGV losses through the summer even as Russia rolled out newer models like the Depesha system.

The trend points to continued high monthly losses as long as Ukrainian drone teams keep finding and engaging the vehicles at current rates. Next steps depend on whether Russian crews adjust tactics, add more armor or electronic jamming to the UGVs, or shift to fewer but better-protected platforms. Without a clear drop in the monthly tally or official Russian statements on new countermeasures, the pattern of rising hits is likely to hold.

The situation could change if Delta tracking accuracy falls or if Russian UGV deployments slow sharply.
