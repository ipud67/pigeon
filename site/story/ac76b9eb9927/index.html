Court blocks Trump's attempt to limit mail-in votes

Translated from Danish · read the original →
The original headlineDomstol blokerer for Trumps forsøg på at begrænse brevstemmer

A federal judge in 2020 ruled that states must count mail-in ballots received up to three days after Election Day if they carried a postmark dated on or before Election Day. That order stood through the 2024 cycle and set the default deadline most states followed for the 2026 midterms.

On September 14 a three-judge panel of the U.S. Court of Appeals for the District of Columbia Circuit upheld that 2020 order and blocked an emergency motion filed by attorneys for former President Trump. The motion had asked the court to let the Department of Justice enforce a stricter same-day receipt rule for federal elections.

The ruling keeps the three-day extension in place for the coming election cycle. The case now returns to the district court for any further arguments on the underlying .
