1d:T502,FinCEN published a notice of proposed rulemaking on October 5 that flags any non-U.S. company controlled by the A7 Network as a primary money-laundering concern tied to Russian illicit finance. The proposal would bar covered U.S. financial institutions from transmitting funds that involve those sub-agents. Written comments are due by November 4.

This sits on the ladder of earlier statutes: the 2021 Combating Russian Money Laundering Act section 9714, later widened by the 2022 NDAA. The current step adds a concrete prohibition on one network already used for sanctions evasion by Russian and Iranian actors including the IRGC.

The rulemaking follows standard notice-and-comment procedure. No court action or final rule has issued. Treasury yield levels from October 2 show a 2s10s spread of 45 basis points but supply no direct signal on how this specific measure will move through the comment period or enforcement.

The process is likely to run at least another 30 days while comments arrive, then shift to FinCEN review before any final rule drops. Next visible rung is the count of public comments posted on regulations.gov and whether the agency widens or narrows the prohibition in the final version.
