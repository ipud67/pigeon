LONDON
September 10, 2026 · 16:22 UTC

Four people stabbed in London, motive unclear for now

Translated from Italian · read the original →
The original headlineQuattro persone accoltellate a Londra, matrice per ora non chiara

Police in London investigate knife attacks on city streets as part of routine response to reports of violence in public places. Officers arrived at a scene in the Haringey area after calls about a disturbance shortly before 4:30 p.m. on Thursday. A witness described helping people involved in what appeared to be a street fight that had broken out on the pavement.

Four people suffered stab wounds in the incident. Emergency services treated the victims at the scene and took them to hospitals for further care. Police have not named the injured or released details about their conditions.

The investigation continues with officers gathering evidence from the area and speaking to anyone who saw what happened. No arrests have been reported so far and the motive remains unclear.
