Truck plows into 2 teens riding bike in NYC, killing one of them: cops

Two teenagers shared one bicycle on East 14th Street in Manhattan on a Friday night in September 2026. An excavation truck made a turn from the street onto a northward route at the same moment. The truck struck the bike and its riders near the intersection.

The crash happened shortly before 11 p.m. on September 18. Police said the 15-year-old died at the scene. The 13-year-old reached a hospital with injuries that required treatment but were not described as life-threatening. Officers placed the driver of the truck in for questioning and began an investigation into the sequence of the turn and the positioning of the bike.

The surviving teenager remained under medical care while detectives examined the truck, the roadway markings, and witness accounts to determine how the two vehicles occupied the same space at the moment of impact.
