Canada’s government says the Canadian government named new directors to the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation

The Canada Deposit Insurance has protected deposits at member financial institutions since Parliament created it in 1967. The federal holds the power to name its board members and senior officers through orders in council. Those positions carry fixed terms and require oversight of a fund that backs deposits up to one hundred thousand dollars per depositor per institution.

On September 18 2026 the government named three new directors to the corporation's board. The appointments followed the public selection process that has governed all such Governor in Council posts since the present took office. The individuals now join the board and assume the statutory duty to administer the insurance fund and set premiums for member banks trust companies and other eligible institutions.

The board operates with these new members in place and continues to report to Parliament through the Minister of Finance on the state of the deposit insurance system.
