Seoul's unification ministry is tracking a high-level North Korean delegation in Moscow led by Jo Yong-won, a close aide to Kim Jong-un and Workers' Party secretary. The trip began Saturday and is framed by state media as strengthening ties with Russia under a growing alliance. It lines up right before the Trump-Xi-Putin meeting set for Thursday on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in Shenzhen. The ministry noted the contrast with a recent party-to-party visit to China by another official and said it will watch what role a dual party-government delegation plays in the lead-up. No details on the Moscow schedule have been released. This pattern of overtures to both neighbors has run for weeks. The situation stays on the current track unless a public statement or new delegation move alters the sequence.
