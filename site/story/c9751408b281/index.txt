17:T511,Algeria cut diplomatic ties with the UAE on Thursday after years of friction that included President Tebboune’s repeated charges of meddling in Algerian internal affairs. The move sits on the ladder of Gulf states tightening links with the US side of the current Hormuz fight, a pattern that started with the April assessment of Iran’s strategy and sharpened when the UAE itself severed trade with Iran on 19 August after two missiles headed toward its waters. No immediate procedural next step has been announced by either capital, though embassy staff will now have a short window to leave and any remaining commercial or consular channels will close.

The break could hold for months unless one side offers a public gesture that the other accepts as ending the hostile pattern. Algeria has few direct levers over the UAE, while the UAE’s focus remains on the Strait of Hormuz naval clashes and its own security ties. Without a visible de-escalation signal from either government the freeze is likely to last at least through the current round of regional strikes.

Next visible rung will be whether Algeria tries to rally other Arab states or whether the UAE simply absorbs the loss and keeps its Gulf alignment unchanged.
