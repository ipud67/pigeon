21:T563,A 38-year-old former Taipei social worker named Chiang was placed in detention without communication on September 24 after prosecutors linked her to the disappearance of more than NT$12 million from the accounts of five elderly people she had served as court-appointed guardian. The money went missing from accounts in Wenshan District between her start in the Elderly Welfare Division in May 2025 and her departure in June. City officials spotted the gap during case hand-over, filed a police report, and prosecutors obtained the detention order on grounds of crime scale and collusion risk.

Police searched her home Tuesday, seized designer items, and arrested her at her new supervisor post in New Taipei City government. She now faces probe under the Anti-Corruption Act. The case sits at the pre-trial stage with evidence still being gathered from bank records, guardianship files, and seized goods.

The investigation is likely to run several more months while prosecutors trace fund flows and interview witnesses. It moves next to formal indictment or release depending on whether the seized assets and transaction logs match the missing sums. If the court later accepts the evidence, the file heads to trial; a not-guilty plea or weak paper trail would stretch the timeline further.
