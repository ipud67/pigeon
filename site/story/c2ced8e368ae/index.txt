1a:T5c3,Heal 360 Urgent Care PLLC, Heal 360 Primary Care PLLC, and owner Dr. Mohammed Amer Mohiuddin agreed to pay the United States $20 million to settle False Claims Act allegations. The claims cover bills sent to the HRSA COVID-19 Uninsured Program between January 2021 and March 2022 for higher-level evaluation and management services that the government says were never performed at dozens of drive-through and walk-up testing sites in Texas. At those sites staff mainly did nasal swabs for COVID-19 tests on uninsured people, using CPT code 99211 for specimen collection, while the clinics billed CPT codes 99203, 99204, 99213, and 99214 that require more complex decision-making or time.

The program ran from May 2020 to April 2022 and paid providers for testing, treatment, and vaccines given to people without insurance. Settlement documents list the payment as resolving the matter without an admission of liability.

This type of False Claims Act settlement usually ends the civil case once the money is transferred. Further action would require the Department of Justice to file new criminal charges or for a whistleblower suit to proceed separately. The $20 million figure sets a benchmark for similar pandemic-era billing reviews still open at HRSA.

Next likely step is payment of the agreed amount and routine compliance checks by the inspector general to confirm no new claims go out under the old pattern.
