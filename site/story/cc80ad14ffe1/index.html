English village 'anti-migrant' votes to symbolically separate from UK

Translated from Italian · read the original →
The original headlineVillaggio inglese 'anti-migranti' vota per separarsi simbolicamente da Gb

Piddington is a village of about 370 people in Oxfordshire that has housed asylum seekers at a former RAF base since the government opened the site in 2022. Local residents objected to the placement from the start, citing pressure on services and the character of the area, and formed a campaign group that gathered more than 200 signatures on a petition against the facility.

On September 15 2026 the village held a ballot that asked residents whether Piddington should symbolically declare itself independent of the United Kingdom over the migrant issue. Turnout reached 85 percent and the measure passed by 88 votes to 12. The result carries no legal force and changes none of the national rules that placed the asylum accommodation there.

The ballot has drawn public statements of support from several Conservative MPs who described the vote as an understandable expression of local frustration with central government policy on asylum housing. The Home Office has not altered its plans for the site, and the symbolic leaves the village still bound by all UK laws and obligations.
