NIGERIA
August 29, 2026 · 17:10 UTC

ONSA issues security alert, calls for continued vigilance

Overview
The ONSA states that the alert arose from the ongoing offensives, gathering and threat monitoring by security agencies in collaboration with local and international partners across the country.
Short history
The Office of the National Security Adviser (ONSA) issued a security alert on 29 August 2026 calling for continued vigilance across Nigeria. The alert followed ongoing military offensives, intelligence gathering, and threat monitoring carried out by Nigerian security agencies working with local and international partners. This is the latest public notice from ONSA on the current internal security situation.
Predictive model
ONSA put out a fresh security alert on August 29 because its teams keep picking up threats during ongoing offensives and joint intelligence work with local and foreign partners. The notice simply repeats the call for everyone to stay sharp and report anything odd. Nothing in the statement shows a new attack or a fresh spike in incidents, just steady monitoring that turned up enough chatter to justify the reminder. This kind of alert has come out before whenever the agencies see persistent risk without a clear end. It will likely stay in place as long as the offensives and intel collection continue at the current pace. The situation shifts only if those operations produce a measurable drop in threat signals or if the government declares a new policy that scales them back. Next step is routine follow-up briefings to state governors and local vigilante groups to keep the reporting channels open. No deadline is attached. Horizon: months, until threat metrics visibly decline or operations are formally wound down.
