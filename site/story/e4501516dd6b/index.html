ISRAEL
September 30, 2026 · 21:38 UTC

A pilot stabbed his co-pilot on a Flydubai flight to Israel before passengers tackled him

A Flydubai flight from Dubai to Tel Aviv carried passengers and at least two off-duty pilots in the cabin on September 30 2026. The aircraft had operated the route for years as a commercial link between the United Arab Emirates and Israel. One of the operating pilots in the cockpit held dual Indian and Israeli nationality and had flown the same aircraft type for the airline.

The plane suddenly lost 17 000 feet in under two minutes. A passenger seated near the front heard a scream and saw the cabin lights go out. The injured pilot forced the cockpit door open before he collapsed. Yaniv Hayun and another man entered and found the second pilot on the instruments between the seats. Hayun grabbed the man in a chokehold and dragged him into the cabin. Two more Israeli passengers joined them. They pinned the attacker down and tied him with his own zip ties and headphone cables. Hayun then returned to the cockpit and pulled back on the controls until the aircraft began to level. The two off-duty pilots who had been seated among the passengers took over from the front and flew the jet the rest of the way.

The aircraft diverted and landed at Tabuk airport in northwestern Saudi Arabia. Passengers were later flown on a repatriation flight to Tel Aviv's Ben Gurion airport where they were met by crowds waving flags. The stabbed pilot remained under medical care. Saudi authorities opened an investigation into possible terrorist links.
