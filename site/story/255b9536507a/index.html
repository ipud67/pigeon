'Ballsack Zionist': Jewish politician Lander insulted while protesting NYC Hindu nationalist event

On August 30 2026, Democratic candidate Brad Lander joined a protest outside a Hindu nationalist event at Madison Square Garden in New York. One protester shouted at him, “You look like a scrotum, you filthy ballsack!” and called him a “ballsack Zionist.” The incident occurred during ongoing street demonstrations tied to the Israel-Hamas war that began with Hamas’s attack on Israel on October 7 2023. Those protests have repeatedly targeted elected officials and candidates seen as supporting Israel.

Thinking ahead

This kind of street clash will keep happening as long as candidates keep showing up at these events and the two protest sides stay locked on opposite sides of the same sidewalk. Next step is either another candidate appearance that draws the same crews or a formal complaint that forces NYPD to decide whether the words cross into harassment. The only thing that changes the pattern is if one side stops coming or the city sets up barriers that keep them apart. It runs at least through the next election cycle.
