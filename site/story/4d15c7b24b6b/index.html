WASHINGTON
November 12, 2026 · 22:04 UTC

President sent four names to Senate for two financial regulators

Once the president sends those names to the the chamber must vote to confirm them before they can take the seats.

Nathan Anonick of Virginia would take a seat on the Commodity Futures Trading Commission for a term that ends June 19 2031 in place of Rostin Behnam whose term has expired.

The seats stay open until confirmation occurs.

Thinking ahead
Nathan Anonick of Virginia would take a seat on the Commodity Futures Trading Commission to replace Rostin Behnam whose term ran out. Kristin N. Johnson of Michigan and Steven Levine of Maryland are tapped for open seats on the Securities and Exchange Commission replacing Caroline A. Crenshaw and Hester Maria Peirce. Matthew Rowland of the District of Columbia would fill out the rest of a term on the Commodity Futures Trading Commission that ends in 2028. These nominations sit with the Senate now. The next clear step is committee review followed by a floor vote if the committees advance them. Confirmation odds turn on whether the Senate majority backs the picks and how quickly it schedules the votes. Without a confirmation vote the slots stay empty.
