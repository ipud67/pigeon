An Armenian member of the Ryuk ransomware gang got a two-year prison sentence after he extorted victims for 1.2 million dollars

Ransomware groups began using encryption software to lock victims' computer systems and demand payment in cryptocurrency for the unlock key in the mid-2010s. Ryuk operators started their attacks in 2018 by gaining initial access through phishing emails or compromised remote desktop connections then deploying the malware across networks to encrypt files on servers and workstations.

On September 23 2026 an Armenian national who worked with the Ryuk gang received a two-year federal prison sentence after he admitted to helping carry out attacks that extracted 1.2 million dollars from victims.

The sentence marks the first conviction against a named Ryuk participant in a U.S. court. The case now rests on how federal prosecutors will use the cooperation agreement and any information the defendant provided to pursue other members of the group still at large.

Thinking ahead
A member of the Ryuk ransomware group got a two-year federal prison sentence on September 23 2026 after he took part in attacks that pulled in 1.2 million dollars from victims. The case marks one more rung in the ladder of U.S. prosecutions against Ryuk actors that started with earlier arrests and guilty pleas tied to the same gang. The sentence is shorter than many cybercrime terms because the defendant cooperated with investigators. This kind of sentencing usually slows the specific operator but leaves the rest of the gang intact. Ryuk has kept launching attacks after similar arrests in the past. The next visible step is whether prosecutors move on other identified members or whether the group simply shifts its infrastructure to new people. The situation stays the same unless law enforcement builds cases on fresh indicators like new wallet addresses, victim reports, or arrests in Armenia or third countries. Two-year sentence likely marks the end of this defendant's visible role; gang activity continues at prior pace until next indictment or takedown.
