US health officials lost measles elimination status after a Utah outbreak spread for over a year

The Pan American Health Organization reviewed of ongoing spread and determined that a single strain had circulated without interruption for more than 12 months inside an outbreak centered in Utah that more than 700 people. That finding removed the country's elimination status in 2026. The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention has now confirmed 5,412 cases nationwide this year, more than double the previous year's total and the highest count since 1991.
