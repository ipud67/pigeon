1b:T5c8,A coalition of interfaith groups and the Palestinian UN delegation put a 100-meter shroud with 20,000 handwritten names of children from Gaza on display at UN headquarters in New York on September 19. The event included speeches by Ambassador Riyad Mansour calling for aid, Israeli army withdrawal, and legal action against Israel, plus photos, clothing items, Quran readings, and a multi-faith service. Organizers said the shroud records the names to keep them from becoming statistics and plan vigils at other diplomatic sites plus exhibitions in Washington and Philadelphia.

This fits the pattern of repeated public displays and statements at the UN since the current round of fighting began. The immediate next procedural step is the General Assembly high-level week already scheduled from September 18 to 28, where members will push for resolutions on accountability and aid. For the display to move to a permanent UN spot, the Palestinian delegation and coalition would need majority support in the relevant UN committees and approval from the host country.

The campaign will likely run for weeks in the named US cities while the General Assembly session continues. It stays visible as long as the Gaza fighting posture holds and supply routes remain restricted. Without a ceasefire agreement or major shift in ground access for aid, the vigils and calls repeat on the same schedule.
