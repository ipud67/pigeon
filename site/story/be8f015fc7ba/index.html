ONDO STATE
September 19, 2026 · 11:19 UTC

Suspected methanol in a herbal drink killed 49 people in Ondo state Nigeria

Local brewers in Ondo State have sold herbal alcohol mixtures for years from small shops and roadside stalls to customers who cannot afford factory-made drinks. These mixtures carry a high alcohol content that sellers often dilute or flavor with herbs to stretch supply and cut costs.

On September 19 2026 at least 49 people died and another 100 fell ill after they drank a batch of the mixture known locally as Monkey Shoulder that contained methanol. State police arrested 15 people connected to the sale and distribution of the batch while laboratory tests continued to confirm the presence of the toxic chemical.

The deaths have left families in several communities without breadwinners and forced hospitals to treat scores of patients with symptoms of methanol poisoning. Police now hold the 15 suspects as investigators trace the exact point in the supply chain where the methanol entered the mixture.

Thinking ahead
