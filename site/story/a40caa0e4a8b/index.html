Microsoft exec called AI the ‘largest theft of labor’ in history, court records show

New York Times sued Microsoft and OpenAI in 2025 over the use of its news articles to train AI models that now generate competing content. The suit claims the companies took the paper's copyrighted material without permission or payment and fed it into systems that produce summaries and articles on demand.

On September 17 2026 a federal court in New York unsealed records from the case that include an internal Microsoft email. In it an unnamed executive described the scale of data collection for AI training as the largest theft of labor in history.

The unsealed document now forms part of the public record in the ongoing copyright and sets the stage for arguments over whether the training process counts as fair use or under existing law.
