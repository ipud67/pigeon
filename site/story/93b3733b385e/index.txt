The pattern shows no shift in Chad’s public position and no visible change on the ground along the border. Sudanese army officials continue to point to supply routes through Chad while Chad points to its own refugee burden and closed crossings. No independent verification of the routes has produced a decisive record that either side accepts.

Without new evidence accepted by both governments or a joint border mechanism that actually monitors traffic, the back-and-forth stays in place. Next step is likely another round of closed-door talks or another public accusation when fighting near the border flares.
