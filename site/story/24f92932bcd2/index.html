Thomas Massie moved to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth over the war in Iran

Congress holds the power to declare war under Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution.

The charges center on Hegseth directing Pentagon operations in the war in Iran without authorization and on his of powers reserved to the branch.

The House now faces an expected vote on the articles this week. The motion arrives days after Republicans lost their in the midterm elections.
