September 16, 2026 · 11:36 UTC

Aaron Guckian Wins G.O.P. Nomination for Rhode Island Governor

Rhode Island has not elected a Republican governor since 2006. Democrats have held the office through three straight terms since then. The state also stayed under Democratic control across that period.

Aaron Guckian won the Republican nomination for governor on September 16. The self-described compassionate conservative defeated his primary opponents and will now run against Democrat Helena Foulkes, a former CVS executive, in the November general election.

The outcome sets up a contest between a Republican challenger who positioned himself as a moderate within his party and a Democrat with private-sector experience in a state that has trended blue for two decades. The race now turns on voter turnout in a midterm cycle and how both candidates frame the state's economy and public services.
