21:T52d,The FMCSA has just renewed Covenant Logistics’ exemption from the front-seat CDL rule during CLP behind-the-wheel training once the learner has already passed the skills test. This is the latest step in a string of similar one-year or two-year waivers the agency has granted the company and a handful of other carriers since the original pilot began years ago. The decision rests on FMCSA’s review of the carrier’s safety data, training records, and the public comments filed this cycle; those same records will be the observable trigger for any future change.

The exemption runs through August 31, 2028 unless FMCSA pulls it early. It will continue as long as Covenant’s crash rates, training logs, and roadside inspection numbers stay inside the terms spelled out in the notice. The next visible rung is the carrier’s required annual report to FMCSA and any fresh public comment period that opens before the 2028 expiration.

If the agency’s safety analysis of those reports shows no uptick in incidents tied to the exemption, renewal is the default move. A measurable rise in violations or crashes during the exempted training would be the clear indicator that forces a change or outright denial next time.
