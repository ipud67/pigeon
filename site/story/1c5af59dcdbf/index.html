Messi retires from international football with 'nothing left to give'

“but he understands”
Overview
Argentina great Lionel Messi announces his retirement from international football, saying it was a decision that "hurt" but he understands "the time has come".
Short history
Lionel Messi announced his retirement from Argentina’s national team on August 31, 2026. He had played for the senior side since 2005, won the 2021 Copa América, the 2022 World Cup, and the 2024 Copa América. In his statement he said he had nothing left to give and that the time had come.
Predictive model
Messi dropped the news today that he is done with Argentina's national team. He said he has nothing left to give and the decision hurts but the time has come. This follows the ladder of him captaining the side to the 2022 World Cup win, the 2024 Copa America title, and then stepping back from friendlies and qualifiers in the last year as his club form at Inter Miami stayed strong but his body showed wear. The next rung looks like a quiet fade unless Argentina's federation or the new coach makes a direct public appeal and Messi changes his mind. That would need visible signs like him reversing the statement in an interview or showing up at a national camp unannounced. Right now those signals are absent. No court action or procedural vote is on the table here. The retirement stands until and unless Messi himself walks it back. Horizon: at least through the 2026 World Cup cycle, likely permanent.
