Beaches from the capital north remain closed at least until midday Sunday while patrol boats, helicopters, drones and tagging gear stay active.

This follows two other fatal shark attacks in the state this year and a non-fatal bite north of Perth on Monday. The order targets one identified animal rather than a broad cull like the one that drew protests in 2014.

The search and closure will likely run a few more days until either the body or the shark is recovered or the immediate risk is downgraded. Next step is capture or confirmed destruction of the marked shark, after which beaches reopen and drone patrols increase. The situation changes only if the shark is destroyed or if no further sightings occur over the next week.
