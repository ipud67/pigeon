19:T45e,The US added 162,000 jobs in August, a number that topped forecasts and came after a string of softer reports earlier this year. That beat pushed talk of a Fed rate hike next month into view, with the 3-month Treasury bill sitting at 3.91 percent and the 2s10s spread at 41 basis points on September 4. Job gains have run between 100,000 and 250,000 most months since the post-pandemic recovery settled in, so this print sits squarely in the middle of the recent range.

The streak of steady hiring points to the expansion holding at a moderate pace rather than tipping into slowdown. How long it lasts will show up in the next two or three monthly prints and in weekly unemployment claims, which have stayed low. If claims stay flat and September and October jobs numbers hold above 150,000, the expansion keeps grinding on; a string of sub-100,000 prints or a quick rise in claims would signal it is rolling over.

Next move likely turns on whether hiring stays in that 150,000 zone or drops sharply. No single indicator yet points one way or the other.
