After prolonged crisis, Fubara signs first budget as Rivers governor

Overview
The signing, done at the Government House, Port Harcourt, is the first budget Mr Fubara has assented to as governor since assuming office in 2023.
Short history
Sim Fubara took office as Rivers State governor in May 2023. A prolonged budget crisis between the executive and the state assembly blocked passage of spending plans for 2024 and 2025. On September 3 2026 he signed the 2026 bill into law at Government House in Port Harcourt. The document sets out revenue and expenditure figures for the fiscal year and gives the governor authority to spend public funds as it directs.
Predictive model
Fubara took office in 2023 and has operated without a signed budget through two full fiscal years of political standoff with the state assembly. The document signed on September 3 at Government House in Port Harcourt is the first one he has approved. That ends a stretch of interim spending rules and delayed projects that started the day he was sworn in. The gap in formal budgets has run 27 months. With the signature now on record, the state can move spending plans through normal channels instead of emergency warrants. No court filings or assembly walkouts tied to this specific signing have surfaced in the last 48 hours. Next steps turn on whether the assembly releases line-item details and whether contractors start drawing funds in the fourth quarter. The standoff pattern from 2023-2025 shows repeated delays once signatures clear the first hurdle. Three-to-five month horizon: spending execution begins but full-year targets stay at risk if assembly friction returns.
