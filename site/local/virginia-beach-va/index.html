Heritage
Founding · 1607
In 1607, English settlers first stepped ashore at Cape Henry before sailing on to found Jamestown. They set up a cross to mark the spot, which is remembered there today.
National Park Service — Cape Henry Memorialprimary / gov
Economy
For a long time the area lived on farming, then it grew into a popular seaside resort. Naval Air Station Oceana and other bases now anchor much of its economy.
National Park Service — Cape Henry Memorialprimary / gov
Statehood
Virginia was one of the original 13 states. It ratified the U.S. Constitution on June 25, 1788, the 10th state to do so.
U.S. National Archives — The Constitutionprimary / gov
