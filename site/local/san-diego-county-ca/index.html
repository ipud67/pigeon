Heritage
Founding · 1769
San Diego began in 1769. Spanish settlers built a mission and a presidio — a walled fort — on the bay. It was the first of the missions that Spain founded up the coast of California.
National Park Service — Cabrillo National Monumentprimary / govCalifornia State Parks — Old Town San Diegoprimary / gov
Economy
The town grew from a mission into a busy Pacific port. Today the U.S. Navy and Marine Corps make San Diego one of the largest military harbors in the country.
National Park Service — Cabrillo National Monumentprimary / gov

California joined the United States on September 9, 1850, as the 31st state.
