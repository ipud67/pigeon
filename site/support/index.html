Support

Something wrong with the app, your account, or a story? Here is who to tell.

How to reach us

Trouble with the app or your account: support@pigeonnewspaper.com

About the paper — a story or a mistake: editor@pigeonnewspaper.com

Privacy questions: privacy@pigeonnewspaper.com

Report a mistake in a story

Use the “Report a problem” button on any page and choose “A fact is wrong”. You can also write to editor@pigeonnewspaper.com and name the story. When we get something wrong, it goes on the corrections page.

Delete your account

Sign in, open your profile page, and look for “Delete my account” at the bottom. You will be asked to type the word DELETE before anything is removed.

If that button says it is not available yet, email support@pigeonnewspaper.com from the address on your account and we will remove it by hand.

Deleting your account does not cancel a paid plan yet. If you have one, email support@pigeonnewspaper.com to stop payments.

Refunds

Our refund policy says when you get your money back.
